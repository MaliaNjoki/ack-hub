# ACK Provincial Archives Policy

## Overview

The Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK) Provincial Archives is responsible for identifying, collecting, preserving, arranging, describing and providing access to archival materials that document the history and development of the Anglican Church of Kenya.

The official ACK Archives page publishes a **Policy Statement** describing the purpose and responsibilities of the Provincial Archives.

## Policy Status

**Policy type:** Official Policy Statement
**Responsible structure:** ACK Provincial Archives
**Provincial level:** Anglican Church of Kenya
**Status:** Officially published by ACK
**Verification:** September 2026

## Vision

The vision of the ACK Provincial Archives is:

> A heritage for maintaining and strengthening the identity and growth of the Anglican Communion in Kenya.

## Mission

The mission of the Provincial Archives is to provide appropriate and permanent care for historical records, tombs and monuments of the Anglican Church of Kenya for research and development.

## Policy Statement

The ACK Provincial Archives exists to:

1. Identify, collect and preserve archival materials that illustrate the growth and development of the Anglican Church of Kenya, both before and after incorporation.

2. Arrange and describe archival materials according to archival principles.

3. Make archival materials accessible on a regular basis, unless access is restricted by a legal requirement or a written agreement with the donor.

4. Provide adequate and appropriate conditions for the storage, protection and preservation of archival collections.

5. Provide regular reference services to individuals, organisations, researchers, government agencies and other interested groups, subject to applicable requirements and fees.

6. Provide educational and outreach programmes whenever possible to increase public awareness and appreciation of the history and development of the Anglican Church of Kenya.

## Key Policy Areas

### 1. Identification and Collection

The Archives identifies and collects materials that document the history, development and activities of the Anglican Church of Kenya.

The collection covers materials relating to both the period before and after the Church's incorporation.

### 2. Preservation

Archival materials are to be preserved under appropriate conditions to protect them from deterioration, loss or damage.

Preservation includes appropriate storage, protection and care of the archival collection.

### 3. Arrangement and Description

Archival materials are arranged and described according to recognised archival principles.

This supports proper management, discovery and retrieval of historical records.

### 4. Access

The Archives provides access to archival materials on a regular basis.

Access may be restricted where:

* A legal requirement applies.
* A written agreement with a donor restricts access.
* Other documented archival restrictions apply.

The AI knowledge base should not assume that every archival record is publicly accessible.

### 5. Reference Services

The Archives provides reference services to:

* Individuals
* Organisations
* Researchers
* Government agencies
* Other interested groups

Reference services may be provided subject to applicable requirements and fees.

### 6. Education and Outreach

The Archives may provide educational and outreach programmes to increase public awareness and appreciation of:

* The history of the Anglican Church of Kenya.
* The development of the Church.
* The importance of preserving historical records.
* Anglican heritage in Kenya.

## Records Covered

The policy statement refers broadly to archival materials illustrating the growth and development of ACK.

Related heritage materials also include:

* Historical records
* Tombs
* Monuments
* Other materials forming part of the Church's historical heritage

The exact contents of individual archival collections should be obtained from the Archives rather than inferred from this policy statement.

## Relationship with ACK Governance

The Provincial Archives is connected to the wider provincial administrative and governance structure of ACK.

The Provincial Secretary's Office also has responsibility for Church archives as part of its listed functions.

Therefore, the Archives policy should be understood alongside:

* Provincial Secretariat
* Provincial Secretary's Office
* Board of Education and Training
* Governance records
* Historical records of the Church

## Data and AI Retrieval Guidance

When responding to questions about ACK archives, the AI should:

1. Identify the Provincial Archives as the relevant provincial archival structure.
2. Distinguish archival policy from general records management.
3. Recognise that access to records may be restricted.
4. Avoid claiming that a particular historical document exists unless supported by an authoritative source.
5. Preserve the distinction between archival materials and ordinary current administrative records.
6. Refer to the official Archives source when information about specific collections or access requirements is required.

## Important Distinction

This document records the **officially published Archives Policy Statement**.

It should not be interpreted as a complete archival manual, records-retention schedule, data-protection policy or information-access policy unless those documents are separately verified.
