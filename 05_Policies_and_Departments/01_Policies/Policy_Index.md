# Anglican Church of Kenya – Policy Index

## Purpose

This index provides a controlled register of policy documents and policy-related instruments identified for the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK) AI knowledge base.

The index is designed to distinguish verified official policies from guidelines, frameworks, statements, historical instruments and documents that have been referenced but whose full text has not been publicly verified.

## Verification Categories

| Status                                     | Meaning                                                                                                          |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Verified – Official**                    | An official ACK source publishes or clearly identifies the document as a policy or policy statement.             |
| **Official Framework/Guideline**           | An official ACK source publishes the instrument, but it is not explicitly presented as a formal policy.          |
| **Referenced – Full Document Not Located** | An official ACK source refers to the policy/instrument, but the complete document has not been located publicly. |
| **Historical**                             | The instrument belongs to an earlier period and should not automatically be treated as current.                  |
| **Unverified**                             | No sufficiently authoritative ACK source has been located.                                                       |

# Important Data-Quality Rule

The ACK Hub must never convert a **topic**, **programme**, **department**, **committee**, **strategy**, **statement**, or **legal requirement** into an official ACK policy without evidence.

For example:

> "ACK has a Finance Office" does not prove that a particular Financial Management Policy exists.

Similarly:

> "ACK promotes environmental stewardship" does not prove that a document titled "ACK Environmental Policy" is an officially adopted policy.

---

# Source Hierarchy

When verifying a policy, use the following hierarchy:

1. Authorised ACK Provincial Constitution
2. Official ACK policy document
3. Provincial Synod resolutions or authorised records
4. Standing Committee records
5. Official Provincial Board or Commission documents
6. Official ACK departmental publications
7. Official ACK institutional documents
8. Other authoritative Church records
9. Secondary sources, clearly identified as secondary

# AI Retrieval Rules

When a user asks:

### "What is the ACK policy on X?"

The AI should:

1. Search the policy index.
2. Determine whether a verified policy exists.
3. If a verified policy exists, retrieve the corresponding policy file.
4. If only a related programme or statement exists, explain that distinction.
5. If the policy is referenced but the full document is unavailable, state this clearly.
6. Never invent policy provisions.
7. Distinguish provincial ACK policies from diocesan or institutional policies.
8. Include the verification date where relevant.
