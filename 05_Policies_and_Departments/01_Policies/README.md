# Anglican Church of Kenya – Policies

## Overview

This folder contains documented policies, policy statements, frameworks, procedures and related governance instruments of the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK).

Policies are important governance instruments because they provide direction for how the Church manages its ministry, people, resources, institutions and responsibilities.

The ACK has identified constitutions, policies, statutes, regulations, rules and systems of management and implementation as important components of its governance capacity.

## Purpose

The purpose of this section is to:

* Document officially published ACK policies and policy statements.
* Preserve policy-related information for the ACK AI knowledge base.
* Distinguish formal policies from guidelines, procedures, frameworks and general statements.
* Record the scope and purpose of each policy.
* Identify the responsible provincial structure where this is known.
* Preserve the source and verification status of policy information.
* Prevent unsupported or generic policies from being presented as official ACK policies.

## Policy Classification

Each document should be classified where possible as one of the following:

### 1. Official Policy

A policy formally issued, adopted or published by an authoritative ACK body.

### 2. Policy Statement

An official statement describing the Church's policy position or governing approach on a particular subject.

### 3. Guideline or Framework

An official document that provides guidance or a framework for implementation but is not necessarily a formal policy.

### 4. Procedure or Regulation

A document describing specific processes, rules or operational requirements.

### 5. Historical or Superseded Policy

A policy or policy instrument that applied during an earlier period and has subsequently been replaced, amended or discontinued.

### 6. Referenced but Not Publicly Available

A policy referred to by an official ACK source but for which the complete policy document has not been located in the publicly available ACK material.

## Data Quality Rule

The ACK Hub must not create a policy simply because the subject would normally require one.

For example, a generic document titled `Procurement_Policy.md` should not be presented as an official ACK policy unless an authoritative ACK source confirms that such a policy exists.

Where a policy is mentioned but its full text cannot be verified, the record should clearly state:

> Referenced by ACK sources; full policy document not located in the publicly available official material.

## Recommended Metadata

Where information is available, each policy record should include:

* Policy title
* Policy type
* Issuing or responsible body
* Scope
* Purpose
* Key provisions
* Implementation responsibility
* Effective date
* Review or amendment information
* Current or historical status
* Source
* Verification date
* Related ACK structures

## Source Hierarchy

Policy information should be verified using the following order of priority:

1. ACK Provincial Constitution and authorised constitutional documents.
2. Official ACK policy documents.
3. Official Provincial Synod resolutions and records.
4. Official ACK Board and Committee documents.
5. Official ACK departmental and institutional publications.
6. Other authoritative Church records.
7. Secondary sources only where necessary and clearly identified.

## Current vs Historical Information

The ACK Hub must distinguish between:

* Current policies
* Historical policies
* Amended policies
* Superseded policies
* Draft or proposed policies
* Policies referenced but not publicly available

A historical policy must not be presented as a current policy without evidence of its continued validity.

## AI Retrieval Guidance

When answering questions about ACK policies, the AI should:

1. Identify the exact policy or policy subject being requested.
2. Determine whether the document is current or historical.
3. Prefer the original ACK source.
4. Distinguish a formal policy from a guideline, framework or general statement.
5. Avoid presenting an unverified document as an official ACK policy.
6. Provide the policy's responsible body where this is known.
7. State when the full policy document is not publicly available.
8. Preserve the policy's date and version information where available.

## Verification Standard

Every policy record should include a verification date.

For current policies, the AI knowledge base should periodically check official ACK publications and governance records for amendments, replacements or new versions.
