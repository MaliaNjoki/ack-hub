# Provincial Constitutional Offices

## 1. Overview

Provincial Constitutional Offices are offices established or recognised within the constitutional and governance framework of the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK).

These offices support the lawful governance, administration, financial accountability and institutional functioning of the provincial Church.

The exact status, powers, appointment procedures and tenure of each office should be determined from the authorised ACK Provincial Constitution and applicable regulations.

This file provides a structured reference for the principal provincial offices and distinguishes them from administrative departments and governance committees.

**Current-source verification:** September 2026.

---

## 2. Constitutional Governance Framework

The provincial governance framework includes:

```text
PROVINCIAL SYNOD
│
├── House of Bishops
├── House of Clergy
└── House of Laity
        │
        ├── Standing Committee
        │
        ├── Provincial Officers / Constitutional Offices
        │
        ├── Provincial Boards
        │
        └── Provincial Commissions
```

The Provincial Secretariat provides administrative and operational support to the provincial Church.

A constitutional office should therefore not automatically be classified as a Secretariat department.

---

## 3. Principal Provincial Offices

The official ACK provincial governance material identifies important provincial offices including:

* Archbishop;
* Provincial Dean;
* Provincial Secretary;
* Provincial Chancellor;
* Provincial Treasurer.

Other offices may be established or recognised by the ACK Constitution or subsequent authorised decisions.

The list in this file should therefore be treated as a structured reference rather than a substitute for the current Constitution.

## 5. Provincial Dean

The Provincial Dean is a sitting bishop elected by the Standing Committee of the Provincial Synod from a list of bishops recommended by the House of Bishops.

The official ACK Provincial Synod page currently identifies:

**Rt. Rev. Dr. Moses Nthukah Masamba**
Bishop of Mbeere
Provincial Dean

The Provincial Dean acts for the Archbishop when the Archbishop is absent from the Church or incapacitated from carrying out the duties of the office.

Because the Provincial Dean is a current office-holder record, the name should always be verified against the latest official ACK source.

---

## 6. Provincial Secretary

The Provincial Secretary is the Executive Officer of the Church at provincial level.

The official ACK description assigns the Provincial Secretary managerial and coordination responsibilities at the Provincial Office.

The current Provincial Secretary is:

**Rt. Rev. Prof. Joseph D. Galgalo**

The Provincial Secretary's functions include:

* public relations;
* coordinating Church governance meetings;
* providing Secretariat functions;
* liaison with dioceses and Church partners;
* staffing and human-resource development;
* general management;
* counselling and conflict resolution;
* safeguarding Church resources;
* asset inventory;
* archives;
* legal and regulatory compliance.

The Provincial Secretary's Office is part of the Provincial Secretariat and should be distinguished from the Provincial Synod and Standing Committee.

For detailed information, see:

`../03_Provincial_Secretariat/Provincial_Secretary_Office.md`

## 10. Provincial Secretary versus Provincial Secretariat

These terms are related but are not identical.

### Provincial Secretary

The individual holding the office of Provincial Secretary.

### Provincial Secretary's Office

The office through which the Provincial Secretary performs the functions of the role.

### Provincial Secretariat

The broader administrative and operational structure supporting provincial Church work.

The distinction is important for AI retrieval.

For example:

**Question:** Who is the Provincial Secretary?

The answer should identify the current office holder.

**Question:** What is the Provincial Secretariat?

The answer should describe the wider administrative structure.

---

## 11. Constitutional Office versus Administrative Department

A constitutional office should not automatically be classified as a Secretariat department.

For example:

| Entity                    | Classification                             |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Archbishop                | Provincial episcopal office                |
| Provincial Dean           | Provincial episcopal/governance office     |
| Provincial Chancellor     | Provincial legal/governance office         |
| Provincial Treasurer      | Provincial financial/governance office     |
| Provincial Secretary      | Provincial executive/administrative office |
| Finance Office            | Secretariat department/office              |
| Mission Department        | Secretariat department                     |
| Education Department      | Secretariat department                     |
| Mothers' Union Department | Secretariat department                     |

The exact constitutional classification should always be verified from the authorised Constitution.

## 12. Appointment and Tenure

Appointment, election, tenure and removal procedures vary according to the office.

The knowledge base should record, where documented:

* office title;
* appointing or electing authority;
* eligibility;
* term;
* date appointed/elected;
* date assumed office;
* end of term;
* vacancy status;
* relevant constitutional provision.

Do not infer tenure periods from the date an office holder appears on a website.

---

## 13. Office Vacancies

A provincial office may become vacant through:

* completion of term;
* retirement;
* resignation;
* death;
* transfer;
* election or appointment to another office;
* removal according to applicable procedures;
* another circumstance recognised by the Constitution.

The knowledge base should distinguish:

**Current office holder**

from

**Former office holder**

and

**Vacant office**.

A vacancy should only be recorded when supported by an authoritative source.

---

## 14. Current Office-Holder Data

Current office-holder information is highly time-sensitive.

For current questions, the AI should prioritise:

1. official ACK announcements;
2. official ACK Provincial Synod pages;
3. official ACK Archbishop/Provincial Office pages;
4. current constitutional or synodical records;
5. official appointment or election documents.

Older documents should be used for historical questions rather than automatically for current personnel.

---

## 15. Constitutional Authority

The formal powers of each office must be determined from the authorised ACK Constitution.

This file should therefore be treated as an index and structured reference.

It should not be used to create constitutional powers that are not explicitly supported by an authoritative source.

For constitutional questions, record:

* Constitution edition/version;
* article or section;
* amendment history;
* applicable regulations;
* relevant Synod resolutions.

---

## 16. Relationship with Provincial Synod

Provincial Constitutional Offices operate within the broader governance framework of the Provincial Synod.

The relationship can be represented as:

```text
Provincial Synod
│
├── Three Houses
│
├── Standing Committee
│
├── Constitutional / Provincial Offices
│
├── Provincial Boards
│
└── Provincial Commissions
```

The Provincial Synod should not be treated as an administrative department.

---

## 17. Relationship with Provincial Secretariat

Some constitutional offices are closely connected to the Provincial Secretariat.

The clearest example is the Provincial Secretary, whose office forms part of the provincial administrative structure.

Other constitutional or governance offices may interact with the Secretariat for administrative support.

However:

> Administrative support does not automatically change the constitutional or governance status of an office.

---

## 18. Historical Office Holders

Historical office holders should be preserved because they are important for institutional research.

For each former office holder, where available, record:

* name;
* office;
* period served;
* appointment/election date;
* end of service;
* relevant achievements or responsibilities where documented;
* source.

Historical names should never be presented as current office holders without current verification.

---

## 19. Data Quality Rules

For AI retrieval involving Provincial Constitutional Offices:

1. Prefer the authorised ACK Constitution for formal powers.
2. Prefer current official ACK sources for office holders.
3. Record dates for appointments and elections.
4. Distinguish current, former and vacant offices.
5. Distinguish constitutional offices from Secretariat departments.
6. Distinguish the Provincial Secretary from the Provincial Secretariat.
7. Do not infer powers from job titles alone.
8. Do not infer tenure from website listings.
9. Preserve historical office-holder records.
10. Record the constitutional article or section where available.
11. Flag conflicting official sources for manual verification.
12. Never invent a constitutional office, office holder or power.
