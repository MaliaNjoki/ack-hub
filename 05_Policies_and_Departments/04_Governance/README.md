# ACK Governance

## Overview

The Governance section documents the principal governance structures through which the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK) is organised and administered at the provincial level.

The ACK's governance framework includes the **Provincial Synod**, its constituent houses, the **Provincial Standing Committee of Synod**, the **House of Bishops**, Provincial Boards and other authorised governance structures.

These structures should be distinguished from the **Provincial Secretariat**, which provides administrative and operational support to the Church.

## Purpose

This section helps the ACK knowledge base answer questions about:

## Provincial Synod

The **Provincial Synod** is the governing council of the Anglican Church of Kenya as a body.

According to the ACK's official description, Provincial Synod operates through three houses:

1. **House of Bishops**
2. **House of Clergy**
3. **House of Laity**

The Provincial Synod deliberates on matters concerning the Church and exercises synodical authority in accordance with the ACK Constitution.

See:

`Provincial_Synod.md`

## House of Bishops

The House of Bishops is one of the three houses of Provincial Synod.

It consists of the bishops of the Anglican Church of Kenya and has responsibilities within the Church's episcopal and governance framework.

The House of Bishops should be distinguished from:

* Provincial Synod as a whole.
* The Archbishop's Office.
* The Provincial Standing Committee.
* Individual diocesan synods.

See:

`House_of_Bishops.md`

## House of Clergy

The House of Clergy is one of the three houses of Provincial Synod.

It represents the clergy within the provincial synodical structure.

The House of Clergy should not be treated as the same body as the House of Bishops or as the Provincial Secretariat.

## House of Laity

The House of Laity is one of the three houses of Provincial Synod.

It provides lay representation within the provincial synodical structure.

The House of Laity is therefore part of the Church's formal synodical governance rather than an administrative department.

## Provincial Standing Committee of Synod

The **Provincial Standing Committee of Synod** is a committee appointed by Provincial Synod to deal with urgent matters that do not necessarily require consideration by the full Provincial Synod.

The ACK's official organogram states that the Boards of Mission, Finance, Education/Training and related provincial structures channel matters through the Standing Committee for deliberation before receiving the final mandate from Provincial Synod.

## Provincial Boards

The ACK has principal Provincial Boards dealing with specific areas of Church ministry and administration.

The major Boards include:

* Board of Mission.
* Board of Social Services.
* Board of Education and Training.
* Board of Finance.

Boards have specialised mandates but operate within the wider governance framework of the ACK.

## Governance and the Provincial Secretariat

Governance bodies and administrative offices have different functions.

### Governance

Includes:

* Provincial Synod.
* House of Bishops.
* House of Clergy.
* House of Laity.
* Standing Committee of Provincial Synod.
* Provincial Boards.
* Authorised commissions and committees.

### Administration

Includes:

* Office of the Archbishop.
* Provincial Secretary's Office.
* Finance Office.
* Mission Department.
* Education Department.
* Mothers' Union Department.
* Other provincial administrative offices.

The Provincial Secretariat supports the administration and implementation of authorised Church decisions but should not be confused with the governing council itself.

## Governance and the Archbishop

The Archbishop is the senior episcopal leader of the ACK and serves within the Church's constitutional and synodical framework.

The Archbishop's Office should therefore be understood in relation to:

* House of Bishops.
* Provincial Synod.
* Standing Committee of Provincial Synod.
* Provincial Secretariat.
* Dioceses.

The Archbishop's Office is not synonymous with Provincial Synod.

## Governance and Dioceses

The ACK is organised into dioceses.

Provincial governance provides a framework connecting the dioceses while respecting the constitutional responsibilities of diocesan structures.

Questions concerning a particular diocese should therefore be answered using the relevant diocesan governance documents as well as applicable provincial provisions.

## Data Quality Rules

The ACK Hub should follow these principles when storing governance information:

1. Use the most recent authorised constitutional material available.
2. Distinguish current governance structures from historical structures.
3. Distinguish governance bodies from administrative departments.
4. Verify current office holders against authoritative ACK sources.
5. Do not treat an old Synod resolution as a current rule without checking subsequent amendments or decisions.
6. Record the date of important governance decisions.
7. Preserve historical information without presenting it as current.
8. Cross-reference related Boards, departments and diocesan structures.

## Current Office Holders
