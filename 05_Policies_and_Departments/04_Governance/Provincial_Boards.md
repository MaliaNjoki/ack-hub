# Provincial Boards

## 1. Overview

The Provincial Boards are specialised governance structures of the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK).

They support the work of the Church at provincial level by focusing on specific areas of ministry, social transformation, education and finance.

The ACK identifies four principal Provincial Boards:

1. Board of Mission
2. Board of Social Services
3. Board of Education and Training
4. Board of Finance

The Provincial Boards form part of the wider provincial governance structure and should be distinguished from the Provincial Secretariat, which provides administrative and operational support.

**Current-source verification:** September 2026.

---

## 2. Place in the ACK Governance Structure

The Provincial Boards operate within the governance framework of the Provincial Synod.

A simplified representation is:

```text id="x6m2sp"
PROVINCIAL SYNOD
│
├── House of Bishops
├── House of Clergy
└── House of Laity
        │
        ▼
Standing Committee of Provincial Synod
        │
        ├── Board of Mission
        ├── Board of Social Services
        ├── Board of Education and Training
        └── Board of Finance
```

The Provincial Secretariat supports the administrative and operational work associated with provincial governance.

## 3. Principal Provincial Boards

### 3.1 Board of Mission

The Board of Mission focuses on the mission and ministry of the ACK.

Its broad areas of work include:

* mission and evangelism;
* discipleship;
* Christian formation;
* ministry development;
* youth and family ministries;
* chaplaincy;
* outreach;
* mission partnerships;
* related ministry initiatives.

The Board of Mission has relationships with specialised ministry structures and movements, including:

* Mothers' Union;
* KAMA;
* KAYO;
* Children's Mission;
* chaplaincy;
* DMA and evangelism;
* other mission-related programmes and initiatives.

For detailed information, see:

`../02_Provincial_Boards/Board_of_Mission/README.md`

---

### 3.2 Board of Social Services

The Board of Social Services focuses on social transformation and related community-development work.

Its areas of work include:

* social development;
* community programmes;
* justice, peace and reconciliation;
* communication;
* research and social transformation;
* development-related programmes;
* environmental and social initiatives;
* oversight of related social-service structures.

The Board of Social Services also has a relationship with **ADS Kenya**, which operates within the ACK's development and social-transformation framework.

For detailed information, see:

`../02_Provincial_Boards/Board_of_Social_Services/README.md`

---

### 3.3 Board of Education and Training

The Board of Education and Training focuses on education and training within the ACK.

Its areas include:

* education ministry;
* theological education;
* ministerial formation;
* training;
* educational institutions;
* continuing education;
* academic and leadership development;
* archives and related educational resources;
* theological education by extension;
* coordination with relevant provincial educational structures.

The Board should be distinguished from individual educational institutions.

For detailed information, see:

`../02_Provincial_Boards/Board_of_Education_and_Training/README.md`

---

### 3.4 Board of Finance

The Board of Finance provides provincial financial governance and oversight.

Its responsibilities include matters relating to:

* financial oversight;
* budgeting;
* financial stewardship;
* investments;
* financial policies;
* grants and loans;
* provincial financial planning;
* related financial committees and structures.

The Board should be distinguished from the Provincial Finance Office, which is an administrative office within the Provincial Secretariat.

For detailed information, see:

`../02_Provincial_Boards/Board_of_Finance/README.md`

## 4. Board Governance

The Provincial Boards operate within the wider constitutional and synodical framework of the ACK.

Their authority and responsibilities should therefore be understood alongside:

* the ACK Provincial Constitution;
* Provincial Synod decisions;
* Standing Committee decisions;
* applicable regulations;
* terms of reference;
* formal Board resolutions.

The Boards should not be treated as independent organisations separate from the ACK governance structure.

---

## 5. Relationship with the Standing Committee

The official ACK governance structure indicates that Provincial Boards channel matters through the Standing Committee of the Provincial Synod for deliberation before matters requiring the authority of the Provincial Synod proceed to the Synod.

A simplified governance pathway is:

```text id="8y7bqk"
Provincial Board
      │
      ▼
Standing Committee
      │
      ▼
Provincial Synod
```

This is a simplified representation.

Not every operational activity of a Board necessarily requires consideration by the full Provincial Synod.

The appropriate route depends on:

* the Constitution;
* delegation of authority;
* the nature of the matter;
* applicable regulations;
* previous Synod resolutions.

---

## 6. Relationship with the Provincial Secretariat

Provincial Boards and the Provincial Secretariat have different functions.

| Structure              | Primary function                                |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Provincial Boards      | Specialised provincial governance and oversight |
| Provincial Secretariat | Administration and operational support          |
| Provincial Synod       | Provincial synodical governance                 |
| Standing Committee     | Continuity and delegated/urgent governance      |

The Secretariat may provide administrative support to Boards, but the Secretariat should not be treated as a Provincial Board.

## 8. Current and Historical Structures

The structure of Provincial Boards can change through:

* Provincial Synod decisions;
* constitutional amendments;
* restructuring;
* changes in ministry priorities;
* changes in committees;
* changes in administrative arrangements.

Historical documents may therefore contain committees or structures that are no longer current.

For example, older ACK strategy documents may use terminology or committee structures that differ from the current provincial structure.

The knowledge base should preserve historical structures but clearly label them as historical.

## 9. Board Leadership

Leadership positions within Provincial Boards are time-sensitive.

When recording a chairperson, secretary, board member or other officer, record:

* full name;
* office;
* Board;
* date of appointment/election;
* term where known;
* source;
* status;
* date verified.

Do not assume that an individual named in an older Board document remains in office.

Current Board leadership should be verified from the latest official ACK source or formal appointment record.

## 11. Board Decisions and Recommendations

The knowledge base should distinguish between:

### Board decision

A decision formally made by the Board within its authority.

### Board recommendation

A proposal submitted to another Church structure for consideration.

### Board report

A document presenting information, findings or progress.

### Board referral

A matter sent to another governance structure for action or decision.

These terms should not be treated as interchangeable.

## 16. Board Membership and Representation

Board membership is governed by the ACK's constitutional and organisational framework.

Depending on the Board and applicable rules, membership may include representatives from different areas of Church leadership and ministry.

The knowledge base should not infer Board membership merely from a person's role elsewhere in the ACK.

For current membership, use:

1. current official ACK Board records;
2. current appointment or election records;
3. Provincial Synod documents;
4. Standing Committee records;
5. authorised constitutional documents.

10. Do not treat historical committee names as automatically current.
11. Preserve original dates and source documents.
12. Flag conflicting official information for verification.
13. Do not invent Board members, committees or powers.
