# Anglican Church of Kenya – Standing Committee of Provincial Synod

## Overview

The Standing Committee of Provincial Synod is an important provincial governance structure of the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK).

It provides continuity in provincial governance between meetings of the Provincial Synod and handles matters within the authority assigned to it by the Constitution and other authorised Church instruments.

The Committee should be understood as part of the governance structure of the Provincial Synod rather than as a department of the Provincial Secretariat.

## Governance Position

The Standing Committee operates within the provincial governance framework of ACK.

Its relationship with the major provincial structures can be represented conceptually as:

```text
Provincial Synod
       │
       └── Standing Committee of Provincial Synod
                │
                ├── Provincial Boards
                ├── Provincial Commissions
                └── Provincial administration
                       │
                 Provincial Secretariat
```

This is a simplified relationship map and is not a substitute for the official ACK Constitution or organogram.

## Main Purpose

The Standing Committee provides continuity and oversight in provincial affairs between sessions of the Provincial Synod.

Its responsibilities may include:

* Considering matters requiring attention between Provincial Synod sessions.
* Coordinating or overseeing matters referred to it by the Provincial Synod.
* Considering reports and recommendations from appropriate provincial structures.
* Facilitating implementation of decisions within its constitutional authority.
* Providing governance oversight on matters assigned to it.
* Referring matters to the Provincial Synod where Synodical consideration or authority is required.

The precise powers of the Committee are determined by the applicable ACK Constitution and authorised governance instruments.

## Relationship with Provincial Synod

The Provincial Synod is the principal synodical governing body at provincial level.

The Standing Committee supports continuity between Synod sessions.

The AI should therefore distinguish:

**Provincial Synod**

from

**Standing Committee of Provincial Synod**

The two structures are related but are not interchangeable.

## Relationship with Provincial Boards

ACK has four principal Provincial Boards:

1. Board of Mission
2. Board of Social Services
3. Board of Education and Training
4. Board of Finance

The Boards operate within the provincial governance framework.

Official ACK governance material indicates that matters from Provincial Boards may pass through the Standing Committee for deliberation before proceeding through the appropriate synodical process.

The exact process depends on the nature of the matter and the authority provided by the Constitution.

## Relationship with Provincial Commissions

Provincial Commissions address specialised matters within their approved mandates.

A Commission may submit recommendations, reports or other outputs through the appropriate provincial governance channels.

The Standing Committee should not be treated as synonymous with a Commission.

## Relationship with the Provincial Secretariat

The Standing Committee is a **governance structure**.

The Provincial Secretariat is the **administrative and operational centre** supporting provincial Church work.

The two may work closely together, but they have different functions.

## Membership

The complete membership of the Standing Committee is determined through the applicable constitutional and synodical arrangements.

Membership may change following:

* Provincial Synod elections
* Appointments
* Changes in office
* Constitutional changes
* Completion of terms
* Other authorised governance processes

A current membership list should therefore be taken from the latest authorised Provincial Synod or Church records rather than reconstructed from historical documents.

## Decision-Making

The Standing Committee may consider matters and make decisions within the authority assigned to it.

The AI should distinguish between:

* A decision
* A recommendation
* A report
* A referral
* A proposal
* A matter reserved for Provincial Synod

A recommendation by the Committee should not automatically be represented as a final Provincial Synod decision.

## Meetings and Records

Important Standing Committee records may include:

* Meeting notices
* Agendas
* Minutes
* Reports
* Recommendations
* Resolutions
* Decisions
* Referrals
* Follow-up actions

Where such records are available, their dates and status should be preserved.

Historical minutes should not be treated as evidence of current policy or membership unless supported by newer records.

## Constitutional Authority

The Standing Committee's powers and responsibilities should be interpreted according to the authorised ACK Constitution and related governance instruments.

Where this file differs from a later constitutional amendment, Synod resolution or other authoritative instrument, the later authorised source should take precedence.

## Historical Information

The Standing Committee forms part of the continuing provincial governance framework of ACK.

Historical records may contain different membership arrangements, office holders, procedures or terminology.

Historical information should therefore be retained with its date and context.

## Data Quality Rules

The ACK AI knowledge base should:

1. Use the official name **Standing Committee of Provincial Synod**.
2. Distinguish the Committee from Provincial Synod.
3. Distinguish governance structures from Secretariat departments.
4. Verify current membership from the latest authoritative records.
5. Avoid presenting historical membership as current.
6. Distinguish Committee recommendations from final Synod decisions.
7. Refer to the Constitution when determining constitutional powers.
8. Preserve dates for historical decisions and appointments.

## AI Retrieval Guidance

When asked about the Standing Committee, the AI should retrieve information in this order:

1. Current authorised Constitution.
2. Latest official Provincial Synod information.
3. Current authorised governance records.
4. Official ACK announcements or reports.
5. Historical records where the question concerns an earlier period.

If current information cannot be verified, the AI should state that clearly.
