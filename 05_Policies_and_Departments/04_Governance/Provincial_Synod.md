# Provincial Synod

## 1. Overview

The Provincial Synod is the governing council of the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK) at the provincial level.

According to the official ACK description, the Provincial Synod exercises its authority through three houses:

1. House of Bishops
2. House of Clergy
3. House of Laity

The Provincial Synod is therefore a principal governance organ of the ACK and provides the synodical authority through which matters concerning the life, ministry, administration and governance of the Church are considered.

**Current-source verification:** September 2026.

---

## 2. Place in the ACK Governance Structure

The Provincial Synod forms part of the highest level of governance within the ACK.

### Simplified structure

```text
ANGLICAN CHURCH OF KENYA
│
├── Provincial Synod
│   │
│   ├── House of Bishops
│   ├── House of Clergy
│   └── House of Laity
│
├── Provincial Standing Committee of Synod
│
├── Provincial Boards
│   ├── Board of Mission
│   ├── Board of Social Services
│   ├── Board of Education and Training
│   └── Board of Finance
│
├── Provincial Commissions
│
└── Provincial Secretariat
    ├── Office of the Archbishop
    ├── Provincial Secretary's Office
    ├── Finance Office
    ├── Mission Department
    ├── Education Department
    └── Mothers' Union Department
```

The Provincial Secretariat should not be confused with the Provincial Synod. The Secretariat provides administrative and operational support, while the Synod is a governance and decision-making organ.

## 3. Three Houses of the Provincial Synod

The Provincial Synod operates through three houses.

### 3.1 House of Bishops

The House of Bishops represents the episcopal leadership of the ACK.

It provides collective episcopal leadership and participates in matters requiring consideration by the bishops of the Church.

The House of Bishops also has an important role in recommending bishops for consideration in relation to the office of Provincial Dean.

---

### 3.2 House of Clergy

The House of Clergy represents ordained clergy participating in the Provincial Synod.

It provides the clergy's representation and participation in provincial synodical deliberations.

Its membership and voting arrangements are governed by the ACK Constitution and applicable synodical regulations.

---

### 3.3 House of Laity

The House of Laity provides lay representation in the Provincial Synod.

It ensures that lay members of the Church participate in provincial governance and synodical deliberations.

Its membership and voting arrangements are governed by the ACK Constitution and applicable synodical regulations.

---

## 4. Functions of the Provincial Synod

The Provincial Synod provides the principal synodical authority of the ACK.

Its functions include:

* deliberating on matters affecting the Church;
* considering matters of provincial governance;
* providing synodical authority for decisions and actions requiring Provincial Synod approval;
* considering reports and matters brought through the appropriate provincial governance structures;
* considering matters relating to the mission and ministry of the Church;
* providing a forum for representation by bishops, clergy and laity;
* considering constitutional and organisational matters;
* considering matters referred to it by the Provincial Standing Committee;
* exercising authority in accordance with the ACK Constitution and applicable Church regulations.

The exact powers, composition, voting procedures and decision-making processes should be interpreted from the authorised ACK Provincial Constitution and applicable regulations.

---

## 5. Provincial Standing Committee and the Synod

The Provincial Standing Committee of Synod is a sub-committee appointed by the Provincial Synod.

According to the official ACK structure, it deals with urgent matters that do not necessarily require the attention of the full Provincial Synod.

The Provincial Boards channel matters through the Standing Committee for deliberation before matters requiring final synodical authority proceed to the Provincial Synod.

This creates an important governance relationship:

```text
Provincial Boards
       │
       ▼
Provincial Standing Committee
       │
       ▼
Provincial Synod
```

This should not be interpreted as meaning that every operational matter must go through the full Provincial Synod. The appropriate route depends on the Constitution, regulations and the nature of the matter.

## 6. Relationship with Provincial Boards

The Provincial Synod provides the broader synodical authority within which the Provincial Boards operate.

The principal Provincial Boards identified by the ACK include:

* Board of Mission
* Board of Social Services
* Board of Education and Training
* Board of Finance

The Boards undertake specialised areas of provincial work and channel relevant matters through the Provincial Standing Committee before matters requiring the authority of the Provincial Synod are considered.

## 7. Provincial Synod Officers

The official ACK Provincial Synod page identifies the following provincial offices associated with the Synod:

* Provincial Dean
* Provincial Chancellor
* Provincial Treasurer
* Bishop of the Church

### Provincial Dean

The Provincial Dean is a sitting bishop elected by the Standing Committee of the Provincial Synod from a list of bishops recommended by the House of Bishops.

The Dean acts for the Archbishop when the Archbishop is absent from the Church or is incapacitated from carrying out the duties of the office.

The current ACK Provincial Synod page identifies:

**Rt. Rev. Dr. Moses Nthukah Masamba**
Bishop of Mbeere
Provincial Dean

### Provincial Chancellor

The Provincial Chancellor is a legal officer associated with the provincial governance structure.

The current official ACK Provincial Synod page identifies:

**Tom Onyango**
Provincial Chancellor

The page identifies him as an Advocate of the High Court, Commissioner for Oaths and Notary Public.

### Provincial Treasurer

The Provincial Synod page identifies:

**Prof. William Otiende Ogara**
Provincial Treasurer

The Provincial Treasurer should be distinguished from the Provincial Finance Office and the Board of Finance.

### Bishop of the Church

The official Provincial Synod page also identifies:

**Rt. Rev. Cleti Ogeto**

The ACK page states that he was consecrated on 20 December 2007.

### Data-quality note

Provincial officers are time-sensitive records. Individual names should therefore be treated as current only when supported by a recent official ACK source.

## 8. Synodical Sessions and Records

Provincial Synod sessions provide formal occasions for provincial deliberation and decision-making.

Synodical records may include:

* agendas;
* minutes;
* reports;
* resolutions;
* communiqués;
* Archbishop's Charges;
* committee reports;
* constitutional or governance matters;
* records of elections and appointments;
* other official synodical documents.

The ACK Provincial Archives preserve records relating to the Provincial Synod, including minutes of Provincial Synod and other historical Church records.

For the AI knowledge base, synodical records should be preserved as separate source documents where available rather than relying only on summaries.

---

## 9. Provincial Synod Resolutions and Decisions

Synod resolutions and decisions are important primary sources for understanding the development of ACK policy, governance and ministry.

When adding a resolution or decision to the knowledge base, record:

* title or subject;
* date;
* synodical session;
* document type;
* relevant resolution or decision;
* source document;
* whether it is current, historical or subsequently amended;
* any later official clarification or superseding decision.

Do not treat an old synod resolution as automatically representing current ACK policy.

---

## 10. Relationship with the Archbishop

The Archbishop is the senior episcopal leader of the ACK, while the Provincial Synod is a collective governance body operating through its three houses.

The Archbishop participates in and provides leadership within the provincial governance structure.

The Office of the Archbishop and the Provincial Synod should therefore be represented as related but distinct entities in the knowledge base.

## 11. Relationship with the Provincial Secretariat

The Provincial Secretariat serves as the administrative and operational centre of the provincial Church.

The Provincial Secretary's Office coordinates governance meetings and provides secretariat functions for the Church.

The relationship can be represented as:

```text
PROVINCIAL SYNOD
     │
     ├── Governance / Synodical Authority
     │
     └── Provincial Standing Committee
              │
              ▼
       Provincial Secretariat
       Administrative Support
```

This diagram is simplified and should not be interpreted as replacing the ACK Constitution or formal governance procedures.

---

## 12. Historical and Current Information

ACK governance information changes over time.

The knowledge base should distinguish between:

* current governance structures;
* historical structures;
* current office holders;
* former office holders;
* current synodical decisions;
* historical synodical resolutions;
* constitutional provisions;
* later amendments or changes.

Where a source is historical, its publication or session date should be retained.

---

## 13. Data Quality and AI Retrieval Rules

For questions about the Provincial Synod, the AI system should:

1. Prefer the current authorised ACK Constitution for constitutional questions.
2. Prefer current official ACK pages for current office holders.
3. Prefer official Provincial Synod documents for synod resolutions and decisions.
4. Preserve the date and session of every synodical document.
5. Distinguish the Provincial Synod from the Provincial Standing Committee.
6. Distinguish the Provincial Synod from the Provincial Secretariat.
7. Distinguish current information from historical information.
8. Avoid presenting an outdated officer as the current office holder.
9. Avoid inferring constitutional powers that are not supported by an authoritative source.
10. Cite the original document whenever a specific resolution, decision or constitutional provision is requested.
