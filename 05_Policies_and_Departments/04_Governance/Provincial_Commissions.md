# Provincial Commissions

## 1. Overview

Provincial Commissions are specialised structures within the governance and ministry framework of the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK).

They provide focused attention to particular areas of Church life, ministry, leadership, service or institutional development.

The term **"Provincial Commission"** should be used carefully because the ACK's organisational structures may change over time. A commission identified in a historical document should not automatically be treated as a current provincial structure.

**Current-source verification:** September 2026.

---

## 2. Place in the ACK Structure

Provincial Commissions operate within the wider provincial governance and ministry framework.

A simplified representation is:

```text
PROVINCIAL SYNOD
│
├── House of Bishops
├── House of Clergy
├── House of Laity
│
├── Standing Committee
│
├── Provincial Boards
│
├── Provincial Commissions
│
└── Provincial Secretariat
```

The exact constitutional status and authority of a particular Commission should be verified from the ACK Constitution, relevant Provincial Synod resolutions, terms of reference or current official ACK documentation.

---

## 3. Purpose of Provincial Commissions

A Commission may be established to provide specialised attention to a defined area of Church work.

Depending on its mandate, a Commission may:

* study a particular issue;
* provide specialist advice;
* coordinate a defined ministry area;
* develop recommendations;
* support implementation of Church priorities;
* undertake research or consultation;
* monitor a particular area of work;
* prepare reports for the appropriate Church authority;
* perform other functions assigned through its mandate.

The specific responsibilities of a Commission depend on its approved terms of reference and governing authority.

---

## 4. Governance and Authority

A Commission does not automatically possess the same authority as the Provincial Synod.

Its authority depends on the instrument through which it was established.

This may include:

* the ACK Provincial Constitution;
* Provincial Synod resolution;
* Standing Committee decision;
* formal appointment;
* approved terms of reference;
* another authorised Church instrument.

For AI retrieval, the source establishing a Commission should be recorded whenever available.

---

## 5. Commission versus Provincial Board

Provincial Commissions and Provincial Boards should not be treated as identical.

| Structure              | General role                                                    |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Provincial Synod       | Principal provincial synodical governance                       |
| Standing Committee     | Continuity and delegated/urgent governance                      |
| Provincial Board       | Specialised provincial governance and oversight                 |
| Provincial Commission  | Specialised or focused commission work according to its mandate |
| Provincial Secretariat | Administrative and operational support                          |

A Commission may work closely with a Board or Secretariat department without becoming a department of that structure.

---

## 6. Commission Mandates

Each Commission should have a clearly documented mandate.

Where available, the ACK Hub should capture:

* Commission name;
* purpose;
* establishing authority;
* date established;
* terms of reference;
* scope of work;
* reporting structure;
* membership;
* chairperson;
* secretary;
* term of appointment;
* relevant reports;
* relevant resolutions;
* current status.

If information is unavailable, the field should be marked **"To be verified"** rather than inferred.

---

## 7. Membership

Commission membership may be appointed or otherwise constituted according to the instrument establishing the Commission.

Membership information is time-sensitive.

For each member, where documented, record:

* full name;
* role;
* Commission;
* appointment date;
* term;
* appointing authority;
* status;
* source.

Do not assume that a person serving on one provincial structure automatically serves on a Commission.

---

## 8. Current versus Historical Commissions

The ACK Hub must clearly distinguish current Commissions from historical ones.

A Commission may cease to operate because:

* its mandate has ended;
* its term has expired;
* it has been replaced;
* its work has been transferred to another structure;
* the Church has been reorganised;
* a later Provincial Synod decision has changed the structure.

Historical Commission records should therefore remain searchable while being labelled as historical.

---

## 9. Provincial Synod and Commissions

The Provincial Synod provides the wider synodical governance framework of the ACK.

Where a Commission is established or its mandate is changed through a Provincial Synod resolution, the relevant Synod record should be linked to the Commission record.

The knowledge base should preserve:

```text
Commission
   │
   ├── Establishing authority
   ├── Terms of reference
   ├── Membership
   ├── Reports
   ├── Recommendations
   └── Related Synod resolutions
```

---

## 10. Standing Committee and Commissions

The Provincial Standing Committee may have responsibilities relating to provincial structures and matters delegated to it.

Where a Commission reports to, is appointed by, or receives a mandate through the Standing Committee, the relationship should be recorded from the relevant official source.

The AI should not infer a reporting relationship solely because two structures appear together in an organisational document.

---

## 11. Commissions and Provincial Boards

Some Commission work may overlap with the subject areas of Provincial Boards.

For example, a Commission may conduct specialist work relating to:

* mission;
* social transformation;
* education;
* finance;
* governance;
* safeguarding;
* ministry development;
* another defined Church priority.

Overlap does not necessarily mean that the Commission is a subcommittee of the relevant Board.

The establishing document should determine the formal relationship.

---

## 12. Commissions and the Provincial Secretariat

The Provincial Secretariat provides administrative and operational support to the provincial Church.

A Commission may receive administrative support from the Secretariat without becoming part of the Secretariat.

The AI should therefore distinguish:

**Governance / specialised structure**

from

**Administrative / operational support.**

---

## 13. Commission Reports

Commission reports may be important sources for understanding ACK policy and institutional development.

When adding a Commission report to the knowledge base, preserve:

* report title;
* Commission;
* reporting period;
* date;
* author or issuing body;
* purpose;
* findings;
* recommendations;
* decisions arising from the report;
* source document.

A Commission recommendation should not automatically be represented as an ACK policy.

---

## 14. Recommendations versus Decisions

The knowledge base should distinguish:

### Recommendation

A proposal made by a Commission for consideration by another authority.

### Decision

A formally authorised action taken by the body with the relevant authority.

### Report

A document presenting findings, progress or analysis.

### Resolution

A formal decision recorded by the appropriate governance body.

These terms should not be used interchangeably.

---

## 15. Current Commission Register

At the time of this knowledge-base build, a complete, authoritative, current public list of every ACK Provincial Commission is not consistently exposed through the official website.

Therefore, the ACK Hub should **not invent or assume a complete current list**.

Instead, current Commission entries should be added when supported by:

1. the authorised ACK Constitution;
2. current Provincial Synod records;
3. official ACK announcements;
4. official terms of reference;
5. current reports or formal appointment documents.

Where a Commission is referenced in an older source but its current status is uncertain, label it:

> **Status: Requires current verification.**

This prevents historical structures from being incorrectly presented as current.

---

## 16. Data Quality Rules

For AI retrieval involving Provincial Commissions:

1. Verify the existence of a Commission from an authoritative source.
2. Record the establishing authority where available.
3. Distinguish current and historical Commissions.
4. Record the date of the source.
5. Do not infer current membership from old documents.
6. Do not infer a Commission's authority from its name.
7. Distinguish Commissions from Provincial Boards.
8. Distinguish Commissions from Secretariat departments.
9. Distinguish recommendations from formal decisions.
10. Preserve terms of reference where available.
11. Link Commission reports to the relevant Commission.
12. Flag uncertain or conflicting information.
13. Use **"To be verified"** rather than filling missing information with assumptions.

---

## 17. AI Retrieval Examples

**Question:** What is a Provincial Commission?

**Answer:** A Provincial Commission is a specialised provincial structure whose purpose, authority and reporting arrangements depend on the instrument establishing it.

**Question:** Is every Commission a Provincial Board?

**Answer:** No. Provincial Boards and Commissions are distinct structures. Their formal relationship must be established from the relevant ACK governance documents.

**Question:** Is an old Commission still active?

**Answer:** Not necessarily. The AI should check the date and current official ACK records before treating a historical Commission as active.

**Question:** Who are the members of a Commission?

**Answer:** Membership should be retrieved from the latest authoritative appointment, terms-of-reference or official Commission record. Older membership lists should be identified by date.
