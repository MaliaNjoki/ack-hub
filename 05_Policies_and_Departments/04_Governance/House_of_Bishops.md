# Anglican Church of Kenya – House of Bishops

## 1. Overview

The **House of Bishops** is one of the three houses that constitute the Provincial Synod of the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK).

The three houses of Provincial Synod are:

1. House of Bishops
2. House of Clergy
3. House of Laity

The House of Bishops represents the episcopal leadership of the Province within the synodical governance structure.

---

## 2. Position Within ACK Governance

The House of Bishops operates within the wider provincial governance framework of the Anglican Church of Kenya.

Simplified structure:

```text
Anglican Church of Kenya
        │
        └── Provincial Synod
              │
              ├── House of Bishops
              ├── House of Clergy
              └── House of Laity
                    │
                    └── Standing Committee of Provincial Synod
```

The House of Bishops should therefore be understood as a **Synodical governance house**, rather than as a department of the Provincial Secretariat.

---

## 3. Composition

The House of Bishops is composed of bishops serving within the Anglican Church of Kenya's provincial episcopal structure.

Membership changes as:

* new bishops are consecrated;
* bishops retire or leave office;
* diocesan jurisdictions change;
* dioceses are created, divided, merged or otherwise reorganised; or
* other ecclesiastical appointments affect provincial membership.

### Data-quality rule

The complete current membership of the House of Bishops should be generated from the **latest authoritative ACK provincial and diocesan records**.

The `Dioceses_and_Bishops.md` file should be used for diocesan bishop information rather than duplicating a complete bishop database here.

---

## 4. Role in Provincial Synod

The House of Bishops participates in the deliberations and decisions of Provincial Synod.

Its role is connected to matters concerning:

* episcopal leadership;
* provincial governance;
* the life and mission of the Church;
* diocesan and provincial relationships;
* ecclesiastical order and ministry;
* matters brought before Provincial Synod; and
* other matters assigned to the Synod by the governing framework of the Church.

The House of Bishops should not be treated as an independent authority outside Provincial Synod. Its work forms part of the Synodical governance system.

---

## 5. Relationship With the Archbishop

The Archbishop of Kenya provides provincial episcopal leadership and presides over the wider provincial governance structure.

The current Archbishop is:

**Most Rev. Dr. Jackson Nasoore Ole Sapit**

The Archbishop's office should be distinguished from the House of Bishops.

### Distinction

| Structure              | Primary function                                   |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Archbishop             | Provincial episcopal leadership                    |
| House of Bishops       | Episcopal house within Provincial Synod            |
| Provincial Synod       | Provincial governing council                       |
| Standing Committee     | Governance and coordination between Synod sessions |
| Provincial Secretariat | Administrative and operational support             |

## 7. Relationship With Standing Committee of Provincial Synod

The House of Bishops and the Standing Committee of Provincial Synod are separate governance structures.

The Standing Committee provides continuity in provincial governance between sessions of Provincial Synod.

Matters considered by the House of Bishops may therefore form part of the wider Synodical governance process, while the Standing Committee has its own constitutionally defined responsibilities.

See:

`Standing_Committee_of_Provincial_Synod.md`

for the separate record of that structure.
