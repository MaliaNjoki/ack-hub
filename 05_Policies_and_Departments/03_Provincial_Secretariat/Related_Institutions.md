# ACK Related Institutions

## Overview

The Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK) works through a number of institutions and organisations that support the Church's ministry, development, administration, education, hospitality and social transformation.

Some of these institutions are closely associated with the Provincial Secretariat, while others operate as separate organisations or institutions with their own governance arrangements.

For the ACK knowledge base, **association with the Provincial Secretariat should not automatically be interpreted as direct departmental ownership or management**.

## Purpose of This File

This file provides a high-level reference to institutions and organisations associated with the provincial ACK structure.

Detailed institutional information should be maintained in separate files where the institution has a sufficiently distinct mandate, governance structure or operational role.

## Anglican Development Services Kenya (ADS Kenya)

**ADS Kenya** is the development and humanitarian arm associated with the Anglican Church of Kenya.

Its work includes development and community-focused programmes.

Areas of work may include:

* Community development.
* Livelihoods.
* Food security.
* Health.
* Education.
* Environmental programmes.
* Disaster response.
* Humanitarian assistance.
* Community empowerment.

ADS Kenya should be treated as a distinct organisational structure rather than simply as a department of the Provincial Secretariat.

### Relationship with ACK

ADS Kenya works within the wider mission and development framework of the Anglican Church of Kenya and may collaborate with provincial Boards, departments and dioceses.

For knowledge-base purposes:

**ACK Provincial Secretariat → Church administrative structure**

**ADS Kenya → development and humanitarian organisation**

## Christian Council of Kenya (CCK)

The **Christian Council of Kenya (CCK)** is a separate ecumenical organisation associated historically and institutionally with the wider Christian community in Kenya.

Where ACK-related information involves CCK, the two organisations should not be treated as identical.

CCK should therefore have its own institutional entry if detailed information is required.

## ACK Guest House

The ACK operates hospitality facilities that serve Church officials, visitors, meetings and related activities.

The ACK Guest House should be classified as a **hospitality institution/service**, not as a provincial department.

Where current locations, management or contact information are recorded, they should be verified against the latest official ACK source.

## ACK St Julian's Centre

ACK St Julian's is associated with the wider provincial Church structure and provides facilities that may support:

* Church meetings.
* Conferences.
* Training.
* Retreats.
* Youth activities.
* Institutional events.

Its operational management should be treated separately from the Provincial Secretariat unless an authoritative ACK source specifies otherwise.

## ACK Guest House Mombasa

The ACK has also operated hospitality facilities in Mombasa associated with Church activities.

As with other hospitality institutions, current operational status, management and contact details should be verified before being included as current information.

## ACK Uzima

ACK Uzima is associated with the ACK's wider institutional and health-related work.

Detailed information about its mandate, services, management and current status should be maintained separately when verified from authoritative ACK sources.

The knowledge base should avoid assuming that an institution's historical association with ACK automatically establishes its present governance or operational status.

## Educational Institutions

The ACK is associated with a number of educational and theological institutions.

These may include:

* Universities.
* Colleges.
* Theological institutions.
* Schools.
* Training centres.
* Language and orientation programmes.

Educational institutions should not be classified as Provincial Secretariat departments.

See:

`../02_Provincial_Boards/Board_of_Education_and_Training/ACK_Institutions.md`

## Provincial Institutions and Investments

Some institutions and assets associated with the ACK may fall within the oversight of provincial financial or investment structures.

These should be distinguished from:

* Provincial Secretariat offices.
* Provincial Boards.
* Diocesan structures.
* Independent legal entities.
* Development organisations.
* Educational institutions.

The appropriate governance relationship should be verified from the relevant ACK constitutional, financial or institutional documents.

## Relationship with the Provincial Secretariat

The Provincial Secretariat may coordinate, communicate or work with related institutions.

However, the existence of a relationship does not by itself mean that the institution is:

* A department.
* A Board.
* A committee.
* A direct office of the Provincial Secretary.
* A unit of the Finance Office.

The knowledge base should record the **type of relationship** where authoritative information is available.

## Data Quality Rules

When adding an institution to the ACK Hub:

1. Confirm its relationship with ACK from an authoritative source.
2. Identify whether it is a department, Board, committee, institution, organisation or separate legal entity.
3. Confirm its current operational status.
4. Avoid treating historical relationships as current without verification.
5. Record the institution's official name.
6. Record its official website or contact information only where publicly provided.
7. Record the verification date.
8. Cross-reference the relevant ACK department or Board where appropriate.

## Current Information

Institutional structures and relationships can change.

Therefore:

**Current institutional status: Verify against the latest authorised ACK source.**

This file should function primarily as a structural index rather than as a permanent substitute for individual institutional records.
