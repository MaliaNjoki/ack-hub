# ACK Mothers' Union Department

## Overview

The Mothers' Union Department is a provincial department of the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK) responsible for supporting, coordinating and facilitating the work of the Mothers' Union across the Province.

The Mothers' Union (MU) is a movement within the Anglican Church that brings together Anglican women and works to promote family life, Christian faith, community transformation and the wider mission of the Church.

The provincial department provides administrative, coordination and programme support for the Mothers' Union and works with diocesan Mothers' Union structures.

## Position within the ACK Structure

The Mothers' Union Department is part of the **Provincial Secretariat**.

It should be distinguished from the **Mothers' Union itself**, which is a Church movement, and from the governance organs through which the movement is overseen.

For the ACK knowledge base:

**Mothers' Union → Church movement/ministry**

**Mothers' Union Department → provincial administrative and coordination office**

**Mothers' Union governance organs → leadership and oversight of the movement**

## Mission of the Mothers' Union

The ACK's stated mission for the Mothers' Union is to promote programmes that contribute to the **well-being of families**, with a vision of seeing **stable families grounded in Christ**.

The movement contributes to the wider mission of the Church through ministry among women, families and communities.

## Core Functions

The provincial Mothers' Union office supports functions including:

* Strategic planning for the Mothers' Union.
* Coordination of provincial Mothers' Union activities.
* Supporting diocesan Mothers' Union leaders.
* Organising training, seminars, conferences and workshops.
* Capacity building for Mothers' Union members and leaders.
* Receiving and coordinating reports from dioceses.
* Supporting women and family-focused programmes.
* Collaborating with other women's societies and groups.
* Supporting programmes for women, girls, children and family life.
* Encouraging income-generating projects.
* Mobilising resources for Mothers' Union programmes.
* Maintaining Mothers' Union correspondence and records.
* Supporting the wider mission of the ACK.
* Coordinating relevant merchandise and published resources with other departments.

These functions are reflected in the ACK's provincial Mothers' Union Coordinator job specification.

## Family and Community Ministry

The Mothers' Union places particular emphasis on family life and community well-being.

Its work may address areas such as:

* Marriage and family life.
* Parenting.
* Women's development.
* Children's welfare.
* Community support.
* Christian formation.
* Fellowship.
* Leadership development.
* Income-generating activities.
* Community transformation.

The specific programmes undertaken may vary between dioceses and over time.

## Training and Capacity Building

The provincial Mothers' Union office supports training and capacity building for members and leaders.

Activities may include:

* Seminars.
* Workshops.
* Conferences.
* Leadership training.
* Fellowship activities.
* Skills development.
* Programme planning.
* Training for diocesan Mothers' Union leaders.

The purpose is to equip members and leaders to participate effectively in the mission of the Church.

## Diocesan Coordination

The provincial department works with Mothers' Union structures in individual dioceses.

This includes:

* Receiving reports from dioceses.
* Supporting diocesan Mothers' Union coordinators and leaders.
* Sharing provincial programmes and priorities.
* Coordinating provincial activities.
* Encouraging implementation of the Mothers' Union mission.
* Supporting communication between provincial and diocesan structures.

The provincial office does not replace diocesan Mothers' Union structures.

## Income-Generating Activities

The Mothers' Union supports initiatives that can improve the welfare of women and communities.

The official provincial coordinator job specification includes encouraging and empowering Mothers' Union members to establish **income-generating projects**.

Such initiatives are intended to contribute to the welfare of women and communities while supporting the wider mission of the Church.

## Resource Mobilisation and Financial Stewardship

The provincial Mothers' Union office also has responsibilities relating to resource mobilisation and financial stewardship.

These include:

* Developing budgets for the provincial Mothers' Union office.
* Supporting resource mobilisation.
* Promoting financial accountability.
* Ensuring responsible use of Mothers' Union resources.
* Supporting funding for approved Mothers' Union programmes.

Financial administration should follow applicable ACK policies and authorised governance arrangements.

## Relationship with the Board of Mission

The Mothers' Union has a strong relationship with the ACK's mission structures.

The historical **ACK Decade Strategy 2018–2027** identified a Mothers' Union Committee under the Provincial Board of Mission.

However, the Board of Mission and the Mothers' Union Department should not be treated as identical structures.

### Board of Mission

A principal provincial Board providing governance and oversight in the mission area.

### Mothers' Union Department

The provincial administrative and coordination office supporting the Mothers' Union.

### Mothers' Union

The Church movement whose members and leaders undertake the ministry.

## Relationship with Other ACK Departments

The Mothers' Union Department may collaborate with other ACK departments and programmes, particularly where activities concern:

* Women.
* Children.
* Families.
* Youth.
* Mission and evangelism.
* Education.
* Social transformation.
* Communication.
* Resource mobilisation.

Collaboration does not mean that these departments are organisationally the same.

## Relationship with Dioceses

Mothers' Union structures exist at different levels of the ACK.

The provincial department provides coordination at the **provincial level**, while dioceses have their own Mothers' Union leadership and programmes.

Diocesan information should therefore be recorded separately in the ACK knowledge base.

## Governance and Accountability

The Mothers' Union Department operates within the wider governance and administrative framework of the ACK.

Relevant structures include:

* Provincial Synod.
* House of Bishops.
* Provincial Secretariat.
* Board of Mission.
* Mothers' Union governance organs.
* Diocesan Mothers' Union structures.

The precise responsibilities of each governance organ should be taken from the applicable ACK constitution, regulations or authorised Mothers' Union documents.

## Important Distinctions

### Mothers' Union Department ≠ Mothers' Union

The department is the provincial administrative and coordination office, while the Mothers' Union is the wider Church movement.

### Mothers' Union Department ≠ Board of Mission

The Board of Mission is a provincial governance structure; the Mothers' Union Department supports administration and coordination of Mothers' Union work.

### Provincial Mothers' Union ≠ Diocesan Mothers' Union

Provincial and diocesan structures operate at different levels of the Church.

### Mothers' Union ≠ General Women's Ministry

The Mothers' Union has its own identity, leadership and programmes. Other women's groups or initiatives should not automatically be classified as Mothers' Union.
