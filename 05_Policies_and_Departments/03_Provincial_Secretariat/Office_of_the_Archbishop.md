# Office of the Archbishop

## Overview

The **Office of the Archbishop** is a central leadership office within the Provincial Secretariat of the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK).

The Archbishop provides episcopal leadership to the Province and works with the House of Bishops, Provincial Synod, Provincial Standing Committee, Provincial Boards, dioceses and other Church structures in advancing the mission and ministry of the ACK.

The official ACK Provincial Staff directory identifies the Office of the Archbishop as a distinct provincial office. It currently lists the Archbishop, the Archbishop's Chaplain, a Secretary and a Driver under the office.

---

## Current Archbishop

The current Archbishop of the Anglican Church of Kenya is:

**The Most Rev. Dr. Jackson Nasoore Ole Sapit**

The official ACK profile identifies him as Archbishop of the Anglican Church of Kenya and also as Bishop of the Diocese of All Saints and Bishop in Ordinary of the Kenya Defence Forces.

He was enthroned as Archbishop on **3 July 2016**. The ACK's Diocese of All Saints profile records that he succeeded the Most Rev. Dr. Eliud Wabukala as Archbishop and became the Diocese's third Bishop.

---

## Role of the Archbishop

The Archbishop is the senior episcopal leader of the Anglican Church of Kenya.

The Office of the Archbishop provides the administrative and coordination support necessary for the Archbishop to carry out provincial leadership responsibilities.

Areas associated with the Archbishop's provincial leadership include:

* Providing episcopal leadership to the Province
* Providing spiritual and pastoral leadership
* Working with the House of Bishops
* Supporting the work of Provincial Synod
* Engaging with dioceses
* Providing leadership on matters affecting the wider Church
* Representing the ACK in national and international engagements
* Supporting mission and social transformation priorities
* Providing leadership and guidance during significant Church transitions
* Working with provincial departments, Boards and institutions

The exact constitutional powers and responsibilities of the Archbishop should be read together with the current **ACK Provincial Constitution**.

---

## Relationship with the House of Bishops

The Archbishop works closely with the **House of Bishops**, which brings together the bishops of the Anglican Church of Kenya.

The Office of the Archbishop should therefore not be treated as a replacement for the House of Bishops.

For knowledge-base purposes:

**Office of the Archbishop**
→ Provincial episcopal leadership and administrative support

**House of Bishops**
→ Collective episcopal body

**Provincial Synod**
→ Provincial governing council

These are related but distinct structures.

---

## Relationship with Provincial Synod

The Archbishop works within the wider governance framework of the Provincial Synod.

The Office of the Archbishop supports the Archbishop's participation in provincial governance, meetings and strategic leadership.

Provincial Synod remains a distinct governance structure and should not be classified as part of the Archbishop's office.

---

## Relationship with the Provincial Secretary

The Office of the Archbishop works closely with the **Office of the Provincial Secretary**.

The two offices have different primary functions:

| Office                        | General function                                                              |
| ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Office of the Archbishop      | Episcopal leadership and support to the Archbishop                            |
| Provincial Secretary's Office | Executive, administrative and coordination functions of the provincial office |

The official ACK staff directory lists a Secretary serving both the **Office of the Archbishop and the Office of the Provincial Secretary**, demonstrating an administrative connection between the two offices.

The Provincial Secretary is described by ACK as the executive officer of the Church who oversees managerial duties at the Provincial Office, which serves as the Secretariat of the Anglican Church.

The current ACK staff directory identifies:

The position should not be confused with the wider **ACK Chaplaincy ministry**, which operates across specialised ministry settings.

## Social Transformation and Public Ministry

The Archbishop's office has also been associated with the ACK's wider social-transformation and public ministry agenda.

ACK's development and social-transformation materials describe the Church's leadership role in addressing societal issues, with the Archbishop's office playing a leadership and advocacy role while technical and community-level support may be provided through ADS Kenya and dioceses.

This illustrates an important distinction:

**Office of the Archbishop**
→ Leadership, representation, advocacy and episcopal direction

**ADS Kenya**
→ Development and technical/programme implementation

**Dioceses**
→ Local pastoral and community-level implementation
