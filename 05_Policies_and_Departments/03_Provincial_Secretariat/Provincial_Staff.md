# ACK Provincial Staff

## Overview

The Provincial Staff structure supports the administrative, operational, programme and ministry functions of the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK) at the provincial level.

Provincial staff work primarily through the **Provincial Secretariat** and its offices and departments.

The provincial staffing structure should be understood separately from the Church's elected, appointed or governance bodies such as the Provincial Synod, House of Bishops and Provincial Boards.

## Provincial Secretariat

The Provincial Secretariat is the main administrative centre through which the ACK's provincial-level operations are coordinated.

Provincial staff may serve in offices and departments including:

* Office of the Archbishop.
* Provincial Secretary's Office.
* Finance Office.
* Mission Department.
* Education Department.
* Mothers' Union Department.
* Other authorised provincial offices and programmes.

The exact staffing complement can change as appointments, vacancies and organisational arrangements change.

## Key Provincial Roles

### Archbishop

The Archbishop is the senior episcopal leader of the Anglican Church of Kenya and provides spiritual and institutional leadership to the Province.

The Office of the Archbishop is supported by staff responsible for administrative and operational assistance.

The Provincial Secretary is the Executive Officer of the Church and oversees managerial and administrative responsibilities at the Provincial Office.

Responsibilities include areas such as:

* Coordination of governance meetings.
* Public relations.
* Liaison with dioceses and partners.
* Human-resource development.
* General management.
* Safeguarding Church resources.
* Records and archives.
* Legal and regulatory compliance.

## Staff and Governance Bodies

Provincial staff should not be treated as members of every ACK governance structure simply because they work at the Provincial Office.

The following are distinct structures:

### Provincial Staff

Administrative, operational and programme personnel serving the provincial Church.

### Provincial Synod

A principal governance body of the Province.

### House of Bishops

The provincial body bringing together the bishops of the ACK.

### Provincial Boards

Governance and oversight structures dealing with specific areas of Church ministry and administration.

### Committees and Commissions

Specialised bodies established to undertake particular governance, advisory or programme functions.

These structures may interact with provincial staff but have different mandates.

## Staff Appointments

Appointments to provincial positions are governed by the applicable ACK constitution, policies, regulations, employment arrangements and authorised Church decisions.

Different positions may have different appointment procedures.

The knowledge base should therefore avoid assuming that all provincial staff are appointed in the same way.

## Current Personnel Information

Individual staff names are **time-sensitive information**.

For the ACK AI knowledge base, current personnel should be sourced from:

1. The official ACK provincial staff directory.
2. Official ACK appointment announcements.
3. Official ACK vacancy or recruitment notices.
4. Official ACK institutional documents.
5. Other authorised ACK publications.

Historical documents should not be used as proof that an individual currently holds a position.

### Verification Rule

When a current official source cannot confirm that a person still holds a position, the entry should be marked:

**Current status: To be verified**

This is preferable to presenting an outdated appointment as current.

## Provincial Staff Directory Data

When personnel data is available from an authoritative current source, the following fields should be captured where appropriate:

| Field             | Description                                 |

## Data Quality Standards

The ACK Hub should maintain a clear distinction between:

* Current staff.
* Former staff.
* Historical office holders.
* Vacant positions.
* Acting appointments.
* Newly appointed staff awaiting assumption of office.
* Staff whose current status has not been verified.

Names should not be moved between these categories without supporting evidence.

## Historical Personnel

Historical provincial staff and former office holders can be useful for research and institutional history.

However, historical personnel should be stored separately from current staff information.

For example:

**Current:** Person currently listed by ACK as holding the position.

**Former:** Person documented as having previously held the position.

**Historical:** Person associated with the office during an earlier period.

This distinction helps prevent the AI knowledge base from returning outdated names when users ask who currently holds an office.

## Official Contact Information

Official provincial staff contact information should only be included when it has been publicly provided by an authoritative ACK source.

Private contact information should not be added to the knowledge base.

Where an office has an official institutional email address, that office contact may be recorded.

## Updating Provincial Staff Data

Because staff appointments can change, the Provincial Staff file should be reviewed periodically.

Recommended verification triggers include:

* New ACK appointments.
* Staff departures.
* Acting appointments.
* New recruitment notices.
* Changes published on the ACK website.
* Provincial Synod decisions affecting offices.
* Changes to departmental structures.

## AI Retrieval Guidance

When answering questions about ACK provincial staff:

1. Check whether the question concerns a **current** or **historical** staff member.
2. Prefer the most recent authoritative ACK source.
3. Provide the position together with the person's name.
4. Include the verification date where relevant.
5. Do not treat an old appointment announcement as proof of current employment.
6. If current status cannot be verified, state **"current status to be verified."**
7. Distinguish staff roles from governance roles.
8. Do not infer a person's current position from their historical association with the ACK.
