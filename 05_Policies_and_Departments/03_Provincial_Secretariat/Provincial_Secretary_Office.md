# Provincial Secretary's Office

## Overview

The **Office of the Provincial Secretary** is a central administrative and executive office of the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK).

The ACK describes the Provincial Secretary as the **Executive Officer of the Church**. The office oversees the execution and implementation of managerial duties at the Provincial Office, which serves as the Secretariat of the Anglican Church of Kenya.

The Provincial Secretary's Office therefore provides a major link between provincial leadership, Church governance structures, dioceses, departments, institutions and external partners.

## Appointment

According to the official ACK Provincial Secretary page, the Provincial Secretary is appointed by the **Archbishop in consultation with the Standing Committee of the Provincial Synod**.

This demonstrates that the office operates within the Church's wider provincial governance framework.

# Core Functions

The official ACK description identifies the following functions of the Provincial Secretary's Office.

## 1. Public Relations

The Provincial Secretary's Office serves as the **public relations office of the Church**.

This involves supporting communication and engagement between the ACK and relevant stakeholders.

The office may therefore coordinate or support:

* Official correspondence
* Public communication
* Institutional communication
* Engagement with Church partners
* Communication relating to provincial activities
* Coordination of information released through the Provincial Office

Public statements and official positions should be attributed to the appropriate ACK authority and source.

---

## 2. Coordination of Church Governance Meetings

The office coordinates the administrative requirements associated with Church governance meetings.

This includes supporting the functioning of provincial governance structures and ensuring that relevant administrative arrangements are made.

Governance structures may include:

* Provincial Synod
* Provincial Standing Committee
* House of Bishops
* Provincial Boards
* Other authorised provincial committees

The Provincial Secretary's Office should not be confused with the governance bodies themselves.

---

## 3. Secretariat Function

The office serves as the **Secretariat of the Anglican Church of Kenya** at provincial level.

This means that it provides administrative support for the functioning of the Province and facilitates coordination between different Church structures.

---

## 4. Liaison with Dioceses and Church Partners

The Provincial Secretary's Office liaises with:

* ACK dioceses
* Church institutions
* Church partners
* International partners
* Other organisations working with the ACK

The official ACK description specifically identifies liaison with dioceses and Church partners around the world, in consultation with the Archbishop's Office and other organs of the Church.

This makes the office an important coordination point between the provincial level and other parts of the Church.

---

## 5. Staffing and Human Resource Development

The office handles provincial staffing needs and contributes to:

* Human resource development
* General management
* Coordination of the Provincial Office
* Staffing administration
* Personnel-related matters

The office therefore has an important administrative role in supporting the human-resource requirements of the provincial structure.

The exact employment policies and procedures should be sourced from the relevant ACK policies and applicable laws.

---

## 6. General Management and Coordination

The Provincial Secretary oversees managerial duties at the Provincial Office.

The role therefore includes coordination of the administrative work necessary for the Provincial Office to function effectively.

This can include coordination between:

* Provincial departments
* Provincial staff
* Church institutions
* Provincial Boards
* Dioceses
* Church partners

---

## 7. Counselling and Conflict Resolution

The official ACK description identifies **counselling and conflict resolution** among the functions of the Provincial Secretary's Office.

This places the office within the Church's administrative support and relationship-management framework.

Specific pastoral or professional counselling responsibilities should not be assumed beyond what is supported by official Church documentation.

---

## 8. Safeguarding Church Resources

The office has responsibilities relating to the **control and safeguarding of Church resources**.

This should be understood alongside the separate responsibilities of:

* Board of Finance
* Finance Office
* Church Commissioners for Kenya
* Provincial departments
* Diocesan financial structures

The Provincial Secretary's Office should therefore not be treated as the Church's sole financial authority.

---

## 9. Church Asset Inventory

The official ACK description states that the Provincial Secretary's Office is responsible for **keeping an inventory of Church assets**.

This function supports:

* Asset documentation
* Institutional accountability
* Record keeping
* Property administration
* Resource management

The asset inventory function should be distinguished from the investment and trusteeship responsibilities of the Church Commissioners for Kenya.

---

## 10. Provincial Archives

The Provincial Secretary's Office has responsibility relating to the **Church archives**.

The ACK Provincial Archives preserve extensive historical and administrative records, including:

* Provincial Synod minutes
* Provincial Board minutes
* Church leadership correspondence
* Financial records
* Administrative records
* Church institutional records
* Property inventories
* Agreements and trust deeds
* Historical Church records

The official ACK Archives page confirms that records of Church leadership and administration and other provincial records are preserved within the Provincial Archives.

The Archives therefore form an important institutional memory resource for the ACK.

---

## 11. Legal and Regulatory Compliance

The Provincial Secretary's Office is responsible for ensuring compliance of the Church with the **laws of Kenya**, according to the official ACK description.

This should be interpreted as an administrative and institutional responsibility.

Where a particular legal issue requires specialist interpretation, the knowledge base should rely on the applicable law and authorised legal advice rather than assuming that every legal matter is determined by the Provincial Secretary's Office.

# Relationship with the Archbishop

The Provincial Secretary works closely with the **Archbishop**.

The official ACK description states that liaison with dioceses and Church partners is carried out in consultation with the Archbishop's Office and other organs of the Church.

The distinction is:

**Archbishop's Office**
→ Episcopal leadership and support to the Archbishop.

**Provincial Secretary's Office**
→ Executive, administrative and coordination functions for the Provincial Office.

The two offices work closely together but should remain separate entities in the knowledge base.

---

# Relationship with Provincial Synod

The Provincial Secretary's Office supports the administration and coordination of Provincial Synod-related activities.

The office should not be classified as the Provincial Synod.

**Provincial Synod**
→ Governance body.

**Provincial Secretary's Office**
→ Secretariat and administrative support.

This distinction is important when answering questions about Church authority and governance.

---

# Relationship with Provincial Standing Committee

The Provincial Standing Committee is a governance structure within the Province.

The Provincial Secretary's Office provides administrative and coordination support to relevant provincial governance processes.

The appointment of the Provincial Secretary itself involves consultation with the Standing Committee of the Provincial Synod, according to the official ACK description.

---

# Relationship with Provincial Boards

The Provincial Secretary's Office interacts with the Provincial Boards but is not itself a Board.

The current principal Boards documented by ACK include:

* Board of Mission
* Board of Social Services
* Board of Education and Training
* Board of Finance

The Provincial Secretary's Office provides administrative coordination within the broader provincial structure.

---

# Relationship with Provincial Departments

The office coordinates with provincial departments including:

* Finance Office
* Mission Department
* Education Department
* Mothers' Union Department
* Other provincial departments and institutions

The current ACK Provincial Staff page separately identifies the Provincial Secretary's Office and these departments.

This means that a department should not automatically be classified as part of the Provincial Secretary's Office, even where the Provincial Secretary provides administrative oversight.

---

# Relationship with Dioceses

The Provincial Secretary's Office serves as an important liaison point between the provincial administration and ACK dioceses.

Its role includes communication and coordination with dioceses and Church partners.

However, each diocese has its own governance and administration.

Therefore:

**Provincial Secretary's Office**
→ Provincial-level administration and coordination.

**Diocesan administration**
→ Administration within an individual diocese.

The two levels should remain separate in the knowledge base.
