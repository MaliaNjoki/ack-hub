# ACK Finance Office

## Overview

The **Finance Office** is the administrative and operational financial office within the Provincial Secretariat of the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK).

It supports the financial administration of the Provincial Office and works alongside the **Provincial Board of Finance**, provincial departments, Church institutions and diocesan structures.

The Finance Office should be clearly distinguished from the **Provincial Board of Finance**.

* **Board of Finance** — governance and financial oversight.
* **Finance Office** — operational and administrative financial management.

The current ACK Provincial Staff directory identifies the Finance Office as a distinct provincial office.

---

## Position within the ACK Structure

A simplified representation is:

**Anglican Church of Kenya**
→ **Provincial Secretariat**
→ **Finance Office**

The Finance Office operates within the broader provincial administration while interacting with the Board of Finance and other Church structures.

It is not itself a Provincial Board.

# Budget Administration

The Finance Office supports the implementation and administration of approved provincial budgets.

This should be distinguished from the work of the **Budget Committee** under the Board of Finance.

### Budget Committee

Provides governance-level consideration of budgeting and budgetary appropriations.

### Finance Office

Supports the operational and administrative implementation of approved financial plans and maintains the associated financial records.

Therefore:

**Budget Committee = oversight/planning function**

**Finance Office = administrative/operational function**

# Relationship with the Board of Finance

The **Provincial Board of Finance** is one of the principal Provincial Boards of the ACK.

The Board provides financial governance and oversight, while the Finance Office provides administrative and operational support.

| Structure                                      | Role                                          |
| ---------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Board of Finance                               | Provincial financial governance and oversight |
| Finance Office                                 | Financial administration and operations       |
| Budget Committee                               | Budget planning/appropriation function        |
| Provincial Institutions & Investment Committee | Institutional and investment matters          |
| Stewardship Committee                          | Stewardship and resource-management function  |
| Terms of Service & Appointments Committee      | Terms of service and appointments             |

The Finance Office should therefore not be presented as equivalent to the Board of Finance.

---

# Relationship with the Provincial Secretary

The Finance Office operates within the **Provincial Secretariat**.

The Provincial Secretary's Office has wider executive and administrative coordination responsibilities, while the Finance Office specialises in financial administration.

The two structures therefore have different primary functions but interact in provincial administration.

# Relationship with Dioceses

The Finance Office operates at the **provincial level**.

ACK dioceses have their own financial and administrative structures.

Therefore, the provincial Finance Office should not be assumed to directly perform all financial functions of every diocese.

Where provincial financial support, grants, loans, reporting or coordination involves dioceses, the relevant authorised provincial and diocesan structures should be identified.

---

# Relationship with Church Commissioners for Kenya

The **Church Commissioners for Kenya (CCK)** is a separate Church institution.

CCK is associated with the investment and trusteeship of Church assets and should not be merged with the Finance Office.

The distinction is:

**Finance Office**
→ Provincial financial administration.

**Board of Finance**
→ Financial governance and oversight.

**Church Commissioners for Kenya**
→ Separate institutional role involving Church property, trusteeship and investment.

This distinction is important for the ACK AI knowledge base.

---

However, asset administration should not automatically be treated as exclusively a Finance Office responsibility.
