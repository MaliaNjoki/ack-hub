# ACK Mission Department

## Overview

The Mission Department is a provincial department of the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK) responsible for supporting and coordinating the Church's mission, evangelism, ministry programmes, and wider efforts toward spiritual and social transformation.

It operates within the provincial structure and works with dioceses, parishes, Boards, departments, ministry groups, and development partners in advancing the mission of the Church.

The ACK's mission work includes evangelism, discipleship, Christian witness, ministry development, community engagement, and programmes that respond to changing social and spiritual needs.

## Position within the ACK Structure

The Mission Department is part of the **Provincial Secretariat** and is listed among the ACK's provincial departments.

It should be distinguished from:

* **Board of Mission** – a governance and oversight structure of the ACK.
* **Mission Department** – the provincial administrative and programme department supporting mission work.
* **Diocesan Mission Departments** – mission structures operating within individual dioceses.
* **Board of Social Services** – responsible for social-service and development-related areas at provincial level.

The Mission Department may work closely with these structures, but they are not interchangeable.

## Core Functions

The Mission Department supports the ACK's mission through areas including:

* Coordination of mission and evangelism activities.
* Supporting dioceses in mission-related programmes.
* Strengthening evangelism and Christian discipleship.
* Supporting ministry programmes and initiatives.
* Coordinating provincial programmes and activities.
* Supporting collaboration between provincial structures and dioceses.
* Developing and supporting approaches to emerging mission challenges.
* Encouraging Christian witness and community engagement.
* Supporting programmes aimed at holistic transformation.
* Working with relevant Church departments, Boards and partners where programmes overlap.

## Mission and Evangelism

Mission and evangelism are central to the identity and work of the Anglican Church of Kenya.

The department supports the Church in taking the Christian message beyond the formal worship setting and into communities.

Mission-related work may include:

* Evangelism and outreach.
* Discipleship.
* Church planting and growth.
* Christian education and formation.
* Community engagement.
* Youth and family ministry initiatives.
* Contextual approaches to mission.
* Support for diocesan and parish mission programmes.

## Programmes and Partnerships

The Mission Department can work with other ACK structures and partners when implementing programmes.

Examples of areas requiring collaboration include:

* Community development.
* Youth engagement.
* Environmental stewardship.
* Family ministry.
* Peace and reconciliation.
* Social transformation.
* Education and training.
* Communication and digital ministry.
* Development and humanitarian programmes.

Where programmes involve development work, the Mission Department may collaborate with **Anglican Development Services Kenya (ADS Kenya)** and other relevant ACK structures.

## Environmental and Creation Care Link

Mission work within ACK can intersect with environmental stewardship and creation care.

The ACK's Green Anglicans initiative is officially associated with the **Board of Mission**, while some environmental programmes may involve collaboration between the Mission Department and other Church structures.

An ACK Green Anglicans concept document, for example, identifies collaboration between ADS and the Mission Department in developing liturgy relating to climate change and conservation.

This means environmental ministry should not automatically be classified as a separate Mission Department programme; its institutional placement should be checked against the relevant ACK source.

## Relationship with the Board of Mission

The Mission Department and Board of Mission are closely related but serve different functions.

### Board of Mission

The Board of Mission is one of the principal Boards of the ACK and provides governance and oversight within the Church's mission area.

### Mission Department

The Mission Department operates as part of the provincial administrative structure and supports implementation, coordination and programme work.

For the ACK knowledge base:

**Board of Mission → governance/oversight**

**Mission Department → provincial administration/programme coordination**

## Relationship with Dioceses

The ACK is organised into dioceses, and much of the Church's ministry takes place at diocesan and parish level.

The Mission Department therefore serves a coordinating and supporting role rather than replacing diocesan mission structures.

Provincial mission initiatives should be understood alongside:

* Diocesan mission departments.
* Parish mission activities.
* Clergy and lay ministry.
* Evangelism teams.
* Youth ministries.
* Family ministries.
* Other recognised ACK ministry structures.

## Governance and Accountability

The Mission Department operates within the wider governance and administrative framework of the ACK.

Its work should therefore be understood alongside:

* Provincial Synod.
* House of Bishops.
* Provincial Secretary's Office.
* Board of Mission.
* Other Provincial Boards.
* Diocesan structures.

The department should follow applicable ACK policies, decisions and authorised programmes.

## Important Distinctions

### Mission Department ≠ Board of Mission

The department is an administrative/programme structure, while the Board is a governance and oversight structure.

### Mission Department ≠ ADS Kenya

ADS Kenya is a separate ACK development organisation/structure. It may collaborate with the Mission Department on programmes but should have its own knowledge-base entry.

### Mission Department ≠ Diocesan Mission Department

The provincial Mission Department operates at the provincial level, while diocesan mission departments operate within individual dioceses.
