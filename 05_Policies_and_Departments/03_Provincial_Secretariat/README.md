# ACK Provincial Secretariat

## Overview

The **Provincial Secretariat** is the administrative and operational centre of the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK) at the provincial level.

It supports the leadership and governance of the Province by coordinating provincial administration, communication, finance, mission, education, records, programmes and other functions.

The Secretariat should be distinguished from the **Provincial Synod**, **Provincial Boards**, **House of Bishops**, and other governance organs. It provides administrative and operational support within the wider governance structure of the Church.

The current ACK provincial staff structure identifies offices and departments including the **Office of the Archbishop, Provincial Secretary's Office, Finance Office, Mission Department, Education Department and Mothers' Union Department**, together with related provincial institutions and organizations.

---

The **Provincial Secretary's Office** is a central administrative office within the ACK Provincial Secretariat.

The Provincial Secretary supports provincial administration and works with the Archbishop, dioceses, departments, boards and other Church structures.

The official ACK material identifies the Provincial Secretary as part of the Church's Head Office leadership.

### Key administrative functions

* Provincial correspondence
* Administrative coordination
* Records and documentation
* Communication with dioceses
* Coordination of provincial meetings

## Finance Office

The **Finance Office** supports the financial administration of the ACK at the provincial level.

It should be distinguished from the **Provincial Board of Finance**, which is a governance/oversight structure.

### Finance Office functions

The office supports areas such as:

* Financial administration
* Accounting
* Financial reporting
* Budget implementation
* Financial records
* Provincial financial transactions
* Financial accountability
* Support to Church leadership and departments

The current ACK staff page identifies personnel serving within the Finance Office, including the Provincial Finance Director/Manager and finance support staff.

### Important distinction

**Board of Finance** = governance and financial oversight.

**Finance Office** = administrative and operational financial function.

## Anglican Development Services – Kenya

**ADS Kenya** is the development arm associated with ACK's social-development work.

It has its own organizational and management structure and should therefore be documented separately rather than treated simply as a Secretariat department.

Related file:

## Church Commissioners for Kenya

The **Church Commissioners for Kenya (CCK)** is the investment arm of the ACK.

CCK is a separate institutional entity responsible for holding Church property in trust and generating income from assets for the benefit of the ACK.

Its governance is distinct from the Finance Office and the Board of Finance.

CCK should therefore have its own knowledge-base record.

---

## ACK Uzima Publishing House

ACK Uzima Publishing House is a Church-related publishing institution.

It was established in 1974, initially as Uzima Press, to print and publish liturgical works for the Anglican Church.

It has its own Board of Directors and management structure and should therefore be treated as a **related Church institution**, not simply as a Secretariat department.

# Secretariat and Provincial Governance

The Provincial Secretariat operates within the wider governance framework of the Anglican Church of Kenya.

A simplified relationship is:

**Provincial Synod**
↓
**Provincial Governance Structures**
↓
**Archbishop / House of Bishops / Provincial Boards**
↓
**Provincial Secretariat and Departments**
↓
**Administrative and Programme Implementation**

This simplified representation should not be interpreted as a complete constitutional hierarchy. The ACK Constitution should be used when answering questions about formal authority, powers and reporting relationships.

---

# Secretariat vs Provincial Boards

The knowledge base should maintain a clear distinction between **governance bodies** and **administrative departments**.

| Structure              | General role                                       |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Provincial Synod       | Provincial governing council                       |
| House of Bishops       | Episcopal leadership and governance                |
| Provincial Boards      | Governance/oversight of major areas of Church work |
| Provincial Secretariat | Administrative and operational support             |
| Departments            | Operational/programme functions                    |
| Church institutions    | Separate organizations with specific mandates      |

For example:

* **Board of Finance** is not the same as the **Finance Office**.
* **Board of Mission** is not the same as the **Mission Department**.
* **Board of Education and Training** is not the same as the **Education Department**.
* **Mothers' Union** is not the same as the **Mothers' Union Department**.

These distinctions are important for preventing incorrect AI-generated answers.

# Records and Documentation

Provincial administration generates and maintains important Church records.

The ACK Provincial Archives preserves materials including:

* Provincial Board minutes
* Provincial Synod minutes
* Parish council records
* Correspondence of Church leadership and administration
* Financial records
* Training records
* Church institutional records
* Property inventories
* Agreements and trust deeds
* Historical administrative records

The Provincial Archives therefore provides an important historical record of the administration and governance of the Church.

---

# Relationship with Dioceses

The Provincial Secretariat supports coordination between the provincial administration and individual dioceses.

However, dioceses retain their own diocesan governance and administrative structures.

The knowledge base should therefore distinguish:

**Provincial level**

* Provincial Synod
* Archbishop
* Provincial Boards
* Provincial Secretariat
* Provincial departments
* Provincial institutions

from:

**Diocesan level**

* Diocesan Bishop
* Diocesan Synod
* Diocesan Standing Committee
* Diocesan departments
* Parishes and other local structures
