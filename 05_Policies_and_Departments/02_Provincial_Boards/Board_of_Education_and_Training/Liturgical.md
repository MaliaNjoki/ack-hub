# ACK Liturgical Function

## Overview

The **Liturgical Council/Committee** is an education and worship-related function within the **Board of Education and Training** of the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK).

Its work relates to the development, coordination and support of liturgical resources and practices used in the Anglican Church.

The current ACK Board of Education and Training brings together the **Provincial Theological Education, Provincial Colleges Council, Education and Liturgical Council**. The ACK's official Board page also identifies a **Liturgy Committee** within the Board.

---

## Purpose

The liturgical function supports the Church by contributing to the development and appropriate use of resources for Anglican worship and Christian formation.

Its broad areas of responsibility include:

* Supporting Anglican worship and liturgical practice.
* Contributing to the development of liturgical materials.
* Supporting the preparation and publication of worship resources.
* Promoting consistency in Anglican liturgical practice.
* Supporting Christian formation through worship.
* Connecting liturgy with Anglican doctrine and tradition.
* Supporting clergy and other church workers through appropriate liturgical resources.
* Working with relevant educational and publishing structures.

---

## Liturgy and Anglican Worship

Liturgy is an important part of Anglican worship and identity.

It provides an organised framework through which the Church conducts services, prayers, readings, sacraments and other forms of corporate worship.

Liturgical resources can include:

* Prayer books.
* Service books.
* Hymn books.
* Lectionaries.
* Church calendars and diaries.
* Confirmation materials.
* Worship guides.
* Other Christian literature.

The ACK specifically identifies hymn books, lectionaries and other Christian literature among the materials produced for use in churches and educational institutions.

## Liturgical Development

Liturgical development involves reviewing, preparing and improving worship resources so that they can serve the Church in changing contexts while maintaining Anglican identity.

This may involve:

* Development of service texts.
* Translation of worship resources.
* Adaptation of materials for local languages.
* Review of worship resources.

Any changes to official liturgical texts should be documented using the appropriate authorised ACK sources.

## Language and Translation

Language is an important part of liturgical accessibility.

The ACK has historically produced and translated liturgical materials in languages used by Anglican communities in Kenya.

ACK Uzima Publishing House records that it has published translations of the **Book of Common Prayer**, as well as other liturgical materials in languages including Kiswahili, Kikuyu, Luo, Kamba, Kisii and Luyia.

The knowledge base should therefore preserve information about:

* Original language.
* Translation language.
* Publication history.
* Edition.
* Date of publication.
* Whether the material is current or historical.

## Our Modern Services

**Our Modern Services** is an important development in the ACK's modern liturgical history.

ACK Uzima Publishing House records that the landmark liturgy adopted by the Province of Kenya in **2002** was subsequently produced in several languages.

The publishing house records editions in:

* Kiswahili.
* Kikuyu.
* Luo.
* Kamba.

It also records limited services in Kisii and Luyia.

For the knowledge base, individual editions should be treated as separate resources where detailed bibliographic information is required.

## ACK Uzima Publishing House

**ACK Uzima Publishing House** is closely connected with the production of liturgical materials.

The institution was originally established in **December 1974** as Uzima Press to print and publish liturgical works for the then Church of the Province of Kenya.

The official ACK account states that the organisation later became **ACK Uzima Publishing House** and that its main objective includes liturgical publishing.

Its stated objectives include:

1. Producing liturgical materials for the Anglican Church of Kenya.
2. Equipping Christians through relevant discipleship and training manuals.
3. Developing Kenyan Christian authorship.

## Provincial and Diocesan Levels

Liturgical work can involve both provincial and diocesan structures.

### Provincial Level

The provincial Liturgical Council/Committee forms part of the Board of Education and Training.

### Diocesan Level

Dioceses may have clergy, committees or other structures responsible for supporting worship and liturgical practice within their local context.

Provincial and diocesan responsibilities should be distinguished in the knowledge base.

## Governance and Authorisation

The knowledge base should distinguish between:

* Historical liturgical material.
* Proposed liturgical changes.
* Approved liturgical resources.
* Published liturgical resources.
* Local adaptations.
* Diocesan practices.

A resource should not be described as an **official ACK liturgical text** unless an authoritative ACK source establishes that status.
