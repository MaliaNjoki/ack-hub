# ACK Educational Institutions

## Overview

The Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK) has established, supported and partnered with a range of educational and theological institutions.

These institutions support the Church's mission through theological education, leadership development, professional education, Christian formation and other forms of training.

The institutions are associated with the ACK's wider **Board of Education and Training** structure but should not automatically be classified as departments of the Board.

## Institutional Categories

ACK educational institutions can be broadly grouped into:

* Universities and higher-education institutions.
* Theological colleges and institutions.
* Christian and ministry-training institutions.
* Language and orientation institutions.
* Specialized education and training institutions.

Institutional relationships with ACK may differ. Some institutions may be directly associated with the Church, while others operate through partnerships, diocesan structures or independent institutional governance.

## Relationship with the Board of Education and Training

The institutions listed above operate within the wider education and training ecosystem of the Anglican Church of Kenya.

The **Board of Education and Training** provides the provincial governance and coordination framework for education-related functions.

However, an institution should not be assumed to be directly administered by the Board simply because it appears in ACK educational information.

Institutional governance may involve:

* ACK provincial structures.
* Dioceses.
* Boards or councils.
* Institutional management.
* University or college governance bodies.
* Partner organizations.

The exact relationship should therefore be recorded for each institution when authoritative information is available.

## Relationship with Provincial Theological Education

ACK theological institutions contribute to the wider provincial theological education system.

They may provide:

* Biblical studies.
* Theology.
* Anglican studies.
* Church history.
* Pastoral ministry.
* Christian leadership.
* Mission and evangelism.
* Practical ministry.
* Christian formation.
* Development studies.

The exact programmes offered by each institution should be obtained from the institution's current official documentation.

## Relationship with Theological Education by Extension

Institution-based theological education and **Theological Education by Extension (TEE)** are complementary but different forms of learning.

### Institutional Education

Usually involves structured programmes delivered through a college, university or theological institution.

### Theological Education by Extension

Provides theological and ministry education through an extension-based model, allowing learners to continue serving while undertaking their studies.

See:

`Theological_Education_by_Extension.md`

for the dedicated TEE record.

For the knowledge base, each institution should ideally contain the following fields:

| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |

| Category         | University, theological college, training institution, etc. |
| Location         | Town/county where applicable                                |

| Leadership       | Current leadership where officially verified                |

## Data Quality and Verification

Institutional information can change over time.

The ACK Hub should verify:

* Current institutional name.
* Current location.
* Current leadership.
* Current programmes.
* Accreditation or registration status where relevant.
* Relationship with ACK.
* Governance arrangements.
* Institutional status.

Where an institution has changed its name, merged, changed governance or ceased a particular programme, the historical information should be retained but clearly labelled as historical.

## Important Distinction

The following concepts should not be treated as interchangeable:

**Board of Education and Training**
The provincial governance structure responsible for education and training functions.

**Provincial Colleges Council**
A coordination/oversight function within the education structure.

**Provincial Theological Education**
The wider theological education function.

**Educational Institutions**
Universities, colleges, schools and other institutions providing education or training.

**TEE**
An extension-based theological education model.

This distinction will prevent the ACK Hub from incorrectly representing institutions as provincial departments.
