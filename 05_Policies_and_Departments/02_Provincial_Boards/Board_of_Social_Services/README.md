# ACK Board of Social Services

## 1. Overview

The **Board of Social Services** is one of the four principal Provincial Boards of the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK).

The Board provides the social-development arm of the Church and seeks to connect the Church's Christian mission with the social, economic and community needs of people in Kenya.

Its work is guided by a holistic approach to ministry in which proclamation of the Gospel is accompanied by practical service, community development, advocacy and efforts to improve people's quality of life.

## 2. Core Areas of Concern

The ACK identifies the following major areas within the work of the Board of Social Services:

* Development
* Communication
* Justice
* Peace and reconciliation
* Research into the roots of societal problems and development of positive solutions

The Board's work therefore covers both direct community development and broader social transformation.

## 3. Anglican Development Services (ADS)

A major vehicle through which the ACK carries out its social-development work is the **Anglican Development Services (ADS)** network.

ADS works with communities and partners in areas including:

* Community development
* Advocacy
* Climate-change adaptation
* Institutional strengthening
* Capacity development
* Sustainability and resource mobilisation
* Knowledge management
* Social transformation

ADS operates through regional structures and works with communities, dioceses and development partners.

## 4. Social Transformation

The Board seeks to contribute to social transformation by:

* Understanding the social and economic challenges affecting communities
* Supporting communities to identify and address their needs
* Promoting sustainable development
* Encouraging responsible stewardship of resources
* Addressing injustice and vulnerability
* Supporting peacebuilding and reconciliation
* Promoting human rights and respect for the rule of law
* Building partnerships that strengthen community development

## 5. Advocacy and Justice

Advocacy forms an important part of the social-development mandate.

The work includes addressing issues affecting communities and promoting:

* Justice
* Good governance
* Human rights
* Peacebuilding
* National cohesion
* Protection of vulnerable groups
* Environmental and climate justice

The ACK's Decade Strategy 2018–2027 identifies advocacy and prophetic ministry as a strategic area, with justice and protection of vulnerable groups forming part of this work.

## 6. Communication and Knowledge

Communication supports the Church's engagement with both its members and wider society.

The Board's official description identifies **The Christian Voice** and **Roots** as publications used as vehicles for dialogue between the Church and the wider world.

Research and knowledge management also support the identification of social problems, documentation of experiences and development of practical responses.

## 7. Environmental and Climate Work

Environmental stewardship forms part of ACK's wider social-transformation work.

The **Green Anglicans** movement promotes awareness and action around the care and restoration of God's creation. Its work includes areas such as:

* Tree growing
* Waste management
* Green energy
* Climate justice advocacy
* Ecopreneurship
* Theology of conservation

Green Anglicans should be treated as a movement/programme within the wider ACK environmental and social-transformation work rather than automatically classified as a separate Provincial Board department.

## 8. Provincial and Diocesan Implementation

The Provincial Board provides strategic direction while social-development work is implemented through appropriate provincial, diocesan and community structures.

The ACK Decade Strategy 2018–2027 identifies community and individual empowerment as a strategic emphasis to be implemented through ADS and diocesan development offices.

This creates a link between:

**Provincial Board → ADS / Development structures → Dioceses → Communities**

## 9. Relationship to ACK Governance

The Board of Social Services operates within the wider governance structure of the Anglican Church of Kenya.

It should be understood as a **Provincial Board**, not as an independent organisation.

Its work contributes to the wider mission and strategic objectives of the ACK and complements the work of the other Provincial Boards:

* Board of Mission
* Board of Social Services
* Board of Education and Training
* Board of Finance
