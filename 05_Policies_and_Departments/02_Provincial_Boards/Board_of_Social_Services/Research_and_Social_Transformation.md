# ACK Research and Social Transformation

## 1. Overview

**Research and Social Transformation** is a functional area connected to the social-development mandate of the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK).

The ACK approaches social development by seeking to understand the context in which people live, identifying the causes of social challenges and developing practical responses.

Research, learning and knowledge management therefore support the Church's wider social-transformation agenda.

## 2. Purpose of Research

Research and evidence help the ACK to:

* Understand the causes and effects of social problems
* Identify the needs of communities
* Inform development programmes
* Support evidence-based decision-making
* Document experiences and lessons
* Identify sustainable solutions
* Improve the effectiveness of interventions
* Strengthen advocacy and community engagement

The purpose is not research for its own sake, but the use of knowledge to support mission and positive social change.

## 3. Understanding Social Problems

The ACK's development approach emphasizes understanding the context and underlying causes of people's challenges.

This may involve examining:

* Poverty
* Inequality
* Food insecurity
* Unemployment and livelihoods
* Climate change
* Environmental degradation
* Access to health services
* Access to education
* Governance
* Justice
* Conflict and peace
* Vulnerability and exclusion

Specific research topics should be documented according to the official programme or study in which they occur.

## 4. Knowledge Management

Knowledge management is an identified area of ADS Kenya's development work.

It supports:

* Collection of information
* Documentation
* Research
* Learning
* Sharing of experiences
* Programme improvement
* Publication of findings
* Preservation of institutional knowledge

Knowledge generated through development programmes can help the Church, communities and partners make better-informed decisions.

## 5. Evidence and Development Programming

Research can contribute to the development cycle by helping organisations to:

**Identify a problem → Understand its causes → Develop a response → Implement the response → Monitor results → Learn from evidence → Improve future interventions**

This approach supports more relevant and sustainable development programmes.

## 6. Social Transformation

Social transformation is a broader ACK mission objective that seeks to improve people's lives and contribute to healthier, just and sustainable communities.

The ACK describes social transformation in terms that include areas such as:

* Physical wellbeing
* Food security
* Quality of living conditions
* Peaceful coexistence
* Justice
* Quality education
* Stewardship
* Environmental responsibility

Social transformation therefore extends beyond economic development and includes the social, spiritual, environmental and community dimensions of wellbeing.

## 7. Role of ADS Kenya

ADS Kenya is a major vehicle through which the ACK carries out its social-development and transformation work.

The official ACK describes ADS as working through:

* Advocacy
* Community development
* Climate-change adaptation
* Institutional strengthening
* Capacity development
* Sustainability and availability of resources
* Knowledge management

Research and knowledge management therefore support the wider development activities coordinated through ADS Kenya.

## 8. Partnerships and Learning

Social-development research and learning can involve collaboration with:

* Dioceses
* Communities
* Government institutions
* Universities and research institutions
* Development partners
* Civil-society organisations
* Faith-based organisations

Partnerships can provide additional knowledge, technical expertise and resources for addressing complex social challenges.

## 9. Research Outputs

Research and knowledge-management activities may produce:

* Reports
* Studies
* Surveys
* Programme evaluations
* Case studies
* Publications
* Learning materials
* Community knowledge
* Policy and advocacy information

Each research output should be stored and classified according to its source, date, subject and institutional authority.

## 10. Historical and Current Information

The ACK has undertaken research and development activities through different structures and programmes over time.

For the ACK Hub:

* Historical studies should retain their original dates.
* Current programmes should be verified against current ACK sources.
* Research findings should not automatically be treated as official ACK policy.
* A research report should be distinguished from an approved Church policy or position.
* Third-party research should be identified as external research.

## 11. Relationship to the Board of Social Services

Research and social transformation support the wider mandate of the **Board of Social Services**.

The relationship can be represented as:

**Board of Social Services
↓
Social Development
↓
Research & Knowledge
↓
Evidence and Learning
↓
Improved Programmes and Social Transformation**

This is a functional relationship rather than evidence of a separate Provincial Research Board.

## 13. Data-Quality Guidance

Research information should be carefully classified according to authority.

### Primary

* Official ACK research
* Official ADS Kenya reports
* Approved ACK strategic documents
* Official provincial publications

### Secondary

* Academic research about the ACK
* Research by recognised institutions
* Development-partner reports

### Tertiary / Informational

* News reports
* General articles
* Unverified online material

The ACK Hub should clearly distinguish these categories so that research findings are not mistakenly presented as official Church policy.
