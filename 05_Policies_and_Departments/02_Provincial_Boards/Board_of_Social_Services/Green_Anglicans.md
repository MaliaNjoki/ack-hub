# Green Anglicans Movement

## 1. Overview

**Green Anglicans** is an environmental and creation-care movement within the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK).

The movement seeks to encourage Anglicans, churches, dioceses and communities to care for God's creation and respond responsibly to environmental challenges.

The ACK connects this work with the **fifth Mark of Mission** of the Anglican Communion:

> To strive to safeguard the integrity of creation and sustain and renew the life of the earth.

## 2. Official Classification

Green Anglicans should be classified as a **movement / ministry initiative**, rather than as a separate Provincial Board.

The current ACK website presents Green Anglicans under the **Board of Mission** section.

For the ACK Hub, it should therefore be cross-referenced from:

**Board of Mission → Green Anglicans**

It may also be referenced from:

**Board of Social Services → Environmental and Social Transformation**

This avoids incorrectly treating it as a department of the Board of Social Services.

## 3. Background in ACK

The ACK adopted the Green Anglicans model in **2017**.

The movement has subsequently been localized within dioceses across Kenya, with diocesan champions supporting environmental awareness and action.

The ACK's environmental work connects Christian theology with practical environmental stewardship.

## 4. Purpose

The movement seeks to support churches, dioceses and communities to fulfil their responsibility as **Earth Keepers** and care for creation.

Its objectives include:

* Promoting productive and responsible environmental management
* Encouraging recycling and responsible waste management
* Promoting tree growing and afforestation
* Encouraging good forest and farm management
* Engaging Anglicans in responsible interaction with the environment
* Promoting clean, affordable and renewable energy
* Promoting climate justice
* Conserving God's creation

## 5. Key Focus Areas

The ACK identifies several practical areas of Green Anglicans work.

### 5.1 Tree Growing

Tree growing and restoration activities seek to increase tree cover and support the restoration of degraded environments.

Activities may include:

* Tree planting
* Seedling production
* Forest restoration
* Fruit-tree planting
* Community tree-growing initiatives

### 5.2 Waste Management

The movement promotes responsible management of waste.

Activities include:

* Waste collection
* Recycling
* Community clean-ups
* Awareness and education
* Reduction of environmentally harmful waste

### 5.3 Green Energy

Green Anglicans promotes adoption of clean and renewable energy solutions.

The objective is to encourage energy approaches that are environmentally responsible and accessible to communities.

### 5.4 Climate Justice

Climate justice is another area of focus.

The movement raises awareness about the effects of climate change and encourages action that protects vulnerable communities and future generations.

### 5.5 Eco-theology

Eco-theology connects Christian faith and environmental responsibility.

The approach encourages Christians to understand care for creation as part of their faith and stewardship responsibilities.

### 5.6 Ecopreneurship

Green Anglicans also promotes environmentally responsible enterprise and livelihood opportunities that can contribute to sustainable development.

## 7. Diocesan Participation

Green Anglicans has been localized within ACK dioceses.

Diocesan champions and other Church members support implementation at local level.

The movement can engage different groups within the Church, including:

* Clergy
* Youth
* Mothers' Union
* KAMA
* Children
* Schools
* Parish communities
* Other Church ministries

## 8. Green Anglicans Day

The ACK observes **Green Anglicans Day** as a special day of prayer, reflection and action for creation.

In 2025, ACK reported that Green Anglicans Day was observed on **10 October 2025**, with activities including tree growing, waste management and promotion of clean energy.

The celebrations involved dioceses and communities across Kenya.

## 9. 2026 Wholesome Ecology Focus

The ACK announced **Wholesome Ecology** as its annual ministry focus for 2026.

The commitments announced by the ACK include:

* Growing an additional 7 million trees
* Expanding access to clean and efficient energy solutions
* Promoting responsible waste management
* Fostering behavioural transformation through eco-theology

These commitments form part of the ACK's continuing creation-care agenda.

## 10. Environmental Stewardship and Social Transformation

Environmental issues are closely connected to social development.

Environmental degradation and climate change can affect:

* Food security
* Livelihoods
* Water resources
* Health
* Communities dependent on natural resources
* Economic wellbeing

Green Anglicans therefore complements the wider ACK social-development agenda while retaining its official placement under the Church's mission/environmental work.

## 11. Relationship with ADS Kenya

ADS Kenya's social-development work includes climate change and environmental management.

Green Anglicans therefore has an important relationship with the wider social-development work of the ACK.

However:

**Green Anglicans ≠ ADS Kenya**

Green Anglicans is an environmental movement, while ADS Kenya is the national coordinating arm of ACK's social-development work.

The two areas can collaborate while remaining distinct structures.
