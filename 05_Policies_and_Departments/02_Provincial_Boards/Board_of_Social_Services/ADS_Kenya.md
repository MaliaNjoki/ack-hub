# Anglican Development Services (ADS) Kenya

## 1. Overview

**Anglican Development Services (ADS) Kenya** is the national coordinating arm of the social-development work of the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK).

ADS Kenya works within the wider social-development mandate of the ACK and seeks to connect Christian mission with practical responses to social, economic and community challenges.

The ACK states that the **Board of Social Services doubles as the ADS Kenya Board**, providing governance and strategic oversight for this area of work.

## 2. Historical Background

The social-development work of the Anglican Church of Kenya has roots dating back to the introduction of Christianity in Kenya.

Early missionary work combined evangelism with responses to community needs, including:

* Education
* Health services
* Community support
* Poverty reduction
* Responses to illiteracy
* Skills development

Over time, the Church developed structured development programmes and partnerships to address changing social and economic challenges.

ADS Kenya became the national coordinating structure for this development work.

## 3. Mandate

ADS Kenya supports the ACK's social-development mission by:

* Mobilising people and organisations to participate in development
* Supporting communities to identify and address their challenges
* Promoting sustainable community development
* Building partnerships for development
* Challenging injustice
* Promoting respect for human rights and the rule of law
* Supporting communities to become more self-sufficient
* Strengthening development capacity within Church structures

The work seeks to integrate Christian principles with practical development.

## 5. Climate Change and Environmental Work

Climate change and environmental management form part of ADS Kenya's development work.

The work seeks to help communities understand and respond to environmental challenges while promoting sustainable approaches to development.

This area connects with the ACK's wider **Green Anglicans** movement and the Church's strategic emphasis on care for creation.

## 6. Regional Network

ADS Kenya coordinates development work through a network of regional ADS structures working across different parts of Kenya.

The ACK has identified regional structures including:

* ADS Pwani
* ADS Eastern
* NAIKA — Nairobi, All Saints and Kajiado
* ADS Mt. Kenya
* ADS Mt. Kenya East
* ADS Central Rift
* ADS South Rift
* ADS North Rift
* ADS Nyanza
* ADS Western

Regional structures work with dioceses, communities and development partners to implement programmes according to local needs.

## 7. Governance

ADS Kenya is linked directly to the ACK's Provincial Board of Social Services.

The ACK states that:

**Board of Social Services → ADS Kenya Board → ADS Kenya → Regional ADS structures → Dioceses and communities**

The Board of Social Services therefore provides the wider provincial governance framework for social-development work.

## 9. Relationship with the ACK

ADS Kenya is not a separate Provincial Board.

It is a major development structure through which the ACK implements and coordinates its social-development mandate.

Its work complements the wider ministry of the Church by addressing practical social and community needs alongside the Church's spiritual mission.
