# ACK Stewardship Committee

## Overview

The Stewardship Committee is a subcommittee under the **Provincial Board of Finance** of the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK).

The ACK's current Boards structure places the Stewardship Committee alongside the Budget Committee, Provincial Institutions & Investments Committee, and Terms of Service & Appointment Committee under the Board of Finance.

The committee forms part of the Church's financial stewardship framework, supporting responsible management and use of resources entrusted to the Church.

---

## Position within the ACK Structure

The structure can be represented as:

**Provincial Synod**
→ **Provincial Board of Finance**
→ **Stewardship Committee**

The Stewardship Committee should therefore be understood as a **committee/subcommittee of the Board of Finance**, rather than as an independent Provincial Board.

---

## Relationship with the Board of Finance

The Provincial Board of Finance supervises and oversees the financial operations of ACK.

According to the ACK, the Board works through subcommittees and advises the Church on matters including:

* Investments
* Budgetary appropriations
* Grants and loans to dioceses
* Financial decisions and their implementation
* Responsible management of Church resources

The Stewardship Committee operates within this broader financial-governance framework.

---

## Stewardship

In the ACK context, stewardship refers to the responsible management of resources entrusted to the Church for the advancement of its mission.

This includes encouraging responsible approaches to:

* Church resources
* Financial accountability
* Giving and resource mobilisation
* Sustainable support for ministry
* Responsible use of assets and funds
* Accountability to the Church and its members

The committee's work should therefore be understood as part of the wider financial and resource-stewardship responsibilities of the Provincial Board of Finance.

---

## Relationship with Budgeting

Stewardship and budgeting are related but distinct functions.

The **Budget Committee** focuses specifically on budget preparation, planning and budgetary appropriations.

The **Stewardship Committee** addresses the broader responsibility of encouraging responsible stewardship of the resources available to the Church.

Together, these functions support financial planning and responsible resource management under the Board of Finance.

---

## Relationship with Investments

Investment matters are also related to stewardship.

The Board of Finance works through its committees in considering investment decisions. The ACK separately identifies the **Church Commissioners for Kenya (CCK)** as the investment arm of the Church, responsible for holding Church property in trust and generating income from assets for the benefit of ACK.

Therefore:

* The Stewardship Committee should not be treated as the same entity as CCK.
* CCK has a distinct legal and institutional role.
* Investment oversight within ACK operates through the Board of Finance and its relevant structures.
* Stewardship provides a broader framework for responsible use of Church resources.

---

## Relationship with Dioceses

Stewardship principles apply across the ACK's provincial and diocesan structures.

The Provincial Board of Finance has responsibilities involving financial support and decisions affecting dioceses, including grants and loans.

At diocesan level, stewardship activities may be implemented through local financial, development, fundraising and ministry structures.

The exact diocesan structure may differ from the provincial structure and should therefore be recorded separately when creating diocesan-level knowledge records.

## Historical Structure

The ACK **Decade Strategy 2018–2027** identifies the Board of Finance and its committees, including:

* Budgets Committee
* Provincial Institutions and Investment Committee
* Terms of Service and Appointments
* Finance and General Purpose Committee

The current ACK Boards page uses a different/current presentation and identifies the **Stewardship Committee** among the committees under the Board of Finance.

For the knowledge base, historical committee structures should therefore be distinguished from the current structure rather than treated as identical.

---

## Relationship with the Finance Office

The **Finance Office** is part of the Provincial Secretariat/staff structure and supports the day-to-day administration of provincial financial matters.

The current ACK Heads of Departments page identifies the Finance Office and lists the Provincial Finance Director/Manager among its staff.

The distinction is:

* **Board of Finance** — governance and oversight
* **Stewardship Committee** — committee supporting the Board's stewardship responsibilities
* **Finance Office** — administrative and operational financial function

This distinction is important for accurate AI-generated answers.
