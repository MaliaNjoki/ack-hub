# ACK Board of Finance

## Overview

The **Provincial Board of Finance** is one of the principal Boards of the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK).

It serves as the financial arm of the Church and supervises and oversees the financial operations of the Anglican Church of Kenya.

Its mandate includes ensuring that financial decisions of the ACK are implemented in accordance with the vision and objectives of the Church.

The Board works through committees and related financial structures to support financial planning, resource management, investment, stewardship and the allocation of financial resources.

## Core Mandate

The Board of Finance is responsible for providing financial oversight that supports the mission and ministry of the ACK.

Its documented responsibilities include:

* Supervising and overseeing the financial operations of the ACK.
* Advising the Church on financial matters.
* Making or supporting decisions concerning investments.
* Considering budgetary appropriations.
* Considering grants and loans to dioceses.
* Supporting responsible management of Church resources.
* Promoting accountability for financial resources entrusted to the Church.
* Supporting the wider mission and advancement of the Anglican Church of Kenya.

## Committees and Functions

The current ACK Boards information identifies the following structures under the Board of Finance:

1. **Budget Committee**
2. **Provincial Institutions & Investment Committee**
3. **Terms of Service & Appointments**
4. **Stewardship Committee**

These committees support different aspects of the Board's financial and administrative mandate.

The ACK Decade Strategy 2018–2027 also identifies the Board of Finance and its committee structure, including:

* Budgets Committee.
* Provincial Institutions and Investment Committee.
* Terms of Service and Appointments.
* Finance and General Purpose Committee.

Because the current ACK website and the historical strategy document use somewhat different committee listings, the ACK Hub should preserve the distinction between **current structure** and **historical/strategic structure** rather than automatically treating the two lists as identical.

## Budget and Financial Planning

Budgeting is an important function of the Board of Finance.

The Board considers financial requirements and budgetary appropriations within the wider governance and mission priorities of the Church.

Budget-related work helps connect:

* Church priorities.
* Available financial resources.
* Provincial programmes.
* Diocesan needs.
* Institutional requirements.
* Planned investments.
* Operational expenditure.

Detailed annual budgets, financial statements and appropriations should be stored as separate financial records where they are officially available.

## Investments and Provincial Institutions

The Provincial Institutions & Investment Committee is one of the structures identified under the Board of Finance.

Investment-related work supports the responsible management and growth of Church resources.

The ACK has institutional and investment interests that contribute to the financial sustainability of the Church.

One important related institution is the **Church Commissioners for Kenya (CCK)**.

CCK describes itself as the investment arm of the ACK and holds movable and immovable property in trust for the Church while generating income from those assets for the benefit of the wider ACK.

For the ACK Hub, the Board of Finance and CCK should be documented as **related but distinct entities**.

## Grants and Loans to Dioceses

The Board of Finance has a documented role in decisions concerning grants and loans to dioceses.

This provides a financial-support mechanism within the provincial structure.

The ACK Hub should distinguish between:

* Provincial financial policy.
* Board decisions.
* Approved grants.
* Loans.
* Diocesan financial records.
* Individual financial transactions.

Specific financial amounts should only be included where supported by an authoritative ACK financial record.

## Stewardship

Stewardship is a core financial and Christian responsibility within the ACK.

The Stewardship Committee is identified as one of the structures under the Board of Finance.

Stewardship involves responsible management of resources entrusted to the Church and supports accountability, sustainability and the advancement of Church ministry.

It connects financial management with the broader responsibility of the Church to use its resources responsibly in fulfilling its mission.

## Terms of Service and Appointments

The current ACK Boards page identifies **Terms of Service & Appointments** as one of the structures under the Board of Finance.

This function should be documented separately from ordinary human-resource administration.

Where Terms of Service and Appointments involve financial implications, such matters should be understood within the wider governance, employment and financial framework of the Church.

## Finance Office

The **Finance Office** is part of the ACK Provincial Secretariat/staff structure and should not be treated as identical to the Provincial Board of Finance.

The Board provides governance and oversight, while the Finance Office provides professional and administrative financial functions within the provincial administration.

The current ACK Heads of Departments page identifies the Finance Office and lists the Provincial Finance Director among its staff structure.

For the ACK Hub:

**Board of Finance**
→ Provincial governance and financial oversight.

**Finance Office**
→ Provincial administrative and professional financial operations.

This distinction is important for accurate knowledge-base classification.

## Relationship with the Provincial Synod

The Board of Finance operates within the wider governance structure of the Anglican Church of Kenya.

The Provincial Synod is part of the Church's provincial governance system, while the Board of Finance performs specialized financial oversight and advisory functions.

Financial policies, decisions and institutional arrangements should therefore be understood within the Church's constitutional and synodical framework.

## Relationship with Dioceses

The Board of Finance has a provincial relationship with ACK dioceses.

This includes financial matters such as:

* Grants.
* Loans.
* Financial guidance.
* Resource allocation.
* Institutional and investment matters.
* Financial accountability.

Diocesan Boards of Finance and diocesan finance offices should be documented separately because they operate at a different level of Church governance.

## Relationship with Church Institutions

The Board of Finance is connected to various ACK institutions and financial structures.

These may include:

* Church Commissioners for Kenya.
* Provincial financial offices.
* ACK institutions and investments.
* Diocesan financial structures.
* Other Church-owned or Church-associated entities.

Each institution should retain its own governance record where appropriate.

## Governance and Accountability

Financial oversight is connected to the wider principles of accountability and responsible stewardship.

The ACK financial structure should therefore distinguish between:

* Governance.
* Financial administration.
* Accounting.
* Investment management.
* Budgeting.
* Auditing.
* Stewardship.
* Institutional management.

These functions may involve different committees, offices and institutions.
