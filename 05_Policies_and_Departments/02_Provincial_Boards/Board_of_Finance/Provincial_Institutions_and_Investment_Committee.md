# ACK Provincial Institutions and Investment Committee

## Overview

The **Provincial Institutions & Investment Committee** is one of the committees identified under the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK) Board of Finance.

Its work relates to the financial oversight of provincial institutions and investment matters within the wider financial governance framework of the ACK.

The current ACK provincial structure identifies this committee under the Board of Finance.

## Purpose

The committee provides a structure through which matters concerning provincial institutions and investments can be considered within the Church's financial governance system.

Its areas of relevance include:

* Provincial institutions.
* Church investments.
* Investment-related decisions.
* Financial sustainability.
* Management and use of Church resources.
* Institutional financial performance.
* Long-term resource development.

Specific powers and responsibilities should be interpreted according to the ACK Constitution, applicable policies, committee terms of reference and current provincial decisions.

## Provincial Institutions

The ACK operates and is associated with various institutions serving its mission, education, development and administrative objectives.

These may include:

* Educational institutions.
* Theological institutions.
* Social-development institutions.
* Publishing and communication institutions.
* Property and investment-related entities.
* Other Church-associated institutions.

Not every ACK-associated institution is necessarily directly administered by the Board of Finance.

The ACK Hub should therefore record each institution's actual governance relationship separately.

## Investment Function

Investment is an important component of the Church's financial sustainability.

The committee provides a governance structure for considering provincial investment matters and institutional interests.

Investment-related considerations may include:

* Financial sustainability.
* Asset management.
* Income generation.
* Risk considerations.
* Institutional performance.
* Long-term value.
* Alignment with Church objectives.

Specific investment decisions should only be recorded when supported by official ACK documentation.

## Church Commissioners for Kenya

The **Church Commissioners for Kenya (CCK)** is an important institution in the ACK's wider investment structure.

ACK describes CCK as the investment arm of the Church.

CCK holds movable and immovable property in trust for the Church and generates income from those assets for the benefit of the ACK.

The ACK Hub should maintain a separate record for CCK because it is an institution with its own governance and operational structure.

The relationship can be represented as:

**ACK**
→ **Board of Finance**
→ Provincial financial and investment oversight

and separately:

**ACK**
→ **Church Commissioners for Kenya**
→ Investment and property management

These structures are related but should not be treated as the same entity.

## Relationship with the Board of Finance

The Provincial Institutions & Investment Committee operates within the wider mandate of the Board of Finance.

### Board of Finance

Provides provincial financial governance and oversight.

### Provincial Institutions & Investment Committee

Focuses on matters relating to provincial institutions and investments.

### Finance Office

Provides professional and administrative financial services.

### Church Commissioners for Kenya

Manages investment and property interests entrusted to it under its own institutional framework.

Maintaining these distinctions will improve the accuracy of the ACK Hub.

## Financial Sustainability

Investment and institutional management can contribute to the financial sustainability of the Church.

The broader objective is to ensure that Church resources are responsibly managed and can support:

* Mission.
* Evangelism.
* Education.
* Social services.
* Clergy and ministry.
* Provincial administration.
* Diocesan support.
* Other approved Church activities.

Financial sustainability should therefore be understood as a means of supporting the Church's wider mission rather than as a separate ministry objective.

## Relationship with Provincial Institutions

Where an ACK institution has financial or investment implications, the relevant matter may interact with the provincial financial governance system.

However, institutional governance remains distinct.

For the ACK Hub, each institution should ideally record:

* Official name.
* Purpose.
* Governance structure.
* ACK relationship.
* Financial relationship where publicly documented.
* Current leadership where officially verified.
* Historical development.
* Current status.
* Source documents.

## Investment Decision-Making

Investment decisions should be recorded according to the level at which the decision was made.

Possible records include:

* Committee recommendations.
* Board decisions.
* Provincial Synod decisions.
* Institutional board decisions.
* CCK decisions.
* Official financial reports.

The ACK Hub should not assume that every investment-related activity is a decision of the Board of Finance.

## Historical and Current Structure

The ACK Decade Strategy 2018–2027 identifies the **Provincial Institutions and Investment Committee** within the provincial financial structure.

The current ACK website also identifies the **Provincial Institutions & Investment Committee** under the Board of Finance.

The terminology is substantially consistent across the two sources, although the exact committee membership and responsibilities may change over time.

Historical information should therefore remain linked to its source period.

## Relationship with Budgeting

Institutional and investment matters may have implications for provincial budgeting.

The relationship can be understood as:

**Budget Committee**
→ considers planned income and expenditure.

**Provincial Institutions & Investment Committee**
→ considers institutional and investment matters.

**Board of Finance**
→ provides the wider financial governance framework.

The two committees should therefore remain separately documented.

## Relationship with Stewardship

Investment and institutional management are also connected to the wider concept of stewardship.

Responsible stewardship involves ensuring that resources entrusted to the Church are managed appropriately and used in support of its mission.

The Stewardship Committee is separately documented under the Board of Finance.

See:

`Stewardship_Committee.md`

for the dedicated record.

## Governance and Accountability

Investment and institutional matters require appropriate governance and accountability.

Relevant records may include:

* Investment policies.
* Committee minutes.
* Board decisions.
* Institutional reports.
* Financial statements.
* Asset registers.
* Audit reports.
* Property records.
* Official agreements.

Sensitive financial and contractual information should only be included in the ACK Hub where the information is authorized for inclusion.
