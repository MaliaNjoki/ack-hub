# ACK Terms of Service and Appointments

## Overview

**Terms of Service & Appointments** is one of the structures identified under the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK) Board of Finance.

It deals with matters relating to terms of service and appointments within the provincial Church structure, particularly where such matters have financial, administrative or governance implications.

The current ACK provincial structure identifies **Terms of Service & Appointments** among the committees/functions under the Board of Finance.

## Purpose

The function provides a governance framework for considering matters concerning:

* Terms of service.
* Appointments.
* Employment-related conditions.
* Remuneration and benefits where applicable.
* Service conditions for Church personnel.
* Related administrative and financial implications.

Specific responsibilities should be interpreted according to the ACK Constitution, applicable policies, approved terms of reference and current provincial decisions.

## Terms of Service

Terms of service establish the conditions under which a person serves the Church.

Depending on the position and applicable Church policies, these may address:

* Appointment conditions.
* Responsibilities and duties.
* Duration of service.
* Remuneration.
* Benefits and allowances.
* Leave arrangements.
* Retirement provisions.
* Conduct and accountability.
* Termination or cessation of service.
* Other applicable employment or service conditions.

Different categories of Church workers may be governed by different arrangements.

## Appointments

Appointments are an important part of the administration and governance of the Church.

The ACK has different categories of personnel and office holders, including:

* Clergy.
* Lay employees.
* Provincial staff.
* Diocesan staff.
* Institutional employees.
* Officers serving in Church structures.
* Other appointed personnel.

The authority responsible for an appointment may differ depending on the position.

The ACK Hub should therefore record the **appointing authority** whenever an appointment is documented.

## Financial Implications

Terms of service and appointments may have direct financial implications.

These may include:

* Salaries.
* Allowances.
* Benefits.
* Pension or retirement arrangements.
* Housing-related provisions.
* Official travel or other approved expenses.

The Board of Finance provides the wider financial governance framework within which such matters may be considered.

Specific remuneration figures should only be included where they are contained in an authorized and appropriate official source.

## Relationship with the Board of Finance

Terms of Service & Appointments operates within the wider financial governance structure of the Board of Finance.

The relationship can be understood as:

**Board of Finance**
→ Provincial financial governance and oversight.

**Terms of Service & Appointments**
→ Consideration of service and appointment matters with financial and administrative implications.

**Finance Office**
→ Professional financial administration and implementation.

**Provincial Secretariat**
→ Wider provincial administrative coordination.

These functions should remain distinct in the ACK Hub.

## Relationship with Human Resources

Terms of service and appointments overlap with human-resource administration but should not automatically be treated as identical.

Human-resource functions may include:

* Recruitment administration.
* Personnel records.
* Payroll.
* Leave administration.
* Performance management.
* Staff welfare.
* Employment documentation.

Terms of Service & Appointments is a governance/committee function concerned with the policy and decision-making aspects of service conditions and appointments.

The ACK Hub should therefore maintain separate records for HR administration where authoritative information is available.

## Relationship with the Provincial Secretariat

The Provincial Secretariat supports the administrative functioning of the Province.

Terms of Service & Appointments may interact with the Secretariat on matters concerning provincial personnel, appointments and service conditions.

However, the Secretariat and the committee represent different parts of the ACK organizational structure.

## Clergy and Lay Personnel

The ACK includes both ordained and lay personnel serving in different capacities.

Terms of service may therefore vary depending on:

* The nature of the office.
* Clergy or lay status.
* Provincial or diocesan level.
* Institutional employment.
* Applicable Church regulations.
* Specific appointment arrangements.

The ACK Hub should avoid applying one set of employment conditions to every category of Church worker unless an official policy explicitly establishes that common standard.

## Appointment Authority

Appointment authority may vary according to the office concerned.

An appointment record should ideally identify:

* Person appointed.
* Position.
* Appointing authority.
* Date of appointment.
* Term or duration where applicable.
* Effective date.
* Relevant policy or constitutional provision.
* Source document.

This structure will allow the ACK Hub to distinguish appointments from general staff listings.

## Historical and Current Structure

The ACK Decade Strategy 2018–2027 identifies **Terms of Service and Appointments** within the provincial financial structure.

The current ACK provincial structure also identifies **Terms of Service & Appointments** under the Board of Finance.

The exact membership, mandate and responsibilities may change over time.

Historical committee arrangements should therefore be linked to their source period.

## Governance and Accountability

Appointments and terms of service are connected to the broader governance and accountability system of the ACK.

Relevant sources may include:

* ACK Constitution.
* Provincial Synod decisions.
* Provincial Standing Committee decisions.
* Board of Finance decisions.
* Terms of service policies.
* Appointment notices.
* Official circulars.
* Human-resource policies.
* Institutional policies.

The highest applicable authority should be recorded where known.

## Data Classification

For the ACK Hub, appointment information should be structured separately from general organizational information.

Recommended fields include:

| Field                | Description                           |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Person               | Name of appointee                     |
| Position             | Office or role                        |
| Level                | Provincial, diocesan or institutional |
| Appointing Authority | Authority responsible for appointment |
| Appointment Date     | Date appointment was made             |
| Effective Date       | Date service begins                   |
| Term                 | Duration where applicable             |
| Terms of Service     | Relevant conditions                   |
| Source               | Official document                     |
| Status               | Current or historical                 |

The ACK Hub should distinguish between:

1. Appointment decisions.
2. Terms of service.
3. Employment administration.
4. Current staff listings.
5. Historical appointments.
6. Institutional appointments.
7. Clergy appointments.

Current appointments and service conditions should always be verified against the latest official ACK source.

Sensitive personnel information should not be included unless it is officially published and appropriate for the knowledge base.
