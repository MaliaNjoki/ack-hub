# ACK Budget Committee

## Overview

The **Budget Committee** is one of the committees identified under the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK) Board of Finance.

It supports the financial planning and budgetary responsibilities of the Provincial Board of Finance and helps connect the Church's priorities with available financial resources.

The current ACK structure identifies the Budget Committee as part of the Board of Finance. Historical ACK strategy documents have also referred to a **Budgets Committee** within the provincial financial structure.

## Purpose

The Budget Committee supports the preparation, consideration and oversight of the ACK's budgetary framework.

Its work helps ensure that financial planning takes account of:

* The mission and priorities of the Church.
* Planned provincial programmes.
* Operational requirements.
* Institutional needs.
* Available financial resources.
* Diocesan and ministry-related requirements.
* Financial sustainability.

## Budget Planning

Budget planning provides a framework for estimating income and expenditure over a defined financial period.

Within the ACK context, budget planning may involve consideration of:

* Expected income.
* Planned expenditure.
* Provincial programmes.
* Personnel and administrative costs.
* Ministry activities.
* Education and training.
* Mission activities.
* Social-development programmes.
* Institutional commitments.
* Capital requirements.

Specific figures should only be included in the ACK Hub when supported by an official budget, financial report or other authoritative ACK record.

## Budgetary Appropriations

The Board of Finance has a documented responsibility for considering **budgetary appropriations**.

The Budget Committee supports this broader financial governance process by examining financial requirements and planned allocations before matters proceed through the appropriate Church governance structures.

Budgetary decisions should therefore be understood as part of the wider ACK governance system rather than as isolated accounting activities.

## Relationship with the Board of Finance

The Budget Committee operates under the wider mandate of the Provincial Board of Finance.

### Board of Finance

Provides provincial financial governance and oversight.

### Budget Committee

Supports the detailed consideration of budgetary planning and appropriations.

### Finance Office

Provides professional and administrative financial operations.

These three functions should remain distinct in the ACK Hub.

## Relationship with the Finance Office

The Budget Committee should not be confused with the Provincial Finance Office.

The **Budget Committee** is a governance/committee structure.

The **Finance Office** is part of the provincial administrative and professional staff structure.

The Finance Office may provide financial information and technical support required for budgeting, while the committee considers budgetary matters within its governance mandate.

## Relationship with Provincial Programmes

The provincial budget supports activities across the different areas of the ACK.

These may include:

* Mission and evangelism.
* Education and theological training.
* Social services and development.
* Youth and children's ministry.
* Communication.
* Administration.
* Provincial governance.
* Institutional activities.
* Other approved Church programmes.

Budget allocations should therefore be understood in relation to the Church's overall priorities and approved plans.

## Relationship with Dioceses

The provincial financial structure also has a relationship with diocesan financial needs.

The Board of Finance has a documented role in considering grants and loans to dioceses.

The Budget Committee's work may therefore contribute to the broader provincial financial planning framework within which diocesan support is considered.

However, provincial and diocesan budgets should be treated as separate records because they belong to different levels of Church administration.

## Budget Monitoring

Budgeting is not limited to preparation.

Effective financial governance also involves monitoring planned expenditure against approved allocations.

Relevant financial records may include:

* Approved budgets.
* Revised budgets.
* Budget implementation reports.
* Financial statements.
* Variance reports.
* Audit reports.

The ACK Hub should preserve these as separate documents where officially available.

## Historical Structure

The ACK Decade Strategy 2018–2027 identifies a **Budgets Committee** under the Board of Finance.

The current ACK website uses the term **Budget Committee**.

For knowledge-base purposes, these references should be connected but not assumed to represent exactly the same committee wording across every period.

### Historical Reference

**Budgets Committee**
Source period: ACK Decade Strategy 2018–2027.

### Current Reference

**Budget Committee**
Source: current ACK provincial structure.
