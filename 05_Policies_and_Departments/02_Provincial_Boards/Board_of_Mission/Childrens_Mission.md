# Children’s Mission

## 1. Overview

The Children’s Mission is one of the four departments under the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK) Provincial Board of Mission. The other departments are the Mothers’ Union (MU), Kenya Anglican Men’s Association (KAMA), and Kenya Anglican Youth Organization (KAYO).

The Board of Mission coordinates the mission of the Church, including evangelism, discipleship, preaching, salvation, and the spiritual growth of Christians. The Children’s Mission forms part of this mission by supporting ministry and Christian formation among children.

**Source:** ACK, Board of Mission.

## 4. Ministry Focus

ACK describes its Children’s Ministry as part of the Church's wider mission and Christian formation.

An ACK anniversary publication describes the Children’s Ministry as targeting children approximately **0–12 years**.

The ministry supports the spiritual, educational, social and character formation of children through church-based programmes and activities.

---

## 5. Children’s Ministry Activities

According to ACK's published material, Children's Ministry includes or supports:

### 5.1 Crèches

Some congregations establish crèches to care for children below approximately three years of age.

### 5.2 Sunday School

Children above the early-childhood stage participate in Sunday School.

Sunday School provides a structured setting for Christian teaching and formation.

ACK's Decade Strategy 2018–2027 specifically identifies the development of a **theologically sound and harmonised Sunday School curriculum** as a strategic action area.

### 5.3 Boys’ and Girls’ Brigade

The disciplined Boys’ and Girls’ Brigade form part of the Church's ministry to children.

ACK describes these programmes as helping children develop life skills and discipline based on Scripture.

### 5.4 Children’s Conferences

ACK's published description includes conferences involving:

* Children
* Teachers
* Brigade officers

### 5.5 Music and Drama

Children's Ministry activities also include music and drama festivals, providing opportunities for children's participation, expression and development.

## 6. Christian Formation

Children's Mission contributes to the Church's broader mission of:

* Christian teaching
* Discipleship
* Faith formation
* Scripture-based character development
* Participation in Church life
* Development of life skills

The ministry should therefore be understood as more than childcare or Sunday activities; it is part of ACK's mission and Christian formation structure.

## 7. Sunday School Curriculum

The ACK Decade Strategy 2018–2027 identifies Sunday School curriculum development as a strategic area under wholesome evangelism and formation.

The strategy calls for a theologically sound and harmonised Sunday School curriculum and identifies the need to address emerging issues affecting children and young people.

## 8. Children and Environmental Stewardship

Children's Ministry also has a documented connection with ACK's environmental mission.

The ACK Green Anglicans concept document identifies **Children Ministry through Sunday School and Boys & Girls Brigades** as a channel through which children can participate in creation care.

## 9. Diocesan and Local Implementation

Children's Ministry is implemented beyond the provincial level through diocesan and local Church structures.

ACK's diocesan information identifies Children's Ministry or Sunday School structures and, in some dioceses, specific Children's Ministry coordinators.

The exact title and organisational arrangement may vary by diocese.

Examples of terms appearing in ACK diocesan records include:

* Children’s Ministry
* Sunday School
* Children’s Ministry Coordinator
* Youth and Children’s Ministry

**Data-quality rule:** Diocesan officers and structures should be recorded against the specific diocese and should not automatically be treated as provincial officers.

## 10. Relationship With Other ACK Ministries

Children's Mission operates within the wider mission structure of ACK and interacts with other ministries where their work overlaps.

### Board of Mission

Children's Mission is directly placed under the Provincial Board of Mission.

### KAYO

KAYO focuses on youth ministry and should not be merged with Children's Mission. The age categories and organisational structures should be kept distinct unless an official ACK source states otherwise.

### Mothers’ Union

The Mothers’ Union is a separate department under the Board of Mission but may support family and children's ministry activities.

### KAMA

KAMA is a separate department under the Board of Mission.

### Chaplaincy

Chaplaincy is a separate ministry area and may serve children in schools and other institutions.

### Green Anglicans

Children participate in environmental stewardship through Sunday School and Boys & Girls Brigades, but Green Anglicans remains a separate ministry initiative within the Board of Mission structure.
