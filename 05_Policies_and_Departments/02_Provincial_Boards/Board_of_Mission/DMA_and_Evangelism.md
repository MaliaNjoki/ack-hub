# Anglican Church of Kenya – Diocesan Missionary Association (DMA) and Evangelism

## 1. Overview

The **Diocesan Missionary Association (DMA)** and **Evangelism** form part of the mission and evangelistic work of the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK).

The ACK identifies **DMA & Evangelism** as one of the areas through which the Board of Mission carries out its evangelistic mandate.

The Board of Mission's overall responsibility is to spread the Gospel of Christ, with emphasis on:

* Ministry of the Word
* Evangelism
* Discipleship
* Preaching
* Salvation
* Spiritual growth
* Commitment to Christ

The Board coordinates mission work with dioceses through the relevant departments and structures.

---

## 2. Historical Background

The ACK's official history records that the **Diocesan Missionary Association was formed in 1962** in the Dioceses of **Fort Hall and Nakuru**.

This development formed part of the wider growth of organised missionary and evangelistic ministry within the Anglican Church in Kenya.

Over time, missionary work became established within diocesan structures across the Anglican Church of Kenya.

**Historical note:** Older ACK documents may use different descriptions of missionary structures and committees. The ACK Hub should preserve the date and source of such information rather than automatically treating historical arrangements as the current organisational structure.

---

## 3. Position within ACK

DMA and Evangelism are associated with the **Board of Mission**.

ACK's official Boards page describes the Board of Mission as the evangelistic arm of the Church and specifically lists:

* Mothers' Union
* Kenya Anglican Youth Organisation (KAYO)
* Sunday School
* Boys & Girls Brigade
* Hospital Chaplaincy
* Armed Forces Chaplaincy
* Diocesan Missionary Association (DMA) & Evangelism

These structures support the Church's responsibility to preach the Gospel and reach people with the Christian message.

---

## 4. Purpose of Evangelism

Evangelism is the sharing and proclamation of the Christian Gospel.

Within the ACK mission structure, evangelism contributes to:

* Proclaiming the Gospel
* Reaching people who have not heard or responded to the Christian message
* Encouraging people to follow Christ
* Strengthening Christian faith
* Supporting discipleship
* Establishing and strengthening Christian communities

Evangelism is therefore connected to both outreach and ongoing Christian formation.

---

## 5. Purpose of DMA

The Diocesan Missionary Association provides a structure through which missionary activity can be organised and supported within diocesan contexts.

Its work is connected with:

* Mission outreach
* Evangelism
* Christian witness
* Church planting and strengthening
* Missionary support
* Community engagement
* Discipleship

The exact organisation and activities of DMA may vary between dioceses.

---

## 6. Mission and Evangelism

The Board of Mission's official mandate places evangelism within the wider ministry of the Church.

Its work includes ensuring that Christians:

* Receive God's Word
* Develop a foundation in Christian faith
* Grow spiritually
* Develop commitment to Christ
* Participate in the mission of the Church

The ACK therefore treats evangelism as part of an ongoing process that includes proclamation, discipleship and spiritual growth.

---

## 7. Key Areas of Mission Work

### 7.1 Gospel Proclamation

Missionary and evangelistic work involves communicating the Christian Gospel through appropriate Church ministries and outreach activities.

### 7.2 Evangelistic Outreach

Evangelistic activities may take place through:

* Local church outreach
* Mission activities
* Diocesan programmes
* Community engagement
* Public Christian witness
* Special evangelistic events

The exact form of outreach depends on the local diocesan context.

### 7.3 Discipleship

Evangelism is linked to helping people develop an ongoing Christian faith.

Discipleship may involve:

* Bible teaching
* Prayer
* Fellowship
* Christian instruction
* Mentorship
* Participation in Church life

### 7.4 Missionary Support

Mission structures may facilitate support for people engaged in missionary ministry.

Support can include:

* Prayer
* Financial support
* Logistical support
* Training
* Community mobilisation

Specific arrangements should be verified from the relevant diocesan or provincial source.

### 7.5 Church Growth

Mission and evangelism contribute to the growth and strengthening of Christian communities.

This may include establishing new ministry areas and strengthening existing congregations.

---

## 8. Diocesan Missionary Areas

Missionary work is not limited to established parish structures.

ACK's diocesan information includes **Diocesan Missionary Areas**, demonstrating that the Church also maintains mission structures in areas where organised diocesan ministry is developing.

The ACK diocesan directory currently identifies missionary areas including:

* Garissa Missionary Area
* Lodwar Missionary Area

The exact status and administration of missionary areas may change over time and should therefore be verified against current diocesan information.

---

## 9. DMA at Diocesan Level

DMA structures may appear within individual dioceses alongside other diocesan departments.

For example, ACK's diocesan information lists **Diocesan Missionary Association** among the departments or structures in some dioceses.

The Diocese of Mount Kenya South, for example, lists **Missionary Evangelism (DMA)** alongside other diocesan departments.

This demonstrates that DMA is not simply a provincial concept; it has a practical diocesan implementation.

---

## 11. Mission and Community Engagement

Evangelism takes place within communities and therefore interacts with the social and practical circumstances of the people being served.

Mission activities may involve:

* Community visits
* Outreach
* Christian teaching
* Prayer
* Fellowship
* Community mobilisation
* Support for vulnerable communities

The nature of these activities depends on the particular mission context.

---

## 12. Missionary Formation and Discipleship

Effective mission requires more than outreach.

The ACK Board of Mission describes its work in terms of both evangelism and helping Christians develop a strong foundation in God's Word.

Mission therefore connects with:

1. Proclamation
2. Response to the Gospel
3. Discipleship
4. Spiritual growth
5. Christian service

This creates a continuing pathway from evangelistic outreach to participation in the life and mission of the Church.

---

## 13. Provincial and Diocesan Relationship

The provincial Board of Mission provides the wider framework for ACK mission and evangelism.

Dioceses implement mission work within their geographical and community contexts.

The relationship can therefore be understood as:

**Provincial ACK**

↓

**Board of Mission**

↓

**DMA / Mission & Evangelism structures**

↓

**Diocesan mission work**

↓

**Parishes, mission areas and local communities**

This structure enables the Church's wider mission mandate to be implemented at local level.

---

## 14. Historical Governance

The ACK Decade Strategy 2018–2027 lists the **DMA Committee** under the Board of Mission.

The same strategy identifies other Board of Mission committees including:

* Children's Ministry Committee
* KAYO Committee
* Mothers' Union Committee
* KAMA Committee
* DMA Committee
* Chaplaincy Committee

This is useful historical/strategy-period information and should be distinguished from the current ACK website's presentation of the Board of Mission.

---

## 15. Current Organisational Note

ACK's current website describes the Board of Mission as responsible for spreading the Gospel and lists its current departments.

At the same time, the ACK Boards page provides a broader description of the Board of Mission's evangelistic arm and explicitly includes DMA & Evangelism.

Therefore, the ACK Hub should preserve both:

* The **current official structure**, and
* The **historical/strategy-period structure** where relevant.

This prevents older committee names from being incorrectly presented as current departments.
