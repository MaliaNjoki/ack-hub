# Anglican Church of Kenya – Mothers’ Union

## 1. Overview

The Mothers’ Union (MU) is a major ministry within the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK) and operates under the **Board of Mission**.

The Mothers’ Union brings together Anglican women for Christian fellowship, prayer, service, family-life support, community transformation and the furtherance of the Gospel of Jesus Christ.

At the provincial level, the Mothers’ Union has a dedicated department and coordinates its work with diocesan Mothers’ Union structures.

The ACK describes the Mothers’ Union as a movement concerned with strengthening and preserving marriage and Christian family life. Its work extends beyond church fellowship to community development, mentorship, advocacy, child protection and support for vulnerable people.

---

## 2. History of the Mothers’ Union in Kenya

The Mothers’ Union was introduced in Kenya in **1918** at All Saints’ Cathedral, initially among European women.

The first African Mothers’ Union members were enrolled on **4 March 1956** at Mwongoiya Church, an area now within the Anglican Diocese of Mount Kenya South.

Mrs. Gladys Beecher, wife of Bishop Leonard Beecher, played an important role in establishing the Mothers’ Union among African women in Kenya.

The movement subsequently expanded across Kenya. Following the division of the Church of the Province of East Africa into the Provinces of Kenya and Tanzania, Mrs. Eseri Olang, wife of Archbishop Manasses Olang, became the leader of the Mothers’ Union movement in Kenya.

The Mothers’ Union subsequently developed structures and activities across the dioceses of the Anglican Church of Kenya.

**Historical note:** ACK's official sources contain both historical descriptions of the Mothers’ Union as a movement and current references to it as a provincial department within the Board of Mission. The knowledge base should preserve this distinction rather than treating historical and current terminology as identical.

## 3. Purpose and Aim

The principal purpose of the Mothers’ Union is to strengthen and preserve:

* Christian marriage
* Christian family life
* Parenting and the Christian upbringing of children
* Fellowship among Christians
* Prayer, worship and service
* Care and support for people experiencing adversity
* Community wellbeing and transformation

The Mothers’ Union combines Christian ministry with practical programmes addressing family and community needs.

---

## 4. Objectives

According to the Anglican Church of Kenya, the objectives of the Mothers’ Union are:

1. To uphold Christ’s teaching on the nature of marriage and promote wider understanding of it.
2. To encourage parents to bring up their children in the faith and life of the Church.
3. To maintain a worldwide fellowship of Christians united in prayer, worship and service.
4. To promote conditions in society favourable to stable family life and the protection of children.
5. To help and comfort people experiencing adversity.

---

## 5. Motto

The Mothers’ Union motto is:

> **“I can do all things through him (Christ) who strengthens me.”**

The motto is drawn from **Philippians 4:13**.

## 6. Core Areas of Work

The Mothers’ Union participates in a range of activities at provincial, diocesan, parish and local levels.

### 6.1 Christian Fellowship and Prayer

Activities include:

* Fellowships
* Prayer meetings
* Worship
* Christian teaching
* Spiritual encouragement
* Christian mentorship

These activities support members in their faith and service within the Church.

### 6.2 Marriage and Family Life

The Mothers’ Union promotes healthy Christian family life through:

* Marriage support
* Family-life teaching
* Parenting guidance
* Encouraging Christian parenting
* Programmes that strengthen families

### 6.3 Mentorship

The Mothers’ Union conducts mentorship activities aimed at supporting individuals, families and communities.

Mentorship may address:

* Christian growth
* Family relationships
* Parenting
* Personal development
* Life skills
* Community responsibility

### 6.4 Child Protection

The Mothers’ Union advocates for the protection and wellbeing of children.

Its work includes raising awareness about issues affecting children and promoting environments in which children can grow safely within families and communities.

### 6.5 Community Advocacy

The Mothers’ Union participates in campaigns and advocacy addressing social challenges.

ACK identifies areas including:

* Domestic violence
* Child marriage
* Female genital mutilation (FGM)
* HIV/AIDS and sexually transmitted infections
* Drug and substance abuse
* Children’s welfare

### 6.6 Economic Empowerment

The Mothers’ Union has been involved in initiatives intended to improve household and community livelihoods.

These include:

* Microfinance initiatives
* Agri-business
* Community economic activities
* Other livelihood-support programmes

### 6.7 Care and Support

The Mothers’ Union participates in caring for vulnerable people and communities.

Examples identified by ACK include:

* Caring centres for needy children
* Supporting people experiencing adversity
* Community-based welfare activities

## 8. Diocesan and Local Structures

The Mothers’ Union operates throughout the dioceses of the Anglican Church of Kenya.

Dioceses have their own Mothers’ Union structures and arrangements for implementing activities.

The provincial Mothers’ Union therefore provides a wider coordinating framework while diocesan and local structures conduct activities according to their respective contexts.

**Data note:** Individual diocesan Mothers’ Union leaders, contacts and programmes should be stored with the relevant diocesan information rather than assumed to be identical across all dioceses.

---

## 9. Relationship with the Board of Mission

The Mothers’ Union forms part of the ACK **Board of Mission**.

The Board of Mission focuses on:

* The Ministry of the Word
* Evangelism
* Discipleship
* Christian formation
* Spiritual growth
* Coordinating mission activities across the Church

The Mothers’ Union contributes to this mission through Christian formation, family ministry, fellowship, prayer, mentorship, community engagement and evangelistic activities.

ACK's current Board of Mission page identifies the Mothers’ Union alongside:

* Kenya Anglican Men’s Association (KAMA)
* Kenya Anglican Youth Organisation (KAYO)
* Children’s Mission

ACK's broader Board of Mission information also references areas such as Sunday School, Boys & Girls Brigade, chaplaincy, DMA and evangelism. The knowledge base should preserve the current Board of Mission structure while recording broader/historical programme descriptions separately where necessary.

---

## 10. Relationship with Dioceses

The Mothers’ Union works through the diocesan structures of the Anglican Church of Kenya.

Its activities may therefore differ between dioceses depending on:

* Local needs
* Diocesan priorities
* Available resources
* Community circumstances
* Local Mothers’ Union structures

The provincial level provides coordination and a common identity, while dioceses and local church structures implement activities within their communities.

## 14. Data Quality and Update Rules

The ACK Hub should distinguish between:

1. **Current information** – current provincial leadership, departments and officially recognised structures.
2. **Historical information** – establishment dates, former structures and historical office holders.
3. **Diocesan information** – information applying to a specific ACK diocese.
4. **Programme information** – activities and initiatives that may change over time.

Current office holders and administrative contacts should be rechecked against the official ACK website before being treated as current.

The system should not automatically assume that a programme or structure mentioned in an older ACK document remains unchanged today.
