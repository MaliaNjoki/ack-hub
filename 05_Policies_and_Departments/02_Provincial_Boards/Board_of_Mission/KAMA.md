# Anglican Church of Kenya – Kenya Anglican Men’s Association (KAMA)

## 1. Overview

The **Kenya Anglican Men’s Association (KAMA)** is a ministry of the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK) under the **Board of Mission**.

KAMA provides a structure through which Anglican men participate in Christian fellowship, spiritual growth, Church mission, family ministry, community service and national life.

The association operates across the ACK dioceses and forms part of the wider mission structure of the Church.

---

## 2. History

KAMA traces its roots to the work of the Mothers’ Union.

According to the Anglican Church of Kenya, retired Archbishop Manasses Kuria was impressed by the work of the Mothers’ Union and saw the need for a corresponding men's organisation.

The organisation was initially known as the **Fathers’ Union**.

The first Fathers’ Union was established at **Mwongoiya**, the same parish associated with the early development of the Mothers’ Union among African women.

The organisation subsequently developed and expanded across the dioceses of the Anglican Church of Kenya.

It later became known as the **Kenya Anglican Men’s Association (KAMA)** and became an established part of ACK ministry.

---

## 3. Position within ACK

KAMA is one of the four departments currently identified by the ACK as operating under the **Board of Mission**.

The four departments are:

1. Mothers’ Union (MU)
2. Kenya Anglican Men’s Association (KAMA)
3. Kenya Anglican Youth Organisation (KAYO)
4. Children’s Mission

The Board of Mission is responsible for the mission of the Church, including spreading the Gospel, evangelism, discipleship, preaching and helping Christians grow in faith and commitment to Christ.

---

## 4. Motto

KAMA's motto is:

> **“As for me and my household, we will serve the Lord.”**

The motto is based on **Joshua 24:15**.

---

## 5. Membership

According to the ACK's official KAMA description, membership is drawn from **Anglican clergy and laity**.

The official ACK description states that members are generally:

* Men aged over 25 years
* Married men, with priority given to married members
* Individuals who identify with the mission of the association
* Publicly enrolled as KAMA members

The ACK also notes that membership may, in some cases, be open to men below 25 who are married.

**Data note:** Membership requirements should be treated according to the current official KAMA constitution or ACK guidance if more detailed membership rules are added to the knowledge base.

## 6. Objectives

The objectives of KAMA include:

### 6.1 Spiritual Growth

To help members grow in:

* Spirit
* Mind
* Body
* Faith
* Christian character

The association seeks to nurture members for Christian service and good works.

### 6.2 Church Mission

KAMA encourages members to take an active role in the mission of the Church.

This includes participation in:

* Evangelism
* Christian service
* Church activities
* Mission programmes
* Supporting the work of the Church

### 6.3 Christian Principles in Society

KAMA promotes Christian principles in:

* National life
* Civic life
* Community life
* Social life

It encourages members to apply Christian values beyond the Church.

### 6.4 Service to Others

KAMA encourages members to serve people who are:

* Distressed
* Disadvantaged
* Living with disabilities

This reflects the association's emphasis on Christian service and compassion.

### 6.5 Stewardship

KAMA encourages members to use their God-given gifts responsibly as stewards of God's varied grace.

Members are also encouraged to use their resources in supporting the work of the Church.

### 6.6 Family and Marriage

KAMA seeks to honour Christian marriage and promote Christian family values.

Family life is therefore an important area of KAMA ministry.

### 6.7 Fellowship and Unity

KAMA promotes:

* Christian fellowship
* Love
* Unity
* Spiritual enthusiasm

These values apply both within the association and in wider society.

### 6.8 Christian Participation in Society

The association encourages Anglican men and women to participate positively as Christians in the:

* Political life
* Social life
* Economic life

of the nation.

This should be understood as a description of the association's stated objective rather than as an endorsement of any particular political party, candidate or position.

### 6.9 Advancing God's Kingdom

A central objective is to promote God's Kingdom and set forth His glory.

## 7. Key Activities

KAMA supports spiritual growth, personal development and Christian ministry through various activities.

### 7.1 Prayer and Bible Study

KAMA organises regular:

* Prayer meetings
* Bible studies
* Fellowship meetings

These activities support spiritual growth and Christian formation.

### 7.2 Conferences and Seminars

KAMA organises:

* Conferences
* Seminars
* Educational activities
* Capacity-building activities

These provide opportunities for learning, fellowship and personal development.

### 7.3 Educational Trips and Exchange Visits

The association may organise:

* Educational trips
* Exchange visits
* Other learning activities

These activities support exposure, fellowship and capacity building.

### 7.4 Fundraising and Church Support

KAMA contributes to fundraising for:

* Association activities
* Special Church projects
* Other approved Church initiatives

Members are encouraged to use their resources to support the mission of the Church.

### 7.5 Support for Christian Ministry

KAMA supports members who offer themselves for full-time Christian service, including:

* Missionary work
* Evangelism
* Pastoral ministry

Support may include prayer and financial assistance.

---

## 8. Personal Development

KAMA's ministry is not limited to worship and fellowship.

Its activities also support personal development through:

* Capacity building
* Educational programmes
* Fellowship
* Mentorship
* Family-life engagement
* Responsible stewardship
* Community service

The association therefore provides opportunities for members to develop spiritually and practically while serving the Church.

---

## 9. Family Ministry

Family life is an important part of KAMA's stated objectives.

KAMA promotes:

* Christian marriage
* Christian family values
* Responsible family leadership
* Fellowship within families
* Faith development within the household

The association's motto also places service to God within the context of the household.

---

## 10. Community and Social Service

KAMA encourages Christian service to people experiencing hardship or disadvantage.

Its objectives specifically include service to:

* Distressed people
* Disadvantaged people
* Persons with disabilities

KAMA members may therefore participate in community-support activities organised through local churches, dioceses and the wider ACK mission structure.

---

## 11. Diocesan and Local Presence

KAMA operates across the dioceses of the Anglican Church of Kenya.

The ACK's diocesan information identifies KAMA alongside other ministry structures such as:

* Mothers’ Union
* KAYO
* Children’s Ministry
* Mission and Evangelism
* Chaplaincy

This means that KAMA ministry is implemented within the local context of individual dioceses and churches.

**Data note:** The names and positions of diocesan KAMA coordinators may change. Such information should be stored with the relevant diocesan record and verified against the latest ACK or diocesan source.

---

## 12. Relationship with the Board of Mission

KAMA is part of the **Board of Mission**.

The Board of Mission provides the wider mission framework for:

* Evangelism
* Discipleship
* Preaching
* Christian formation
* Spiritual growth
* Mission coordination

KAMA contributes to these objectives through men's ministry, Christian fellowship, Bible study, prayer, evangelism, service, family ministry and community engagement.

---

## 13. KAMA and the Wider ACK Mission

KAMA is one component of ACK's wider mission ministry.

Other current Board of Mission departments include:

* Mothers’ Union
* Kenya Anglican Youth Organisation
* Children’s Mission

ACK's strategic documents have also historically listed committees dealing with areas such as Children's Ministry, KAYO, Mothers’ Union, KAMA, DMA and Chaplaincy.

**Data note:** The current ACK website should be used as the primary reference when describing the present organisational structure, while older strategic documents should be identified as historical or strategy-period information.
