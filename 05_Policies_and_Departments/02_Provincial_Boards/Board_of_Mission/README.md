# Anglican Church of Kenya – Board of Mission

## Overview

The Board of Mission is one of the principal provincial Boards of the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK).

It is responsible for coordinating and supporting the mission, evangelism, discipleship and Christian formation work of the Church at the provincial level.

## Core Areas

The Board of Mission provides oversight and coordination for major mission-related ministries and organisations within the ACK provincial structure.

These include:

* Mothers' Union (MU)
* Kenya Anglican Men's Association (KAMA)
* Kenya Anglican Youth Organisation (KAYO)
* Children's Mission
* Chaplaincy
* Evangelism and mission programmes

## Key Functions

The Board's mission-related responsibilities include:

1. Supporting the spread of the Gospel.
2. Promoting evangelism and Christian discipleship.
3. Supporting Christian formation and spiritual growth.
4. Coordinating mission-related ministries and programmes.
5. Supporting the participation of different groups in the mission of the Church.
6. Encouraging effective ministry and outreach within the ACK.
7. Providing provincial-level coordination for mission activities.

## Relationship to ACK Governance

The Board of Mission operates within the wider provincial governance structure of the Anglican Church of Kenya.

The provincial governance system includes:

* Provincial Synod
* Provincial Standing Committee of Synod
* House of Bishops
* Clergy
* Laity
* Provincial Boards

The Board of Mission should therefore be understood as a **provincial Board**, rather than as an independent organisation separate from ACK.
