# Provincial Permanent Commission on Division and Creation of Dioceses

## Overview

The Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK) has used a **Provincial Permanent Commission of Division and Creation of Dioceses** to examine and make recommendations concerning proposals for the division or creation of dioceses.

An official ACK report records the Commission's involvement in the elevation of the Kisii Area Bishopric into the Diocese of Upper Southern Nyanza.

## Official Name

**Provincial Permanent Commission of Division and Creation of Dioceses**

The official ACK source uses this terminology.

## Type

**Type:** Provincial Permanent Commission

**Area:** Provincial ecclesiastical governance and diocesan organisation

**Status:** Documented in official ACK records

**Verification date:** September 2026

## Mandate

The Commission's documented role includes examining proposals concerning:

* Division of existing dioceses.
* Creation of new dioceses.
* Elevation of bishoprics or areas into diocesan status.
* Ecclesiastical and organisational arrangements associated with proposed new dioceses.

The precise full terms of reference of the Commission should be obtained from the authorised provincial records where required.

## Documented Example: Upper Southern Nyanza Diocese

An official ACK report concerning Bishop John Orina Omangi records that in **2022**, the Provincial Permanent Commission of Division and Creation of Dioceses was chaired by **Rt. Rev. Moses Masamba, Bishop of Mbeere Diocese**.

The Commission conducted scrutiny of a proposal to elevate the Kisii Area Bishopric into a Diocese.

Following the Commission's review, the proposal was presented to the Provincial Synod.

The Provincial Synod unanimously endorsed and adopted the Commission's motion.

The new Diocese of Upper Southern Nyanza was declared effective **1 January 2023**.

This provides a documented example of the Commission's role in the provincial process for creating a diocese.

## Relationship with Provincial Synod

The Commission does not replace the authority of the Provincial Synod.

The documented process demonstrates the following sequence:

```text
Proposal
   ↓
Provincial Permanent Commission
   ↓
Scrutiny / Review
   ↓
Recommendation or Motion
   ↓
Provincial Synod
   ↓
Synodical Consideration and Decision
```

The exact procedure for a particular diocesan proposal should be determined from the applicable Constitution, regulations and authorised provincial records.

## Relationship with the House of Bishops

The Commission operates within the wider episcopal and provincial governance framework of ACK.

Matters concerning the creation or division of dioceses may involve bishops and other provincial governance structures.

However, the AI should not infer the Commission's complete membership from one historical example.

## Commission Outputs

A Commission may produce or present:

* Findings from its review.
* Recommendations.
* Motions or proposals.
* Reports concerning proposed diocesan changes.
* Supporting information for consideration by the Provincial Synod.

A Commission recommendation should not automatically be described as a final Church decision.

## Important Governance Distinction

The Commission's role should be distinguished from that of:

### Provincial Synod

The Provincial Synod is a principal governing body of ACK and considers matters brought before it through the appropriate provincial processes.

### Standing Committee of Provincial Synod

The Standing Committee provides continuity in provincial governance between Synod sessions and handles matters within its constitutional authority.

### House of Bishops

The House of Bishops is one of the three houses of Provincial Synod and represents episcopal leadership.

### Diocese

A Diocese is an established ecclesiastical jurisdiction of the ACK.

The Commission assists with the process of considering proposed diocesan changes; it is not itself a Diocese.

## Data Quality Rules

The AI should:

1. Use the official Commission name where available.
2. Distinguish documented historical membership from current membership.
3. Never assume that a Commission mentioned in a historical document remains active in exactly the same form.
4. Distinguish a Commission's recommendation from the final decision of the Provincial Synod.
5. Verify diocesan creation or division dates against authoritative ACK records.
6. Avoid inventing terms of reference where the full document is unavailable.
