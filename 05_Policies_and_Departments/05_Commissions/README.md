# Anglican Church of Kenya – Provincial Commissions

## Overview

This folder documents Provincial Commissions and other specialised commission-level structures of the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK).

Provincial Commissions support the Church by providing specialised attention to particular areas of ministry, governance, professional expertise or Church life.

## Purpose

The purpose of this section is to:

* Document verified ACK Provincial Commissions.
* Explain the mandate and area of responsibility of each Commission.
* Record the relationship between Commissions and the wider ACK governance structure.
* Distinguish Commissions from Boards, departments, offices and institutions.
* Preserve historical Commission information where relevant.
* Support accurate retrieval by the ACK AI knowledge base.

## Governance Context

Provincial Commissions operate within the wider provincial governance framework of ACK.

They should be understood in relation to:

* Provincial Synod
* House of Bishops
* House of Clergy
* House of Laity
* Standing Committee of Provincial Synod
* Provincial Boards
* Provincial Secretariat
* Dioceses

The exact authority, membership and reporting arrangements of a Commission should be determined from the Constitution, Synod records, Commission terms of reference or other authoritative ACK documents.

## Commission vs Board

A **Provincial Board** is a principal provincial governance structure with an established area of responsibility, such as:

* Mission
* Social Services
* Education and Training
* Finance

A **Commission** is generally a more specialised structure established to address a defined area of responsibility.

The AI should not treat every committee, task force, working group or programme as a Provincial Commission.

## Commission vs Department

A department is normally part of the administrative or operational structure of the Provincial Secretariat.

A Commission is a governance or specialised structure whose authority and mandate should be established from authoritative ACK records.

For example:

* Mission Department → Provincial Secretariat
* Board of Mission → Provincial Board
* Provincial Commission → Specialised provincial structure

These structures may collaborate but should not be merged in the knowledge base.

## Verification Standard

The ACK website and publicly available Church documents do not always provide a single, continuously updated public register of every Provincial Commission.

Therefore, this folder follows a strict verification standard.

A Commission should be added as a current Commission only when supported by an authoritative source such as:

1. ACK Provincial Constitution
2. Provincial Synod records
3. Standing Committee records
4. Official ACK Commission documentation
5. Official ACK announcements or reports
6. Other authorised Church records

## Current vs Historical Commissions

The AI must distinguish between:

* Current Commissions
* Historical Commissions
* Temporary Commissions
* Special-purpose Commissions
* Proposed Commissions
* Commissions whose current status has not been verified

A Commission mentioned in an old document must not automatically be treated as currently active.

## Recommended Commission Record

Each individual Commission file should, where information is available, contain:

* Official name
* Commission type
* Mandate
* Authority or establishing body
* Membership
* Chairperson
* Secretariat/support structure
* Functions
* Reporting relationship
* Meetings
* Key outputs
* Current status
* Historical information
* Source
* Verification date

## Membership and Leadership

Commission membership and leadership may change through appointments, elections, Synod decisions or changes in the Commission's mandate.

Current names should therefore always be accompanied by:

* Source
* Date
* Role
* Verification date

Where current membership cannot be verified, the file should state:

> Current membership not verified from publicly available authoritative ACK records.

## AI Retrieval Guidance

When answering a question about an ACK Provincial Commission, the AI should:

1. Identify the exact Commission.
2. Determine whether it is current or historical.
3. Identify its establishing or reporting authority where available.
4. Retrieve its verified mandate.
5. Distinguish it from Boards, departments and committees.
6. Avoid assuming current membership.
7. Cite the authoritative source.
8. State when information is unavailable or requires verification.

## Data Quality Rules

The ACK Hub must not:

* Invent Commission names.
* Assume a committee is a Commission.
* Convert a ministry programme into a Commission.
* Treat historical membership as current.
* Infer a Commission's authority from its name alone.
* Present unverified leadership information as current.
