# ACK Prayer Book

## Purpose

This folder provides a structured reference for prayer books and related liturgical publications associated with the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK).

It is designed for ACK Hub retrieval, research, and information management. The folder records the identity, purpose, language, historical context, source information, and verification status of ACK prayer-book resources without reproducing copyrighted liturgical texts.

---

## 1. ACK Liturgical Publishing Context

ACK Uzima Publishing House traces its origins to the publication of liturgical works for the Anglican Church in Kenya, including prayer books and hymn books.

ACK identifies liturgical publishing as a principal function of Uzima Publishing House. Its published catalogue has included prayer books, modern services, confirmation resources, the Church Lectionary and Diary, and other materials used for worship and Christian formation.

### Primary institutional source

**ACK Uzima Publishing House**

The official ACK description of Uzima records its history, mandate, liturgical publications, and development of *Our Modern Services*.

---

## 2. Our Modern Services

*Our Modern Services* is a major ACK liturgical resource.

The ACK Decade Strategy records that in **2002** the Provincial Liturgical Committee released *Our Modern Services* as a complete alternative to the Book of Common Prayer.

ACK Uzima Publishing House states that *Our Modern Services* was adopted by the Province of Kenya in 2002 and subsequently produced in languages where the Anglican Church has a significant presence.

Detailed information is maintained in:

`01_Our_Modern_Services.md`

---

## 3. Related Prayer-Book Resources

ACK Uzima's published catalogue identifies several related resources, including:

* *Kitabu Kipya Cha Ibaada* — Kiswahili translation of *Our Modern Services*.
* *Kiswahili Prayer Book* — identified by Uzima as an abridged version of *Kitabu Kipya Cha Ibaada*.
* *Modern English Services*.
* *Mothers' Union Prayer Book*.
* Other liturgical materials and worship resources.

Each resource should be documented separately so that ACK Hub does not incorrectly merge distinct publications.

---

## 4. Languages

ACK's liturgical publishing history includes materials in English, Kiswahili and a number of Kenyan languages.

ACK Uzima specifically records editions or services of *Our Modern Services* in:

* Kiswahili
* Kikuyu
* Luo
* Kamba

It also records limited services in:

* Kisii
* Luyia

Language availability should be treated as **resource-specific**. The availability of one service or edition in a language must not automatically be interpreted as availability of the entire prayer-book collection in that language.

---

## 5. Relationship to the Liturgy Section

The `Liturgy/` folder contains structured references to worship and sacramental services.

The `Prayer_Book/` folder is different:

* `Liturgy/` describes **services and liturgical practice**.
* `Prayer_Book/` identifies and documents **prayer books and published liturgical resources**.

Where the same service appears in both areas, the files should cross-reference each other rather than duplicate large sections of content.

---

## 6. Copyright and Text Handling

The Prayer Book section is intended for structured information retrieval and reference.

The repository should **not reproduce complete copyrighted prayer-book texts, prayers, Eucharistic liturgies, collects, hymns, responses, rubrics, or other substantial copyrighted material** unless the material is confirmed to be legally available for reproduction.

For copyrighted resources, ACK Hub should instead store:

* title;
* resource type;
* publisher;
* language;
* historical context;
* intended use;
* service categories;
* source information;
* verification status;
* retrieval guidance;
* links or references to authorised sources where available.

Short quotations should only be used where legally appropriate and necessary for identification or analysis.

---

## 7. Source Hierarchy

Prayer-book information should follow this source priority:

1. **Official ACK sources**
2. **ACK Uzima Publishing House**
3. **Official ACK publications and authorised institutional documents**
4. **Authoritative library/catalogue records**
5. **Reliable secondary sources**, clearly identified as secondary
6. **Unverified online material**, which should not be treated as authoritative ACK information

Where sources disagree, the disagreement should be recorded rather than silently resolved.

---

## 8. Historical vs Current Information

ACK Hub must distinguish between:

* historical prayer books;
* current authorised resources;
* older editions;
* translations;
* abridged editions;
* individual services;
* resources listed in historical catalogues.

A publication appearing in an older Uzima catalogue should not automatically be described as a currently authorised or currently available ACK resource.

Current availability or status should be verified against the latest authoritative source where possible.

---

## 9. AI Retrieval Rules

When answering questions about ACK prayer books, ACK Hub should:

1. Identify the specific resource being requested.
2. Distinguish the Prayer Book from the wider `Liturgy/` collection.
3. Identify the language where relevant.
4. Distinguish current information from historical information.
5. Prefer official ACK and Uzima sources.
6. Avoid inventing prayer-book wording.
7. Avoid presenting general Anglican practice as an ACK-specific rule.
8. State when the exact text or current edition has not been verified.
9. Cross-reference the relevant `Liturgy/` file where a service is involved.
10. Preserve copyright boundaries when handling published liturgical texts.

## 10. Verification Status

Each Prayer Book resource should use one of the following evidence labels:

### VERIFIED — ACK

Directly supported by an official ACK source or authorised ACK publication.

### VERIFIED — UZIMA

Directly supported by ACK Uzima Publishing House.

### VERIFIED — CATALOGUE

Supported by a reliable bibliographic or catalogue record.

### CONTEXT — ANGLICAN

General Anglican or historical context that should not be presented as uniquely ACK-specific.

### PLACEHOLDER — VERIFY

A proposed category or detail requiring confirmation from an authoritative source.
