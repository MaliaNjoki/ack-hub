# ACK Liturgical Resources

## 1. Purpose

This file provides a structured reference to liturgical and worship resources associated with the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK).

It serves as a bridge between the individual prayer-book resources in this folder and the wider ACK liturgical, worship, publishing and church-calendar resources maintained elsewhere in ACK Hub.

It does not reproduce copyrighted liturgical texts.

---

## 2. ACK Liturgical Publishing

ACK's official publishing institution, **Uzima Publishing House**, has a long-standing role in publishing Christian and liturgical materials for the Anglican Church in Kenya.

The official ACK Uzima catalogue identifies resources including:

* *Our Modern Services*;
* *Kitabu Kipya Cha Ibaada*;
* Kiswahili Prayer Book;
* *Modern English Services*;
* Mothers' Union Prayer Book;
* hymn books;
* Church Lectionary and Diary;
* other Christian and educational publications.

**Evidence status:** VERIFIED — UZIMA

## 4. Our Modern Services

*Our Modern Services* is a major ACK modern liturgical resource.

ACK Uzima identifies it as the landmark liturgy adopted by the Province of Kenya in **2002**.

The ACK Decade Strategy 2018–2027 records its development in the context of the work of the Provincial Liturgical Committee.

## 9. Church Lectionary and Diary

The **Church Lectionary and Diary** is part of ACK's published liturgical resource environment.

It should be treated separately from the Prayer Book resources.

The Lectionary and Diary provides church-calendar and worship-planning information and should not be assumed to contain the complete text of the services in *Our Modern Services*.

ACK Hub maintains a separate calendar reference:

`../Liturgical_Seasons_and_Colours.md`

Current calendar information should be verified against the applicable ACK Church Diary and Lectionary or other authoritative current ACK source.

**Evidence status:** VERIFIED — UZIMA / ACK

The Prayer Book and Liturgy sections have different purposes.

### Liturgy

For example:

```text
Prayer_Book/
```

## 14. Language and Translation

ACK has a significant history of translating and publishing liturgical materials for Kenyan worshipping communities.

ACK Uzima identifies language editions or services associated with *Our Modern Services* in:

* English;
* Kiswahili;
* Kikuyu;
* Luo;
* Kamba.

It also identifies limited services in:

* Kisii;
* Luyia.

Language information must be interpreted at the **resource or service level**.

The existence of a resource in one language does not establish that every related liturgical resource exists in that language.

---

## 15. Historical vs Current Resources

ACK Hub should distinguish between:

### Historical resources

Publications or editions documented as part of ACK's historical liturgical development.

### Current resources

Resources whose current status, authorisation or availability has been verified from a current authoritative source.

### Related resources

Publications that support worship or Christian formation but are not themselves prayer books.

A historical publication should not automatically be labelled a current authorised resource.

---

## 16. Source Hierarchy

Use the following source hierarchy:

1. Official ACK sources
2. ACK Uzima Publishing House
3. Official ACK publications
4. Authoritative library and bibliographic records
5. Reliable secondary sources

When a current ACK source conflicts with an older catalogue, the newer authoritative source should be considered for current-status claims while the historical information may still be retained as historical evidence.

---

## 17. Copyright and Text Handling

ACK Hub should not reproduce complete copyrighted prayer books, service books, hymn books, prayers, liturgies, rubrics or substantial copyrighted passages.

For published resources, store:

* title;
* resource type;
* language;
* publisher;
* historical context;
* purpose;
* relationship to other resources;
* source;
* verification status;
* retrieval guidance.

Exact wording should only be reproduced where an authorised source and applicable rights permit it.

---

## 18. AI Retrieval Rules

When answering questions about ACK liturgical resources, ACK Hub should:

1. Identify the exact resource being requested.
2. Distinguish prayer books from service descriptions.
3. Distinguish *Our Modern Services* from *Modern English Services*.
4. Distinguish *Kitabu Kipya Cha Ibaada* from the Kiswahili Prayer Book.
5. Identify language only where verified.
6. Distinguish historical from current information.
7. Use official ACK and Uzima sources first.
8. Cross-reference the `Liturgy/` folder where relevant.
9. Avoid inventing tables of contents or prayer wording.
10. Preserve copyright boundaries.
11. Identify uncertainty where edition-level information is unavailable.
