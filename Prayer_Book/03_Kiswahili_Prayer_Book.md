## 4. Relationship to Our Modern Services

*Our Modern Services* was adopted by the Province of Kenya in **2002**.

ACK Uzima identifies *Kitabu Kipya Cha Ibaada* as its Swahili translation and separately identifies the Kiswahili Prayer Book as an abridged version of that translated resource.

Therefore, ACK Hub should preserve the publication relationship rather than assuming that the Kiswahili Prayer Book reproduces the complete contents of *Our Modern Services*.
