# Mothers' Union Prayer Book

## 1. Purpose

This file provides a structured reference to the **Mothers' Union Prayer Book**, a prayer resource listed by ACK Uzima Publishing House.

It is intended for ACK Hub research and information retrieval. It records verified information about the resource without reproducing its copyrighted prayers or liturgical text.

---

## 2. Resource Identity

**Title:** Mothers' Union Prayer Book

**Church context:** Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK)

**Language resources identified by ACK:** English, Kiswahili and Kikuyu

**Resource type:** Prayer resource

**Publishing institution:** ACK Uzima Publishing House

**Primary ministry context:** Mothers' Union

**Evidence status:** VERIFIED — UZIMA

ACK Uzima's official catalogue identifies the **Mothers' Union Prayer Book** as a separate prayer resource within its publishing programme.

---

## 3. Mothers' Union Context

The Mothers' Union is an established ministry within the Anglican Church of Kenya.

ACK's provincial structure includes a **Mothers' Union Department**, while Mothers' Union activities also operate through diocesan and local church structures.

The prayer book should therefore be understood within the worship, prayer, fellowship and family-life ministry context of Mothers' Union.

The existence of a Mothers' Union prayer resource does not, by itself, establish that every Mothers' Union activity follows one identical service format throughout all ACK dioceses.

ACK Uzima identifies material associated with the Mothers' Union Prayer Book in:
