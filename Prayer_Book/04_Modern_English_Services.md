# Modern English Services

## 1. Purpose

This file provides a structured reference to **Modern English Services**, a liturgical resource listed by ACK Uzima Publishing House.

It is intended for ACK Hub research and information retrieval. It documents the resource without reproducing its copyrighted liturgical text.

---

## 2. Resource Identity

**Title:** Modern English Services

**Church context:** Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK)

**Language:** Modern English

**Resource type:** Liturgical services

**Historical source:** 1662 Book of Common Prayer

**Publishing institution:** ACK Uzima Publishing House

**Evidence status:** VERIFIED — UZIMA

ACK Uzima's catalogue describes *Modern English Services* as services from the **1662 Book of Common Prayer that have been put into modern English**.

---

## 3. Relationship to the Book of Common Prayer

The resource has a direct historical relationship with the **1662 Book of Common Prayer (BCP)**.

ACK Uzima describes it as a modern-English rendering of services from the 1662 BCP.

This should be distinguished from:

* the original 1662 Book of Common Prayer;
* *Our Modern Services*;
* *Kitabu Kipya Cha Ibaada*;
* the Kiswahili Prayer Book.

These are related to Anglican liturgical publishing but are not interchangeable resources.

---

## 4. Relationship to Our Modern Services

ACK Hub should maintain a clear distinction between:

### Modern English Services

A resource based on services from the 1662 Book of Common Prayer rendered in modern English.

### Our Modern Services

The landmark ACK liturgy adopted by the Province of Kenya in 2002.

The two resources should therefore not be merged simply because both are modern-language liturgical resources.

See:

`01_Our_Modern_Services.md`

for the separate ACK reference to *Our Modern Services*.

---

## 5. Publishing Context

ACK Uzima Publishing House was founded to print and publish liturgical works for the Anglican Church in Kenya.

Its early publishing programme included translations of the Book of Common Prayer into Kenyan languages.

The later catalogue includes *Modern English Services* alongside *Our Modern Services*, *Kitabu Kipya Cha Ibaada*, the Kiswahili Prayer Book and the Mothers' Union Prayer Book.

**Evidence status:** VERIFIED — UZIMA
