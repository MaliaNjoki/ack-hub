# Our Modern Services

## 1. Purpose

This file provides a structured, evidence-based reference to **Our Modern Services**, a major liturgical resource of the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK).

It is intended for ACK Hub research and information retrieval. It records verified information about the resource without reproducing the copyrighted liturgical text.

---

## 2. Resource Identity

**Title:** Our Modern Services

**Church:** Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK)

**Liturgical status:** Major ACK liturgical resource

**Adoption:** 2002

**Publishing institution:** ACK Uzima Publishing House

**Historical context:** Developed as part of the Anglican Church of Kenya's movement toward liturgical forms suited to the Kenyan church and its worshipping communities.

ACK Uzima Publishing House describes *Our Modern Services* as the **landmark liturgy adopted in 2002 by the Province of Kenya**.

**Evidence status:** VERIFIED — ACK / UZIMA

---

## 3. Historical Development

ACK's liturgical publishing history includes earlier translations and adaptations of Anglican liturgical resources, including translations of the Book of Common Prayer into Kenyan languages.

The development of *Our Modern Services* represents a later stage in the development of Anglican liturgy in Kenya.

The official ACK Decade Strategy 2018–2027 records the development of a modern service of Holy Communion by the Provincial Liturgical Committee and the subsequent release of *Our Modern Services*.

The 2002 adoption should therefore be treated as an important milestone in the development of ACK's modern liturgical resources.

**Evidence status:** VERIFIED — ACK

---

## 4. Relationship to the Book of Common Prayer

*Our Modern Services* should not be treated as simply another edition of the Book of Common Prayer.

ACK's Decade Strategy describes *Our Modern Services* as a **complete alternative to the Book of Common Prayer** within the Province of Kenya.

The distinction is important for ACK Hub retrieval:

* **Book of Common Prayer (BCP):** historic Anglican liturgical tradition and source of earlier Anglican services.
* **Our Modern Services:** modern ACK liturgical resource adopted by the Province of Kenya in 2002.

ACK Hub should preserve this distinction when answering questions about Anglican and ACK liturgy.

**Evidence status:** VERIFIED — ACK

---

## 5. Publishing and Distribution

ACK Uzima Publishing House was established to print and publish liturgical works for the Anglican Church in Kenya.

Its historical publishing programme included:

* prayer books;
* hymn books;
* translations of Anglican liturgical resources;
* church registers;
* Church Lectionary and Diary;
* other Christian and educational publications.

The publishing of *Our Modern Services* forms part of this broader ACK liturgical publishing mandate.

**Evidence status:** VERIFIED — UZIMA

---

## 6. Language Editions

ACK Uzima states that *Our Modern Services* has been produced in languages where the Anglican Church has a significant presence.

The official ACK source specifically identifies:

* **English**
* **Kiswahili**
* **Kikuyu**
* **Luo**
* **Kamba**

It also records **limited services** in:

* **Kisii**
* **Luyia**

Language availability must therefore be recorded at the resource/service level.

ACK Hub should **not assume that every service or the complete resource is available in every listed language**.

**Evidence status:** VERIFIED — UZIMA
