# Kitabu Kipya Cha Ibaada

## 1. Purpose

This file provides a structured reference to **Kitabu Kipya Cha Ibaada**, an ACK Kiswahili liturgical resource.

It is intended for ACK Hub research and information retrieval. It records verified information about the publication without reproducing its copyrighted liturgical text.

---

## 2. Resource Identity

**Title:** Kitabu Kipya Cha Ibaada

**Church:** Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK)

**Language:** Kiswahili

**Resource type:** Liturgical / prayer-book resource

**Relationship to Our Modern Services:** Kiswahili translation

**Publishing institution:** ACK Uzima Publishing House

**Evidence status:** VERIFIED — UZIMA

ACK Uzima's official catalogue identifies *Kitabu Kipya Cha Ibaada* as the **Swahili translation of Our Modern Services**.

---

## 3. Relationship to Our Modern Services

*Kitabu Kipya Cha Ibaada* should be understood as the Kiswahili-language counterpart of *Our Modern Services*.

ACK Hub should therefore maintain the following relationship:

```text
Our Modern Services
        │
        └── Kitabu Kipya Cha Ibaada
            Kiswahili translation
```

This relationship should not be interpreted as meaning that every later Kiswahili prayer-book publication is identical to *Kitabu Kipya Cha Ibaada*.

---

## 4. Distinction from the Kiswahili Prayer Book

The ACK Uzima catalogue lists two separate resources:

### Kitabu Kipya Cha Ibaada

Identified as the **Swahili translation of Our Modern Services**.

### Kiswahili Prayer Book

Identified as an **abridged version of Kitabu Kipya Cha Ibaada**.

These should remain separate resources in ACK Hub.

Do not merge them into one file or describe them as interchangeable without supporting evidence.

## 5. Publishing Context

ACK Uzima Publishing House was established to print and publish liturgical works for the Anglican Church in Kenya.

Its publishing history includes translations of Anglican liturgical resources into Kenyan languages and later development of ACK-specific modern liturgical resources.

The development and publication of *Kitabu Kipya Cha Ibaada* belongs to this wider ACK liturgical publishing history.

**Evidence status:** VERIFIED — UZIMA

## 7. Liturgical Context

Because *Kitabu Kipya Cha Ibaada* is identified by ACK Uzima as the Kiswahili translation of *Our Modern Services*, it belongs to the ACK modern liturgical resource tradition associated with the Province of Kenya.

The parent resource, *Our Modern Services*, was adopted by the Province of Kenya in **2002**.

For historical information about the development and adoption of *Our Modern Services*, see:
