# Liturgy — Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK)

## 1. Purpose

This file provides a structured reference for understanding worship, liturgy and principal services within the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK).

It is intended to help the ACK Hub AI answer questions about Anglican worship while directing detailed, service-specific questions to the appropriate authorised ACK liturgical sources.

---

## 2. Primary Liturgical Source

The ACK's authorised liturgical resources should be treated as the primary authority for the wording and order of worship services.

**Our Modern Services** is an important ACK liturgical resource and should be consulted as the primary source where an authorised copy is available.

The ACK Hub should not reproduce or distribute the complete contents of copyrighted liturgical books unless the relevant licence or permission allows such use.

This file therefore provides a structured reference rather than a replacement for the authorised liturgical books.

---

## 3. Purpose of Anglican Liturgy

Anglican liturgy provides an organised framework for corporate Christian worship.

It brings together elements such as:

* Prayer
* Scripture
* Praise and worship
* Confession
* Proclamation of the Gospel
* Sermons and teaching
* Sacraments
* Thanksgiving
* Intercession
* Blessing and dismissal

The precise order and wording depend on the particular service and the authorised liturgical resources in use.

---

## 4. Principal Areas of ACK Worship

The ACK's worship life includes regular Sunday worship, Holy Communion and other authorised services associated with Christian life and ministry.

Major areas include:

* Holy Communion
* Baptism
* Confirmation
* Marriage
* Funeral and burial services
* Ordination
* Morning and Evening Prayer
* Prayer and thanksgiving
* Special services and celebrations
* Services associated with the Church's liturgical calendar

The exact forms and authorised wording should be verified against the applicable ACK liturgical resource.

---

## 5. Holy Communion

Holy Communion, also known as the Eucharist or the Lord's Supper, is a central form of Christian worship in Anglican tradition.

A Communion service normally includes elements such as:

* Gathering and preparation
* Scripture readings
* Sermon or homily
* Prayers and intercessions
* Confession and absolution where prescribed
* The Peace
* Preparation of the table
* Eucharistic prayer
* Distribution of Holy Communion
* Thanksgiving
* Blessing and dismissal

The exact order and wording must be taken from the authorised ACK service book rather than reconstructed from a generic Anglican source.

---

## 6. Baptism

Baptism is a Christian sacrament and forms part of the Church's ministry of initiation.

ACK baptismal worship may involve:

* Presentation of candidates
* Questions and declarations
* Renunciation of evil
* Profession of faith
* Prayer
* Baptism with water
* Reception into the Christian community
* Thanksgiving and blessing

The authorised ACK service should be consulted for the official order and wording.

---

## 7. Confirmation

Confirmation is associated with the reaffirmation and strengthening of Christian faith and commitment.

A confirmation service may include:

* Presentation of candidates
* Examination or affirmation of faith
* Renewal of baptismal commitments
* Prayer
* Laying on of hands
* Episcopal ministry
* Thanksgiving and blessing

The precise requirements and service form should be obtained from the authorised ACK liturgical source.

---

## 8. Marriage

Marriage is marked by a formal Christian service conducted according to the Church's authorised liturgical and canonical requirements.

Questions concerning:

* Eligibility
* Banns
* Marriage preparation
* Required declarations
* Witnesses
* Legal requirements
* Marriage service wording

should be answered using the relevant current ACK regulations and authorised marriage service.

Do not infer current legal or ecclesiastical requirements from a historical service book.

---

## 9. Funeral and Burial Services

The ACK provides liturgical ministry for Christians and families during death, mourning and burial.

Funeral and burial worship may include:

* Scripture readings
* Hymns and worship
* Prayers
* Sermon or homily
* Thanksgiving and remembrance
* Commendation
* Committal
* Blessing

The exact service should be obtained from the authorised ACK funeral and burial liturgical resource.

---

## 10. Ordination

Ordination services relate to the setting apart of persons for authorised ministry within the Church.

Depending on the order of ministry, ordination services may involve:

* Presentation of candidates
* Examination
* Declarations and affirmations
* Litany and prayer
* Laying on of hands
* Prayer for the Holy Spirit
* Reception into the particular order of ministry
* Eucharistic celebration where prescribed

The exact form depends on the order of ministry and the authorised ACK service.

Different services involve different ministers and participants.

Depending on the service, these may include:

* Bishop
* Priest

## 15. Liturgy and Church Governance

Liturgical practice operates within the wider constitutional and ecclesiastical framework of ACK.

The ACK Hub should distinguish between:

* The Constitution
* Canonical or ecclesiastical regulations
* Liturgical books
* Church Diary and Lectionary
* Provincial policies
* Diocesan directives
* Local customs

A local practice should not automatically be presented as a universal ACK rule.

## 16. Local and Diocesan Variation

Some aspects of worship may vary according to:

* Diocese
* Parish
* Local language
* Cultural context
* Authorised local practice
* Special occasions

Where a question concerns a particular diocese or parish, the AI should identify the relevant local authority where possible.

Local variation should not be confused with a change to the official provincial liturgy.

---

## 17. AI Retrieval Rules

When answering liturgy-related questions:

1. Identify the service or liturgical occasion.
2. Consult the authorised ACK liturgical source where available.
3. Use the current ACK Church Diary and Lectionary for date-specific questions.
4. Distinguish official ACK practice from general Anglican practice.
5. Do not invent service wording.
6. Do not present reconstructed prayers as official ACK prayers.
7. Identify the source when quoting or closely paraphrasing an authorised service.
8. Do not reproduce copyrighted liturgical material beyond applicable permissions.
9. Treat local diocesan practices as local unless an authoritative source establishes them more broadly.
10. When uncertain, state that the authorised service book or current ACK directive should be consulted.

---

## 18. Copyright and Source-Control Rule

The ACK Hub may reference authorised liturgical books without automatically reproducing their complete contents.

Before adding a complete liturgical book to the repository, verify:

* Copyright status
* Licence terms
* Permission to store the text
* Permission to process the text
* Permission to distribute or expose the text through the AI system

Where permission has not been established, store structured summaries, metadata and references rather than the complete copyrighted text.
