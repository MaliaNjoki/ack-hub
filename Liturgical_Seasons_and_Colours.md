# Liturgical Seasons and Colours (ACK)

## Main Seasons
- **Advent**: Purple / Violet
- **Christmas**: White / Gold
- **Epiphany**: White (then Green)
- **Lent**: Purple / Violet
- **Holy Week**: Red (except Maundy Thursday – White)
- **Easter**: White / Gold
- **Pentecost**: Red
- **Trinity Sunday**: White
- **Ordinary Time / Trinity Season**: Green

## Notes
- Always follow the official Church Diary & Lectionary of the ACK when available.
- Local custom may sometimes vary slightly.
