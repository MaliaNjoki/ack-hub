# Pastoral and Congregational Services

## Purpose

This file provides a structured reference for pastoral and congregational services within the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK).

Each claim is assigned an evidence status:

* **VERIFIED — ACK:** directly supported by an identified official ACK source.
* **CONTEXT — Anglican/Christian:** general Anglican or Christian background; not an ACK-specific claim.
* **PLACEHOLDER — VERIFY:** a retrieval category or possible practice that requires verification from an authorised ACK, diocesan or parish source.

This file does not reproduce complete authorised prayers, service texts, hymns, responses or other copyrighted liturgical material.

---

# Part I — Verified ACK Information

## 1. Pastoral Ministry

### VERIFIED — ACK

The ACK describes pastoral ministry as including:

* Preaching.
* Teaching.
* Healing.
* Prayer.
* Pastoral care.
* Worship.
* Evangelism.

These activities are identified by ACK in its description of the mission of the Church.

**Source:** Official ACK, Archbishop's Charge / Mission of the Church.

### Evidence boundary

The source establishes that these activities form part of ACK pastoral ministry.

It does **not**, by itself, establish:

* A single provincial service for each activity.
* A mandatory order for pastoral visits.
* Exact prayer texts.
* Exact procedures for healing ministry.
* Exact responsibilities of every ministry role.

Those details remain **PLACEHOLDER — VERIFY**.

## 2. ACK Liturgical Publishing

### VERIFIED — ACK

ACK Uzima Publishing House documents the publication of liturgical materials for the Anglican Church of Kenya.

Its documented materials include:

* Prayer books.
* Hymn books.
* Liturgical resources.
* Church registers.
* Church Lectionary and Diary.
* *Our Modern Services*.

ACK Uzima states that *Our Modern Services* was adopted by the Province of Kenya in 2002 and was published in several Kenyan languages.

### Evidence boundary

The existence of a published resource does not prove that:

* Every parish currently uses it identically.
* Every service is available in every language.
* Every published practice remains current.
* A particular prayer is mandatory in every ACK service.

Those questions require current service-specific verification.

---

## 3. Provincial and Diocesan Structure

### VERIFIED — ACK

ACK maintains provincial and diocesan structures.

Official ACK diocesan information documents dioceses and their ministry structures, including ministries such as KAYO, Children Ministry and Mothers' Union.

### Evidence boundary

A ministry appearing in one diocesan structure does not automatically prove that:

* Every diocese organises it identically.
* Every parish uses the same programme.
* Its local activities constitute a province-wide liturgical requirement.

Provincial, diocesan and parish information must therefore be kept distinct.

# Part X — Data Quality Rules

* **VERIFIED:** A claim must have an identifiable official ACK source.
* **CONTEXT:** General Anglican/Christian information must be labelled as contextual.
* **PLACEHOLDER:** Unverified service-specific information must not be presented as ACK fact.
* **CURRENT:** Current practice requires current-source verification.
* **LOCAL:** Parish and diocesan practice must not automatically be generalised to the Province.
* **HISTORICAL:** Historical material must be labelled as historical.
* **EXACT TEXT:** Retrieve from an authorised source rather than reconstructing it.
* **SOURCE PRIORITY:** Prefer official ACK sources.
* **SCOPE:** Always distinguish provincial, diocesan and parish information.
* **Last verification:** September 2026.
