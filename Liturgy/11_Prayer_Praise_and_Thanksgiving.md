# Prayer, Praise and Thanksgiving

## Purpose

This file provides a structured reference for prayer, praise, worship, and thanksgiving within the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK).

### Evidence labels used in this file

* **VERIFIED — ACK:** Directly supported by an official ACK source.
* **CONTEXT — Anglican/Christian:** General Anglican or Christian background; not presented as an ACK-specific rule.
* **PLACEHOLDER — VERIFY:** A useful retrieval category or likely practice that requires verification from the applicable authorised ACK service, diocesan resource, or current Church Diary/Lectionary.

This file does not reproduce complete authorised prayers, collects, liturgical responses, hymns, or other copyrighted worship texts.

---

# Part I — ACK-Specific Information

## 1. Prayer and Worship in the ACK

### VERIFIED — ACK

The ACK describes pastoral ministry as including:

* Preaching.
* Teaching.
* Healing.
* Prayer.
* Pastoral care.
* Worship.
* Evangelism.

ACK therefore explicitly identifies prayer and worship within its description of pastoral ministry.

ACK's official website also describes the Church as a province of the Anglican Communion.

### PLACEHOLDER — VERIFY

The following should not automatically be treated as a single province-wide ACK worship structure without checking the applicable service:

* Exact order of worship.
* Exact sequence of prayers.
* Congregational responses.
* Required postures.
* Who leads particular prayers.
* Diocesan or parish variations.

---

## 2. ACK Liturgical Resources

### VERIFIED — ACK

ACK Uzima Publishing House states that it was founded to print and publish liturgical works, including prayer and hymn books for the Anglican Church in Kenya.

Its documented publications and activities include:

* Liturgical materials.
* Prayer books.
* Hymn books.
* Church registers.
* Church Lectionary and Diary.
* *Our Modern Services*.
* Materials in Kenyan languages.

ACK Uzima also states that *Our Modern Services* was adopted in 2002 by the Province of Kenya and that editions were produced in Kiswahili, Kikuyu, Luo and Kamba, with limited services available in Kisii and Luyia.

### PLACEHOLDER — VERIFY

Do not infer from the existence of a published resource that:

* Every parish currently uses it in exactly the same way.
* Every service is available in every language.
* A particular prayer is currently authorised.
* A particular prayer is mandatory in every ACK service.

Those points require service-specific verification.
