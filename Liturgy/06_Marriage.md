# Marriage in the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK)

## 1. Purpose

This document provides a structured reference to Marriage within the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK).

It covers the broad theological, pastoral, liturgical and administrative context of Christian marriage without reproducing the complete wording of authorised ACK marriage services.

The current authorised ACK liturgical resources, Constitution, applicable Church regulations and diocesan requirements remain the primary authorities for exact requirements and procedures.

---

## 2. Christian Marriage in the ACK

Marriage is part of the pastoral and liturgical life of the Church.

Within Anglican Christian teaching, marriage is understood in relation to:

* Christian discipleship
* Mutual commitment
* Faithfulness
* Love
* Family life
* Prayer
* Christian witness
* Responsibility and service

The Church accompanies couples through preparation, the marriage service and continuing pastoral care.

---

## 3. Marriage Preparation

Couples seeking marriage in the ACK may undergo a period of preparation before the service.

Preparation may include:

* Meetings with clergy
* Christian teaching on marriage
* Prayer
* Relationship preparation
* Discussion of family responsibilities
* Pastoral counselling
* Examination of relevant Church requirements
* Administrative documentation

The exact preparation programme may vary by diocese and parish.

---

## 4. Eligibility and Church Requirements

Marriage within the ACK is subject to applicable Church and civil requirements.

Depending on the circumstances, these may include:

* Appropriate notice
* Baptismal or Church documentation where required
* Identification documents
* Marriage preparation
* Publication of banns or other authorised procedure where applicable
* Required declarations
* Civil legal requirements
* Any applicable diocesan permissions

The ACK Hub should not present a single administrative checklist as universally applicable unless supported by a current official ACK source.

## 5. Broad Structure of a Marriage Service

The exact authorised order may vary.

A marriage service may broadly include:

### A. Gathering

The congregation gathers for worship and the couple is presented.

### B. Introduction and Declarations

The minister addresses the couple and congregation and establishes the purpose of the service.

### C. Scripture and Instruction

Scripture readings and an address or sermon may be included.

### D. Questions and Vows

The couple makes the authorised declarations and marriage vows.

### E. Exchange of Rings

Where applicable, rings are exchanged as a sign associated with the marriage commitment.

### F. Prayers

The Church prays for the couple, their marriage and their future life together.

### G. Declaration of Marriage

The minister makes the authorised declaration concerning the marriage.

### H. Signing and Documentation

Where required, the appropriate marriage documentation is completed.

### I. Blessing and Dismissal

The service concludes with prayer and blessing according to the authorised form.

The exact wording and sequence must be taken from the current authorised ACK marriage service.

## 9. Congregational Participation

Marriage is celebrated in the presence of the Church community.

The congregation may participate through:

* Prayer
* Scripture readings
* Hymns and worship
* Responses
* Witnessing the marriage
* Supporting the couple
* Thanksgiving

The Church's role continues beyond the wedding ceremony through pastoral support and Christian fellowship.

## 12. Marriage Records

Marriage creates important Church and legal records.

Records may include:

* Names of the couple
* Date of marriage
* Place of marriage
* Minister
* Witnesses
* Marriage register information
* Relevant legal documentation

The ACK Provincial Archives identifies marriage records among Church records preserved by the Church.

Current record-keeping, retention and legal requirements should be verified against official ACK and applicable Kenyan legal sources.

## 13. Pastoral Care After Marriage

The Church's ministry does not end with the wedding ceremony.

Pastoral support may include:

* Prayer
* Marriage counselling
* Family ministry
* Conflict support
* Christian fellowship
* Parenting support
* Spiritual formation
* Referral to appropriate professional or pastoral services where necessary

Pastoral care should respect applicable safeguarding and confidentiality requirements.
