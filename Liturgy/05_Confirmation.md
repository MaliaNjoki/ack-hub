# Confirmation in the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK)

## 1. Purpose

This document provides a structured reference to Confirmation in the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK).

It explains the purpose, participants, preparation and broad liturgical structure of Confirmation without reproducing the complete wording of authorised ACK service books.

The current authorised ACK liturgical resources and applicable ecclesiastical requirements remain the primary authority for the exact service, declarations, prayers, responses and rubrics.

---

## 2. Confirmation in the ACK

Confirmation is an important rite within Anglican Christian formation and initiation.

It provides an opportunity for appropriate baptised Christians to make or affirm their commitment to the Christian faith and to receive prayer for the strengthening of their life and ministry as members of the Church.

Confirmation should be understood in relation to Baptism, Christian formation, discipleship and participation in the life of the Church.

---

## 3. Relationship Between Baptism and Confirmation

Baptism and Confirmation are related but distinct.

### Baptism

Baptism marks Christian initiation and incorporation into the life of the Church.

### Confirmation

Confirmation provides an opportunity for appropriate baptised Christians to affirm or renew their commitment to the Christian faith and continue their growth as disciples.

The exact theological formulation and liturgical requirements should be taken from current authorised ACK resources.

See:

`Liturgy/04_Baptism.md`

for the dedicated Baptism reference.

## 4. Candidates for Confirmation

Confirmation candidates may include baptised members of the Church who have reached an appropriate level of Christian understanding and preparation.

Preparation and eligibility requirements may vary according to:

* Age
* Previous baptism
* Christian instruction
* Parish requirements
* Diocesan requirements
* The direction of the bishop or authorised minister

The ACK Hub should not assign a universal minimum age or eligibility rule unless supported by a current authoritative ACK source.

## 5. Preparation for Confirmation

Confirmation normally requires appropriate preparation.

Preparation may include instruction in:

* The Christian faith
* Holy Scripture
* The Creeds
* The Sacraments
* The Lord's Prayer
* Christian worship
* Prayer
* Christian moral life
* Anglican identity and practice
* Christian service
* Responsibilities of Church membership

Candidates may also receive pastoral guidance and participate in worship and Christian fellowship as part of their preparation.

The exact curriculum and duration of preparation should be verified against current diocesan or provincial requirements.

---

## 6. Role of the Bishop

Confirmation is traditionally associated with episcopal ministry within Anglicanism.

The bishop or other authorised minister presides over the service according to the applicable ecclesiastical and liturgical requirements.

The ACK Hub should not assume that every practical arrangement is identical across all dioceses.

Where a bishop delegates or authorises another minister to participate in the service, the applicable current Church requirements should be consulted.

Confirmation candidates may have sponsors or other members of the Church supporting them through the process.

Their role may include:

* Encouragement

The exact role and requirements for sponsors should be verified from the applicable ACK or diocesan guidance.

## 8. Broad Structure of a Confirmation Service

The exact authorised order may vary according to the service being used.

A Confirmation service may broadly include:

### A. Gathering

The congregation gathers for worship and the candidates are presented.

### B. Presentation of Candidates

Candidates are formally presented to the bishop or authorised minister.

### C. Examination and Declarations

Candidates may be asked questions concerning:

* Christian faith
* Repentance
* Commitment to Christ
* Christian discipleship
* Responsibilities as members of the Church

### D. Profession or Affirmation of Faith

Candidates participate in the authorised affirmation or profession of Christian faith.

### E. Prayer and Laying on of Hands

The service may include prayer for the candidates and the laying on of hands according to the authorised liturgical form.

### F. Thanksgiving and Intercession

The Church gives thanks and prays for the candidates and their continuing Christian life.

### G. Holy Communion

Holy Communion may form part of the wider service where appointed and authorised.

### H. Blessing and Dismissal

The service concludes with the authorised prayers, blessing and dismissal.

**Important:** This is a structural summary. The exact sequence and wording must be taken from the current authorised ACK Confirmation service.

## 9. Laying on of Hands

The laying on of hands may form part of the Confirmation rite.

It is accompanied by authorised prayer and represents the Church's prayer for the candidate.

The ACK Hub should not reproduce or reconstruct the exact prayer associated with this action without the authorised source.

## 10. Confirmation and Christian Discipleship

Confirmation is not an endpoint of Christian formation.

Confirmed members are encouraged to continue growing through:

* Worship
* Prayer
* Scripture
* Christian fellowship
* Service
* Mission
* Leadership
* Giving
* Participation in Church ministries

Christian formation continues throughout the life of the believer.

---

## 11. Young People and Confirmation

Confirmation may form an important part of the Christian formation of young people.

Preparation should support understanding rather than treating Confirmation simply as a ceremony.

Relevant ministries may include:

* Sunday School
* Youth ministry
* Children's ministry
* KAYO
* Parish discipleship programmes
* Bible study
* Christian mentorship

Where a candidate is a child or young person, the relevant safeguarding and pastoral requirements should also be observed.

## 12. Confirmation Records

Confirmation is an important Church event and should be appropriately recorded.

Relevant records may include:

* Candidate's name
* Date of Confirmation
* Place of Confirmation
* Minister/bishop
* Parish or congregation
* Baptismal information where applicable
* Confirmation register details

The exact fields, retention requirements and administrative procedures should be verified against current ACK administrative and archival guidance.

The ACK Provincial Archives identifies confirmation records among the historical Church records preserved by the Church.
