# Ordination in the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK)

## 1. Purpose

This document provides a structured reference to Ordination in the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK).

It covers the broad ecclesiastical, theological and liturgical context of ordination without reproducing the complete wording of authorised ordination services.

The current ACK Constitution, authorised liturgical resources and applicable Church regulations remain the primary authorities for exact requirements, eligibility, ministerial orders and service wording.

## 2. Ordination in the ACK

Ordination is the Church's formal recognition and setting apart of persons for authorised ordained ministry.

Within the Anglican tradition, ordained ministry includes distinct orders and responsibilities established by the Church.

The ACK Hub should distinguish:

* Ordination as a liturgical act
* The order of ministry to which a person is ordained
* The canonical or constitutional requirements for ministry
* The training and formation required before ordination
* The continuing responsibilities of ordained ministers

## 3. Orders of Ordained Ministry

The Anglican tradition recognises three principal orders of ordained ministry:

1. **Bishop**
2. **Priest**
3. **Deacon**

These orders have distinct ecclesiastical responsibilities.

The ACK Constitution and current authorised Church regulations should be used when determining the precise requirements and functions associated with each order.

## 9. Broad Structure of an Ordination Service

The exact authorised order varies according to the order being conferred.

An ordination service may broadly include:

### A. Gathering

The congregation gathers for worship and the candidates are presented.

### B. Presentation of Candidates

Candidates are formally presented to the appropriate bishop or ecclesiastical authority.

### C. Examination

The candidates may be examined concerning:

* Christian faith
* Doctrine
* Calling
* Character
* Commitment to ministry
* Responsibilities of ordained service

### D. Declarations and Affirmations

Candidates make the authorised declarations and affirmations required by the Church.

### E. Scripture and Sermon

Scripture is read and an address or sermon is given.

### F. Litany and Prayer

The Church prays for the candidates and for the ministry into which they are being ordained.

### G. Laying on of Hands

The bishop and other authorised ministers participate in the laying on of hands according to the authorised order of service.

### H. Ordination Prayer and Declaration

The authorised prayer and declaration of ordination are made.

### I. Vesting or Presentation Where Applicable

Additional authorised actions may accompany the service depending on the order and local practice.

### J. Holy Communion

Holy Communion may form part of an ordination service where appointed.

### K. Blessing and Dismissal

The service concludes according to the authorised liturgical form.

**Important:** This is a structural summary. The exact sequence, declarations and prayers must be taken from the current authorised ACK ordination service.
