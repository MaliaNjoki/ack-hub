# Anglican Church of Kenya – Provincial Constitution

## 1. Document Status

This file is the constitutional reference entry for the **Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK)** knowledge base.

The ACK Constitution is the principal constitutional instrument governing the organisation, leadership and governance of the Anglican Church of Kenya at provincial level.

**Important:** This file is intended to provide structured constitutional information and source guidance. It must not be treated as a substitute for the complete authorised text of the current ACK Provincial Constitution.

### Current-version rule

The ACK Hub should use the **most recent authorised version** of the Provincial Constitution.

Where the authorised current version cannot be verified, the AI must state that the constitutional provision requires verification rather than presenting an unverified version as current.

---

## 2. Purpose

The Provincial Constitution provides the constitutional framework for the governance and administration of the Anglican Church of Kenya.

It should be read together with other authorised Church instruments where applicable, including:

* Canons;
* regulations;
* rules;
* policies;
* Synod resolutions;
* authorised amendments; and
* other formally approved governance instruments.

These instruments should not automatically be treated as having the same constitutional status.

---

## 3. Constitutional Authority

The Constitution provides the framework within which the Church's provincial governance operates.

Official ACK material has referred to the Constitution as a guiding instrument for governance and leadership.

The Constitution should therefore be treated as a **primary governance source** when answering questions concerning ACK constitutional structures, offices, authority and procedures.

## 5. Provincial Synod

Provincial Synod is the principal provincial governing council of the Anglican Church of Kenya.

The Synod is organised into three houses:

1. House of Bishops
2. House of Clergy
3. House of Laity

The Constitution and authorised Synodical instruments should be used to determine the precise powers, membership, procedures and voting arrangements of the Synod.

## 14. Relationship With Other Church Instruments

The Constitution should be distinguished from other Church documents.

| Instrument              | General function                                                   |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Provincial Constitution | Constitutional framework of the Province                           |
| Canons                  | Ecclesiastical/legal rules within the Church's governing framework |
| Regulations             | Detailed rules governing specified matters                         |
| Policies                | Approved policy direction                                          |
| Synod resolutions       | Decisions or directions adopted through Synodical processes        |
| Procedures              | Operational implementation                                         |
| Historical documents    | Records of previous arrangements or decisions                      |

The exact legal or ecclesiastical status of an instrument should be determined from the authorised source.

## 15. Amendments and Revisions

Constitutional provisions may be amended or revised through the procedures authorised by the governing framework of the Church.

The ACK Hub should maintain a clear distinction between:

* the current Constitution;
* proposed amendments;
* approved amendments;
* historical constitutional versions; and
* superseded provisions.

A proposed amendment must not be presented as part of the current Constitution unless it has been formally approved and brought into effect.

## 16. Current Versus Historical Information

Constitutional information can become outdated when the Church adopts amendments or changes its governance arrangements.

The AI should therefore distinguish:

**Current constitutional provision**

from

**Historical constitutional provision**

**Proposed or reported constitutional change**.

## 17. Constitutional Source Hierarchy

For constitutional questions, the preferred source hierarchy is:

1. Latest authorised ACK Provincial Constitution
2. Official authorised constitutional amendments
3. Official ACK Canons and regulations
4. Official Provincial Synod records
5. Official ACK governance documents
6. Official ACK announcements and archives
7. Other reliable institutional sources

A secondary source should not override an authoritative constitutional document.

---

## 18. AI Retrieval Rules

When answering a constitutional question:

1. Identify the relevant constitutional subject.
2. Check the latest authorised constitutional source.
3. Check whether an amendment affects the provision.
4. Distinguish constitutional authority from administrative practice.
5. Distinguish current provisions from historical provisions.
6. Give the relevant article/section number when the authorised text is available.
7. Do not invent article numbers.
8. Do not quote constitutional wording unless it has been verified from the authorised source.
9. If the current provision cannot be verified, clearly state that verification is required.
10. Cross-reference detailed ACK Hub records rather than duplicating their contents.
